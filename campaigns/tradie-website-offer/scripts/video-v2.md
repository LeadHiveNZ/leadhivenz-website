# Video v2 script: $500 tradie website (written 21/10/2026)

Why v2: video v1 is fatiguing. First 3 Meta days (11–13 Oct) vs last 3 (18–20 Oct):
link CTR 1.71% → 0.86%, landing page views 37 → 11, cost per lead $13.25 → ~$30.
Zero leads 19–20 Oct. The hook still stops people (3-second view rate ~26% in both periods)
and drop-off is unchanged (about half gone by 6.5s, two-thirds by 13s), so the problem is the
same ~3,900 NZ viewers seeing the same pitch (frequency 2.1), not the opening itself.
The NZ static got $3 of delivery in a week: Meta won't run statics here, so v2 has to be video.

Rules from the data:
1. Say "$500" in the first 2 seconds and have it on screen from frame 1 (v1 said it at 9.8s,
   by which point most viewers had gone).
2. Show a real website. v1 never showed the product.
3. Name the trades that are converting (builders, painters, aircon, property maintenance,
   landscaping) and talk to established owners.
4. Everything that matters inside 12 seconds; total 20–25s.
5. Look different from v1: new shirt (not the All Blacks top), new spot (outdoors / by the ute /
   on a site), different framing.

## Hooks (film all three; same body after each = 3 ads)

- **A, product reveal (recommended lead):** hold your phone to the camera showing
  southmaintenance.co.nz. "This website cost five hundred bucks."
  On screen: `THIS COST $500`
- **B, call-out:** "Builders, sparkies, painters: if your website is a Facebook page, listen up."
  On screen: `TRADIES: $500 WEBSITE`
- **C, risk reversal:** "I'll build your business a website for $500, and you don't pay me a
  cent until you've seen it." On screen: `$500 · PAY AFTER YOU SEE IT`

## Body (same for all three, ~15s)

> Most web agencies charge five grand or more. I build tradie websites for five hundred.
>
> I'll build you a live demo first, free, ready within 24 hours. Love it, it's yours. Don't love
> it, walk away.
>
> It shows up on Google, it's got a big call-now button, and you can send it to your customers
> for reviews.
>
> Here's one I built. *(cut to phone screen recording scrolling southmaintenance.co.nz, 3–4s)*
>
> Tap the link, pop in your name and number, takes thirty seconds, and I'll give you a ring.

On-screen text beats: `$5,000+ → $500` · `FREE DEMO IN 24 HRS` · `PAY AFTER YOU SEE IT` ·
`REAL SITE I BUILT ↓` · end card `TAP BELOW · 30-SEC FORM`

## Shot list

1. Hook A: phone held up to the lens, site visible, then pull the phone away to reveal your face.
2. Hooks B and C: straight to camera, chest-up, outdoors.
3. Body: one take straight to camera, same spot.
4. B-roll: phone screen recording of southmaintenance.co.nz scrolling top to bottom (10s), and
   of leadhivenz.com/website showing the name + mobile step (5s).
5. Vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, burned-in captions (sound-off viewers), 20–25s per version.

## Launch plan

Upload the three cuts to Drive as "Tradie Website v2 A/B/C". Add all three to the NZ ad set
alongside v1, same $20/day. After 3 days, pause v1 and any v2 cut with no leads.
For Australia later: swap the call-out to "Aussie tradies" and re-film hook B.
