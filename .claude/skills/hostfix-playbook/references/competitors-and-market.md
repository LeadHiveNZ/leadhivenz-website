# Competitors & Market

Sources: Turno demo, Breezeway founder (Jeremy Gall), Hospitable CEO (Pierre-Camille Hamana), Daniel Rusteen (host tools by level). Mostly US data. Some figures come from interviews whose timestamps looked unreliable, so verify before quoting publicly.

## Turno (cleaning marketplace + software)
- 8+ years, 55,000+ vetted cleaners, 4.8M+ marketplace turnovers a year. Separate apps for hosts and cleaners.
- Founded by an Airbnb host frustrated with organising cleans.
- Marketplace: the host posts a job, cleaners bid a fixed price within 24h, and bids expire after 48h. Profiles show background check, licence, insurance and host ratings, plus **Airbnb guest cleanliness scores** for properties the cleaner has serviced.
- Syncs reservations from Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com, iCal and 28 property management systems to auto-create cleaning jobs.
- Primary and backup cleaners, auto-forwarding, photo checklists, inventory alerts, **problem reporting** (shared by text to maintenance people), auto-pay on check-out via Stripe.
- Fees: **5% (bank) or 8% (card)** on marketplace cleans. Software free for 1–2 properties, then about US$8/listing/month.
- **Gap for HostFix:** Turno spots problems but hands them off. HostFix fixes them at a fixed price. Position HostFix as "the fix for every problem your cleaner finds", and as a possible partner or integration rather than a head-on rival.

## Breezeway (operations platform for property managers)
- Founder previously built FlipKey. 2,000+ property management customers worldwide.
- Thesis: the STR industry has shifted from *getting bookings* (marketing, now settled) to *delivering the promise* (operations). Guests' expectations won't go backwards. Remove "negative surprises".
- Technology removes the back-office work guests never see, freeing people for hospitality. It's not for cutting staff.
- Field adoption needs consumer-grade UX; validated photos and checklists matter.
- Tight operations give managers the confidence to take on more properties.
- **For HostFix:** big managers already think in these terms. Pitch HostFix as part of their operations: reliable maintenance delivery and fewer bad reviews.

## Hospitable (property management system)
- Started in 2015 as message automation, then grew into a unified inbox, operations, multi-channel and pricing.
- Claims: only ~35% of US listings use software (vs. ~80% adoption in other small-business categories); **~85% of hosts have fewer than 7 properties** and find most systems too expensive or complex.
- Response: a $0 "Essentials" plan; revenue from payments (2.5% fee on free, 1.5% on paid), upsells and insurance. "Simple at one door. Simple at one hundred."
- Building a marketplace matching burnt-out owners with top property managers.
- About 70% of support conversations are resolved by AI.
- **For HostFix:** the small-host majority is underserved. Keep HostFix dead simple and low-commitment for them. Integrating with or partnering with property management systems is a channel to the managers.

## What hosts already use and pay for (Daniel Rusteen)
- Level 1: dynamic pricing (PriceLabs $20/month or 1%), digital guidebook (Hostfully, free tier), PMS ($20–50/listing/month), keypad lock (~$129).
- Level 2: noise monitoring (NoiseAware $15/month), damage waivers (Waivo $22–44/month; offering guests a $75 non-refundable waiver instead of a $500 deposit), direct booking sites (Bookl.ee $12/month; Boostly $62/month + $3–4k setup).
- Level 3: listing analytics (IntelliHost $16/listing/month), Wi-Fi email capture (StayFi), market data (AllTheRooms), **Turno** for cleaning, accounting (Hurdlr $8/month).
- Hosts are used to paying $8–50 per listing per month for tools that save time or protect revenue. Maintenance is missing from this stack, which is HostFix's opening.
- Possible partnerships: guidebook (put the HostFix QR or report link in the digital guidebook), PMS, cleaners.
