## 2. SUMMARY
This webinar presents a complete overview and product demonstration of Turno, a specialized software platform designed for short-term rental (STR) property managers and hosts to source, schedule, manage, and automate property turnovers. The presenters explain the core operational challenges facing STR operators—such as rising supply, increased competition, and the necessity of maintaining 5-star cleanliness reviews. They demonstrate how Turno addresses these by combining a marketplace of vetted cleaners with workflow automation software. Viewers are guided through linking reservation channels, searching the marketplace, onboarding internal team members, building checklists, tracking inventory, automating payments via Stripe, monitoring quality analytics, and configuring project settings.

### Step-by-Step Platform Setup & Feature Walkthrough

#### 1. Linking Booking Channels & Integrations (05:10–06:38)
* **Purpose:** Sync calendar reservations so Turno can automatically generate turnover cleaning projects triggered by guest checkout dates.
* **Navigation:** Click user initials/avatar in the top right corner $\rightarrow$ select **Integrations**.
* **Supported Platforms:**
  * **Property Management Systems (PMS) & Channel Managers:** Hostaway, Hostfully, Guesty, Guesty for Hosts, Hospitable, Host Tools, iGMS, Jetstream, Kigo, LMPM, Booking Factory, Bookster, Direct, Escapia, eviivo, FantasticStay, Hosthub, Hostify, Smoobu, Toket, Track HS, Uplisting, Zeevou, etc. (28 supported).
  * **Online Travel Agencies (OTAs) & Direct Links:** Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, and generic iCal feeds.
* *Mechanism:* Connecting a PMS automatically imports all property listings, reservations, and calendar data without needing to link individual channels manually.

#### 2. Sourcing Cleaners via the Marketplace (06:39–09:44)
* **Navigation:** Left sidebar $\rightarrow$ **Cleaner Search** $\rightarrow$ Click **New Search** (top right).
* **Search Setup Workflow:**
  1. *Select Property:* Choose an existing property or input a new address.
  2. *Property Specifications:* Enter unit size (sq ft / sq m), number of bedrooms, and number of bathrooms.
  3. *Describe Cleaning Needs:*
     * Estimated turnover frequency (number of turns per month).
     * Estimated project duration (e.g., 4.5 hours).
     * Provision of cleaning supplies (Host provides vs. Cleaner provides).
     * Laundry requirements (wash and dry linens/towels on-site).
     * Attach a property checklist (optional at this stage).
     * Special notes/instructions for bidding cleaners.
  4. *Click "Find Cleaners":* The request is dispatched to local marketplace cleaners.
* **Reviewing Bids:**
  * Cleaners submit project bids within 24 hours.
  * **Cleaner Profiles Display:**
    * Fixed project price bid.
    * Distance from the property (e.g., 32 miles away).
    * Total cleaning projects completed on Turno.
    * Year joined the platform.
    * Badges: Background Check verified, Business License, Liability Insurance.
    * **Dual Rating System:**
      1. *Host Rating:* Score (out of 5.0) and written reviews from other hosts using Turno.
      2. *Airbnb Guest Rating:* Cleanliness ratings (1–5 stars) pulled directly from public Airbnb guest reviews for properties that specific cleaner serviced over the preceding 90 days.
* **Bid Acceptance Rules:**
  * Cleaners' bids expire after **48 hours**.
  * Accepting a bid is **non-binding** (does not establish a locked contract or immediate payment).
  * Recommendation: Accept 2 to 5 top candidates, initiate communication, and select 1 primary cleaner and 1–2 backup cleaners.

#### 3. Managing Teammates & External Service Providers (09:44–11:37)
* **Navigation:** Left sidebar $\rightarrow$ **My Team**.
* **Cleaner Onboarding State:**
  * Accepted marketplace cleaners appear in a "waiting room" status (not yet assigned to properties).
  * While unassigned, cleaners have zero visibility into property calendars or upcoming projects.
* **Assigning Roles & Properties:**
  * Assign cleaner as **Primary** or **Backup**.
  * Set individual property pricing (fixed per-project rate or hourly rate).
* **Adding Non-Marketplace & Outside Vendors:**
  * Click **Invite Teammate** (invite via SMS or email).
  * Assign custom roles: Cleaning, Check-in/Welcoming, Inspection, Linen Delivery, Maintenance, Pool Cleaning.
* **Co-Hosts Tab:** Invite co-hosts or property managers to access and manage the host side of the Turno account.

#### 4. Managing Projects: Schedule vs. List View (11:38–13:56)
* Cleanings are called **Projects**.
* **Schedule View (Grid/Calendar):**
  * Displays properties along the Y-axis and calendar dates along the X-axis.
  * *Grey line:* Guest reservation dates.
  * *Colored blocks on top:* Cleaning project schedule.
  * *"No Teammates" label:* Project is awaiting cleaner acceptance.
  * Cleaner's name displayed: Project has been accepted.
* **List View:** Separates workflow into tabs:
  1. *Pending Projects:* Turnovers generated but not yet accepted by the assigned cleaner (cleaners see these as "Project Requests").
  2. *Accepted Projects:* Turnovers accepted and confirmed by primary or backup cleaners.
  3. *Canceled Projects.*
* **Project Card Details:**
  * Start and finish cleaning windows (e.g., 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM).
  * "Same Day Arrival" alert if a guest checks out and another checks in on the same day.
  * Guest count and reservation details for the incoming booking.
  * Attached checklists, inventory tracking, property access codes, and notes.

#### 5. Manual Projects (13:57–14:49)
* Used for tasks outside the automated booking calendar (deep cleans, test cleans, mid-stay cleans, maintenance repairs, pool service).
* Supports **Single Projects** or **Repeating Projects** (e.g., repeating daily, weekly, or twice a year).
* Functions identically to automated projects: cleaners receive an alert, accept/reject, complete checklists, log hours, and receive auto-payment.

#### 6. Cleaner Workflow & Auto-Pay Mechanics (14:50–15:19; 22:39–23:48)
* **Check-In:** When the cleaner arrives on-site, they hit **Check-in** on the Turno for Cleaners mobile app. The host receives an arrival notification.
* **Execution:** Cleaner completes tasks, checks off checklist items, snaps required photos, and updates inventory levels.
* **Check-Out:** The cleaner taps **Check-out**.
* **Payment Trigger:** Tapping "Check-out" automatically authorizes and initiates the payment transfer via Stripe.

#### 7. Checklist Configuration (15:20–17:23)
* **Navigation:** Left sidebar $\rightarrow$ **Checklists** $\rightarrow$ **New Checklist**.
* **Customization Options:**
  * Group tasks by room (Bathroom, Kitchen, Master Bedroom, etc.).
  * **Require Photo Documentation:** Adds a camera icon to specific line items; cleaners cannot complete the item without uploading a photo.
  * **Add Reference Image:** Host uploads an exemplary photo (e.g., how throw pillows must be arranged on a bed) for cleaner reference.
  * **Mandatory Checklist Completion:** Cleaners are blocked from checking out and triggering payment until 100% of checklist items are marked complete.
* **Templates:** Access **Popular Checklists** for pre-built STR templates (e.g., Airbnb Cleaning Checklist Template, Disinfection guides).
* *Best Practice:* Keep checklists concise and focused on property specifics rather than common cleaning fundamentals (avoid 200-line lists).

#### 8. Inventory Tracking (17:24–18:16)
* **Navigation:** Left sidebar $\rightarrow$ **Inventory**.
* **Tracking Modes:**
  * *Precise:* Exact numerical counts.
  * *Estimate:* Qualitative levels (e.g., Full, Medium, Low, Empty).
* **Threshold Alerts:** Turn on **Report a Problem** when stock hits a selected threshold (e.g., "Low" or "Empty"). Cleaners log current stock during their clean; if stock falls below threshold, an automated alert appears in the host's Problems tab.

#### 9. Problem Reporting (18:17–19:10)
* Cleaners flag broken, missing, or depleted items directly through the Turno for Cleaners app with photos and descriptions.
* **External Sharing:** Hosts can instantly share a reported problem via text or email to maintenance personnel or third parties without requiring them to have a Turno account.
* **Problem Resolution:** Unresolved problems can be marked "Solved" once repaired or restocked, archiving them to the Solved tab.

#### 10. Property-Level Customization Settings (19:11–22:38)
* Under **Properties**, select a property to customize:
  * *General Tab:* Access door codes, unit square footage, bed/bath count, and Wi-Fi credentials (sharing Wi-Fi aids cleaners in uploading high-res photos in low-reception areas).
  * *Calendars Tab:*
    * **"Make projects visible to teammate":** Configure how far in advance cleaners see and accept projects:
      * *Immediately* (cleaners see bookings as far out as 2 years).
      * *1 week, 2 weeks, or 4 weeks before project start*.
  * *Check-in/out Tab:* Define default check-out (e.g., 11:00 AM) and check-in (e.g., 3:00 PM) times.
  * *Cleaning Scheduling Window:*
    * Schedule immediately after guest checkout (default used by 95% of hosts).
    * Schedule immediately before guest check-in (for long vacancy gaps).

#### 11. Payments, Fees & Taxes (22:39–23:48)
* **Marketplace Cleaner Payments:** Must be processed through Turno via Stripe.
* **Platform Fee Structure for Marketplace Cleans:**
  * **ACH (Bank Account):** 5% platform fee (e.g., a \$100 cleaning bid costs \$105 total).
  * **Credit / Debit Card:** 8% platform fee (e.g., a \$100 cleaning bid costs \$108 total).
* **Tax Compliance:** Stripe automatically generates and distributes 1099 tax forms to marketplace cleaners; hosts do not need to issue separate 1099s.
* **Outside / Personal Cleaners:** Hosts can choose to pay external cleaners through Turno via Stripe or off-platform (cash, check, external payroll) for free.

#### 12. Quality Center & Analytics (24:50–25:28)
* Syncs with Airbnb host data to display:
  * Overall Cleanliness Rating over time (target: 5.00 stars).
  * Cleanliness rating percentile compared to similar hosts (e.g., "3% higher than similar hosts").
  * Cleaner-specific performance rankings and issue history linked directly to corresponding guest review scores.

#### 13. Advanced Project Automations (25:29–26:16)
* Navigation: User avatar $\rightarrow$ **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **Projects**.
* **Direct Assign / Auto Assign:** Automatically assigns incoming projects to selected cleaners without manual dispatch.
* **Auto-Forward to Backup Cleaners:** Automatically releases unaccepted projects to backup cleaners if the primary cleaner fails to accept or reject $X$ days before the scheduled start date.
* **Mandatory Checklist Completion:** Forces full checklist verification prior to clean checkout.

#### 14. Check-in / Welcoming Projects (24:05–24:49)
* An automated secondary project scheduled immediately before guest arrival.
* Designed for pre-stay inspections, staging welcome gifts/flowers, writing notes, or verifying heating/cooling controls.

## 5. WARNINGS, PITFALLS & CAVEATS
* **48-Hour Bid Expiration Window (08:21):** Marketplace cleaner bids expire automatically after 48 hours. Hosts must accept bids before the deadline to keep candidate proposals active and unlock direct messaging/phone contact.
* **Unassigned Cleaners Have No Access (09:58):** Simply accepting a cleaner's bid does not give them access to property jobs. Cleaners remain in an inactive state until the host explicitly links them to specific properties under the *My Team* or *Properties* tab.
* **Overly Long Checklists Reduce Compliance (17:07):** Building massive, granular checklists (e.g., 200 items detailing basic tasks like sweeping floors) leads to cleaner fatigue and skipped items. Reserve checklists for unique property idiosyncrasies and critical quality points.
* **Calendar Visibility Window Oversight (19:50):** If calendar visibility is set to "Immediately," cleaners can see and accept reservations booked up to two years in advance. If hosts want cleaner commitments closer to real-time, adjust visibility to 2 or 4 weeks before the project date.
* **Mandatory Checklist Checkout Gate (16:18, 26:05):** Enabling "Mandatory Checklist Completion" will block cleaners from finishing the job and receiving payment if even a single item or required photo is omitted.
