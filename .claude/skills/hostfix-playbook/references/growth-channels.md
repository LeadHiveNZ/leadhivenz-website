# Growth Channels

Sources: Gustaf Alströmer (How to Get Users and Grow), Rob Walling, Supademo, SidelineSwap, Adam Lyttle (app launch), plus the LeadHive playbook's Meta and Google ads material (see the `leadhive-playbook` skill).

## 1. Match the channel to the behaviour (Alströmer)
| Behaviour | Channel | HostFix fit |
|---|---|---|
| Known target list | **Sales / outreach** | ★★★ Every STR listing in Queenstown/Wanaka is findable. Build the list and contact them. |
| Word of mouth | **Referrals / virality** | ★★★ Hosts, cleaners and managers all talk to each other. |
| Rare, searched-for need | SEO / Google search ads | ★★ "Airbnb maintenance Queenstown", "emergency plumber Queenstown" (the tradie side benefits too). |
| High lifetime value | Paid social (Meta) | ★★ Already running. Fine for awareness and founding offers. Never scale paid ads before revenue (Alströmer). |

Scale paid channels only once retention shows people stay (see `launch-plan.md`). Otherwise it's a "leaky bucket".

## 2. Highest-leverage channels now
1. **Direct outreach to a known list.** Build a sheet of Queenstown/Wanaka STR owners and managers (listings, local management companies, the Facebook groups below). Founder messages and calls (`sales-and-pricing.md` templates).
2. **Cleaning companies as partners.** They're in every property weekly and see every problem first. Offer a referral credit per new owner and make reporting easy. One cleaning company can bring dozens of properties.
3. **Local host Facebook groups and communities.** SidelineSwap went where transactions were already happening (Facebook groups and forums). Join Queenstown/Wanaka/NZ Airbnb host groups. Answer maintenance questions helpfully; post finished-job stories (with permission). Never spam or pose as someone else.
4. **Property managers as multipliers.** One manager = dozens of properties. See the manager motion in `sales-and-pricing.md`.
5. **Referrals from founding owners.** Ask on every day-7 call. Design the invite like Airbnb's (Alströmer): social proof ("Joe invited you to HostFix"), clear value ("your first call-out-free job"), urgency (a deadline), exclusivity (button says "Accept invitation", not "Sign up").
6. **Partnerships and integrations** (Walling's six channels: SEO, content, outreach, partnerships, integrations, paid). Digital guidebooks (put the QR or report link in Hostfully-type guidebooks) and PMS integrations later.

## 3. Launch-day tactics that do and don't apply
- Walling: notify the waitlist 2 weeks ahead, tease with screenshots and video, offer founding incentives. ✅
- Product Hunt / Hacker News / Reddit: one-off spikes, weak for a local service. ❌ (except maybe NZ founder communities for credibility)
- App Store (Adam Lyttle): put the exact keyword in the app title (e.g. "HostFix – Airbnb Maintenance"), keywords in the subtitle without repeats, and a short, high-bid Apple Search Ads "visibility" campaign on exact-match brand terms during launch week. Useful but secondary; HostFix customers mostly arrive by referral, ads and outreach, not App Store browsing.
- **Shareable moments** (Lyttle): the "job done" text or report with before and after photos, branded, that owners forward to co-hosts or partners. Every share is free marketing.

## 4. Content angles (from the videos plus HostFix's ads)
- "Stop ringing round for tradies" (customer language).
- "Your cleaner finds it. HostFix fixes it."
- Real job stories: problem → fixed price → time to fix → guest review saved.
- Founder story: Joe building it in public (Supademo: about 20% of traffic from building in public on LinkedIn).
