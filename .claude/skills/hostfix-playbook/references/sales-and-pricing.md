# Selling to Hosts & Managers, and Pricing

Sources: Tom Blomfield / YC (closing first contracts), Gustaf Alströmer / YC (first customers, talking to users), Rob Walling (pricing), Oskar Bader (early-user offer), Turno (fees), Hospitable CEO (less-than-free model), Daniel Rusteen (what hosts pay for tools). Briefs in `sources/`.

## 1. Two customer types, two motions
| | Small owners (1–6 properties) | Property managers (10–100+) |
|---|---|---|
| Share of market | ~85% of hosts (Hospitable CEO, US data) | Few, but high leverage |
| Motion | Self-serve sign-up + quick founder text/call | Founder-led sales, onboarding visit |
| Offer | Founding price, simple monthly or per-job pricing | Founding manager partnership, paid plan with opt-out |
| What they value | Not ringing round, no call-out fee, fixed price | Reliability at scale, reporting, less admin for their team |

Big managers are the "seed with the best" accounts (see `marketplace-supply.md`). One 63-property manager is worth more than the whole 25-property founding cap. Don't let the cap block a manager deal; create a separate founding manager tier.

## 2. The closing structure that works (Tom Blomfield, YC)
B2B progression: design partner → free pilot → paid pilot → **recurring contract with an opt-out** → customer success.
- Push to the **paid monthly plan with a 30–60 day money-back or opt-out**. If they do nothing, it continues; there's no second sales process after a pilot.
- If a pilot is unavoidable, keep it **7–14 days** and define success upfront ("5 jobs completed, each accepted within 15 minutes and finished within 24 hours, at the quoted price").
- Value equation: "If HostFix saves your team X hours a week ringing tradies and avoids Y bad reviews, what's that worth?" Price at a fraction of the value.
- Find the **champion** (the ops or maintenance person) and treat them like a co-founder. Map who approves: owner, ops manager, accounts.
- Visit in person to move deals: "I'm in Queenstown Thursday. Coffee?"
- Scarcity, honestly: "We're only taking a handful of founding managers this season, so the tradies can keep up."

Discovery questions (Blomfield, Alströmer):
- "Walk me through what happens today when a guest reports a broken shower at 6pm."
- "What part of managing maintenance do you hate most?"
- "How often does it happen? Who handles it? What does it cost you when it goes wrong?"
- "What have you tried? What's frustrating about it?"
- Don't ask "Would you use this?" (people say yes to be polite), and don't take feature requests at face value; find the problem underneath.

## 3. Owner call (5–10 minutes, after the form)
1. "Thanks for filling in the form. What made you reach out?" (Clarify)
2. "How do you handle repairs at the moment?" (Get their words.)
3. "How many properties, and where? Who cleans them?"
4. Explain in 3 lines: fixed price, no call-out fee, accepted in 15 minutes, done in 24 hours, cleaner or guest can report it, a text at every step.
5. Offer: founding price, plus "I'll set your properties up with you."
6. Close: "Want me to lock in your spot and book a time to set up the properties?" Take payment or card details on the call where possible.
7. Ask for referrals: "Who else do you know with an Airbnb here?"

Speed to lead: reply to every form the **same day** by text (many hosts prefer text).

## 4. Pricing benchmarks
- **Turno** (cleaning marketplace): hosts pay the cleaner's price plus a **5% platform fee by bank transfer or 8% by card**. Software is free for 1–2 properties, then about US$8/listing/month. Outside cleaners can be paid off-platform for free.
- **Hospitable** (PMS): moved to a **$0 subscription "Essentials" plan**, earning instead from payments (2.5% transaction fee on free, 1.5% on paid plans) plus upsells (early check-in, insurance). "Less than free: we generate revenue as you do."
- **What hosts already pay** (Daniel Rusteen): PMS US$20–50/listing/month, PriceLabs $20/month, noise monitor $15/month, damage waiver $22–44/month, Turno $8/listing.
- **Walling:** don't price so low that 100 customers can't fund the business.

**HostFix pricing options to test**
1. **Job margin only:** free for owners; HostFix takes a margin or fee on each fixed-price job (the Turno/Hospitable model). Lowest friction for small owners.
2. **Per-property subscription + fixed job prices:** predictable recurring revenue. Founding half price.
3. **Hybrid:** free for 1–2 properties (like Turno), subscription for managers with service levels.

Whatever the model: founding offers should be **paid**, with a value-add (free property setup and photo walkthrough) rather than only a discount (Walling). Payment is the only real proof of value (Alströmer).

## 5. Outreach templates
**Cold message to a host or manager (Alströmer's rules: 6–8 sentences, plain text, say you're the founder, add social proof, one ask):**
> Hi [Name], I'm Joe, founder of HostFix in Queenstown. We fix Airbnb maintenance at fixed prices with no call-out fee: a vetted, insured tradie accepts within 15 minutes and it's done within 24 hours. Your cleaner or guests can report issues by QR code, and you get a text at every step. We're onboarding a small group of founding properties now ([X] already signed, including local managers). Worth a 10-minute chat this week?

**Interview request (no pitch):**
> Hey [Name], I'm talking to Queenstown hosts about how they handle repairs between guests: what works and what's a pain. Could I grab 15 minutes this week? Not selling anything, just learning.
