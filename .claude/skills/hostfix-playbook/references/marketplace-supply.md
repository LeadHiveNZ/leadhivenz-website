# Marketplace Cold Start & Tradie Supply

Sources: Henri Asseily (marketplace cold start, Bizrate/Shopzilla), SidelineSwap founder (first 1,000 listings), Gustaf Alströmer ("contractors are notoriously hard to sell to"), Breezeway founder (field staff adoption), Turno demo (how a cleaner marketplace runs supply). Briefs in `sources/11-…`, `12-…`, `05-…`, `13-…`, `15-…`.

## 1. Which side first? Supply.
- Asseily: find the **rarer, harder side** (usually supply), start with a tiny cohort (he used 3 merchants: one small, one medium, one large), and over-serve them with "everything they need". Use them to attract demand. Market power comes later.
- For HostFix, tradies are the hard side: Queenstown has a well-known tradie shortage, contractors are hard to sell software to (YC), and the promise of "accepted in 15 minutes, done in 24 hours" depends entirely on them.
- **Rule:** don't open a new area or trade in ads until there are at least **2–3 committed tradies per trade** (primary plus backup) able to meet the service levels.

## 2. Seed with the best (SidelineSwap)
- SidelineSwap launched with only 10–15 sellers, but they were All-American college athletes. That made the platform look premium and pulled in buyers, who then became sellers.
- One niche first (lacrosse), other categories ignored until it worked. Then roll out category by category, always starting at the top of the market.
- **HostFix:** recruit the best-reviewed, most reliable plumber, electrician and handyman in Queenstown/Wanaka, not whoever is free. Launch with 3 trades in 1 area. Add trades (locksmith, appliance repair, glazier, gardener) and towns one at a time.

## 3. The supply pitch: over-serve them
Asseily's pitch, adapted: "We want you to be the first [trade] on HostFix in Queenstown. We'll send you the jobs, handle the owner, pay you fast, and give you the property details and access before you arrive. We'll also give you feedback from owners and help you get reviews."

What a tradie gets:
- **Guaranteed, pre-priced work** with no quoting, chasing or invoicing.
- Full job details before arriving: access code, location of the mains and switchboard, photos, guest schedule.
- Fast payment (pay on job completion, like Turno's checkout-triggers-payment).
- Off-peak fill-in work.
- Exclusivity or priority in their area as a founding tradie.

**LeadHive advantage:** Joe already sells to and works with tradies. Offer LeadHive clients first access to HostFix jobs, and HostFix tradies a LeadHive package. Each business feeds the other's supply.

## 4. Run supply like Turno
Turno's mechanics (55,000+ cleaners) worth copying:
- Each property has a **primary and 1–2 backup** providers; jobs auto-forward to the backup if the primary doesn't accept in time.
- Provider profiles show: fixed price, distance, jobs completed, year joined, **background check / licence / insurance badges**, and ratings from hosts.
- Accepting a provider is non-binding until they're assigned to a property.
- Check-in and check-out in the provider app, a **photo checklist required before completion**, and payment triggered automatically on check-out.
- Short checklists only: "the longer the list, the less they read".

## 5. Make the tradie side effortless (Breezeway)
- Field staff use their own phones. If the app feels like a database, they won't use it; it has to feel like Instagram.
- Collect **validated information**: before and after photos on every job. That removes surprises for owners and protects tradies in disputes.

## 6. Doing it by hand (SidelineSwap's "hand-to-hand combat")
SidelineSwap's founders held inventory at their own houses and personally ran the selling in Facebook groups (the founder calls it "catfishing", posing as the athletes). The HostFix version, minus the deception:
- Joe personally dispatches and follows up on every early job.
- Joe posts finished-job stories (with permission) in local host groups.
- Joe fills supply gaps himself, by phoning around to find a tradie, until the network is deep enough.

Hand work doesn't scale; replace it with tools once the flywheel turns (SidelineSwap later built an eBay import tool for power sellers). The HostFix equivalent: import from property management systems for managers.

## 7. Supply health metrics
- Acceptance time (target under 15 min), completion time (target under 24 h), share of jobs needing a backup, tradie no-shows, and tradie earnings per week from HostFix (tradies stay when the work is meaningful).
