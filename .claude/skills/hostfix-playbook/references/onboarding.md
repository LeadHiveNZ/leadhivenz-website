# Onboarding & Setup

Built from: Turno's setup flow (integrations, properties, team, checklists, automations), Breezeway (photos, field adoption), Blomfield and Alströmer (customer success and the onboarding gap), Walling (free onboarding as a launch incentive). Adapt as the product evolves.

Why it matters: Blomfield cites a company with $4M of signed contracts but under $2M actually implemented. Onboarding is where deals become usage. Alströmer: onboarding is "the missing step" in founder sales.

## 1. Owner onboarding (small hosts): target under 20 minutes
1. **Welcome text within 1 hour** of payment: what happens next, and book the setup time.
2. **Property profile** for each property: address; lockbox or keypad code (and how it's changed; Rusteen recommends keypad locks over smart locks); location of the **water mains, switchboard, gas shut-off and hot water cylinder** (with photos); appliance brands; Wi-Fi; parking; normal check-in and check-out times.
3. **Who can log jobs:** add the cleaner (name, mobile) and turn on guest QR reporting. Print or send the **QR code sticker** for inside the property (laundry, kitchen, guidebook).
4. **Approval rules:** auto-approve jobs under $X; approval needed above $X; who to text.
5. **Payment method** on file.
6. **Test job:** log a small real or practice job together so they see the 15-minute acceptance and the texts.
7. **Day-7 check-in call:** questions, plus "What else breaks in this place?" Ask for a referral.

Founding offer value-add: Joe (or a tradie) does the property walkthrough and photos for them.

## 2. Property manager onboarding (10+ properties)
1. Kick-off call with the **champion** (ops or maintenance lead): service area, property list, approval thresholds, invoicing (per property or monthly statement), reporting needs.
2. **Bulk property import:** spreadsheet first (Blomfield: use CSV, don't wait for integrations). Later: integrations with the property management systems they use. Turno integrates with 28, including Guesty, Hostaway, Hostfully, Hospitable, Smoobu, Uplisting and Lodgify.
3. Add their **cleaning teams** as reporters (cleaners are the eyes on the property, per Breezeway).
4. Agree **service levels** in writing: acceptance within 15 minutes, completion within 24 hours, after-hours rules, an emergency definition, and what happens if missed (credit).
5. Pilot structure: paid month 1 with an opt-out (see `sales-and-pricing.md`). Define success metrics upfront.
6. **Weekly report** for the first month: jobs, average acceptance and completion time, spend, issues. Then monthly.
7. **Day-30 review:** confirm results, expand to all properties, ask for a case study or testimonial.

## 3. Tradie onboarding
1. **Vetting:** licence (registration where applicable), public liability insurance certificate, ID, references or reviews, an agreement signed.
2. **Trades and service area;** availability windows; after-hours yes or no.
3. **Fixed price list** for the common jobs in their trade (agreed upfront; review quarterly).
4. **App setup:** accept and decline, check-in and check-out, the photo checklist (before and after photos required), job notes.
5. **Payment details:** pay on completion (Turno triggers payment at check-out); state the payment timing clearly.
6. **Primary or backup** assignment per area or property.
7. **First job shadowed:** Joe on the phone or on site. Feedback after.
8. Monthly: jobs sent, earnings, ratings. Address any declines or delays quickly.

## 4. Cleaner onboarding (reporters and referral partners)
1. Add them to the owner's or manager's account as a reporter.
2. Show them how to log a problem with photos in under 60 seconds. Keep the checklist short (Turno: long lists get ignored).
3. Partnership option for cleaning companies: a referral credit for each new owner they bring, and/or for jobs they report.

## 5. Onboarding checklist (copy per account)
- [ ] Paid / card on file
- [ ] Property profiles complete (access, mains, switchboard, cylinder, photos)
- [ ] Cleaner added; QR code placed
- [ ] Approval threshold set
- [ ] Test job completed
- [ ] Day-7 call done; referral asked
- [ ] (Managers) service levels signed, weekly report scheduled, day-30 review booked
