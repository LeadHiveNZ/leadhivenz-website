# Launch Plan & Metrics

Written on 19 Nov 2026 from the video lessons plus HostFix's state then (Queenstown launch, 25 founding properties at half price, 9 leads / 121+ properties including managers with 63 and 30 properties). Re-check the current status in the Drive sheets "HostFix Launch List" and "HostFix Leads" before acting.

## 14-day plan
1. **Day 1: speed to lead.** Text every lead on the launch list. Several leads, including a 30-property manager, had no follow-up yet. Book 10-minute calls.
2. **Days 1–3: lock in the two big managers** as founding manager partners on a paid month with a 30-day opt-out. Keep the 25-property cap for small owners only.
3. **Days 1–7: sign supply.** 2–3 top-rated Queenstown tradies (plumber, electrician, handyman) on agreed fixed prices, with a backup for each. Start with anyone in the LeadHive network. Don't promise 15 minutes / 24 hours anywhere they can't cover.
4. **Day 3 onwards: start doing jobs by hand.** Jobs come in by text; Joe dispatches, texts the owner updates and logs every job (time to accept, time to finish, price, satisfaction).
5. **Days 3–10: interview 10 hosts** about how they handle repairs today. Capture exact phrases and rewrite ads and the landing page with them.
6. **Days 5–14: join Queenstown/Wanaka host Facebook groups** and approach 2 cleaning companies about referral credits.
7. **Day 14: review.** Jobs done, service levels hit, repeat jobs, paid conversions, what to build next in the app.

## Metrics
**Product-market fit (Alströmer):** define the "value action" as a completed job and measure repeat usage.
- Jobs per property per month; % of properties with a 2nd job within 60 days; retention by sign-up month (a retention curve that flattens = good).
- Survey: "How would you feel if you could no longer use HostFix?" A large share of "very disappointed" answers is a strong signal.

**Service:** median acceptance time (target under 15 min); % completed within 24h; % needing a backup tradie; tradie declines; owner rating per job.

**Sales:** leads → contacted same day → calls → paid; properties under management; cost per founding property from ads.

**Economics:** revenue per job (margin or fee), revenue per property per month, tradie earnings per week from HostFix, churn.

## Watch-outs
- **Trough of sorrow** (Alströmer): after launch excitement, growth stalls for a while. Most startups die by quitting, not by competition (Supademo).
- **Leaky bucket:** don't scale ads before retention holds.
- **Founder focus:** HostFix only grows with Joe personally driving it. Decide weekly hours alongside LeadHive and Tipsy Tui and protect them.
- **Over-promising supply** in a tradie-short town is the biggest single risk to reputation.
