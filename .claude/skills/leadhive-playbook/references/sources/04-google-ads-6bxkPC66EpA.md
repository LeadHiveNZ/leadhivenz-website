### 3. CORE CONTENT

#### Step 1: Initial Campaign Objective & Bidding Setup (00:09 – 01:35)
* **Objective:** Click **"Create a campaign without guidance"** (00:19), then select **"Search"** as the campaign type (00:23). Skip conversion goal selection for initial setup by clicking **"Continue"** (00:30).
* **Naming Convention:** Format the campaign name using the vertical abbreviation, bid strategy, keyword type, and bid adjustment tag: `elec - Max clicks - generic - 300%` (or `CBA` for Call Bid Adjustment) (00:40 – 01:10).
* **Bidding Strategy:** Select **"Clicks"** (Maximize Clicks) (01:25). Set a maximum cost-per-click (CPC) limit of **$1.25** (01:30).

#### Step 2: Network & Location Settings (01:35 – 02:55)
* **Networks:** Uncheck both **Google Display Network** and **Search Partners** ("unselect everything here, because it's useless") (01:35 – 01:40).
* **Location Targeting:**
  * Open **"Advanced search"** $\rightarrow$ **"Add locations in bulk"** (02:40).
  * Paste target ZIP codes (02:42).
  * **Critical Setting:** Type **"United States"** in the location restriction dropdown box before saving, preventing Google from targeting matching ZIP code numbers in other countries (02:44 – 02:50).
  * **Location Option:** Select **"Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations"** (02:50).
* **Language:** Select **English** (02:55).

#### Step 3: Audience Segments & Ad Schedule (02:55 – 03:32)
* **Audience Segment:** Search for "home services" and select **"Home & Garden Services"** (In-market) (03:00 – 03:15).
* **Targeting Mode:** Choose **"Targeting"** (narrow reach) rather than "Observation" to filter out billing and customer support searchers (03:15 – 03:20).
* **Ad Schedule:** Set active operating hours (e.g., **Mondays – Fridays, 07:30 to 21:00 / 9:00 PM**) (03:20 – 03:30).

#### Step 4: AI Max for Search Campaigns [Beta] (03:32 – 04:15)
* Enable Google's **"AI Max for Search campaigns"** (Beta option) (03:35).
* Under **Asset Optimization**, keep **"Test customization"** enabled so Google can automatically test generative headlines, descriptions, and keywords while maintaining your fixed bidding strategy (03:40 – 04:10).

#### Step 5: Keyword & Asset Generation via ChatGPT (01:52 – 02:37 & 04:20 – 06:00)
* Open ChatGPT and generate bulk campaign assets using prompts (01:52 – 02:30).
* **Keywords Pasted into Ad Group:** `electrician near me`, `licensed electrician`, `emergency electrician`, `residential electrician`, `commercial electrician`, `24 hour repair`, `electrical repair`, `home wiring service`, `electrical contractors` (04:20 – 04:30).
* **Dynamic Keyword Insertion (DKI):** Use the syntax `{KeyWord:Call Now}` or `{KeyWord:Default Text}` inside headlines and descriptions (04:50 – 05:10, 05:45 – 05:55). Inserting DKI at least twice improves Ad Strength scores by aligning ad text directly with user search queries (05:55 – 06:00).

#### Step 6: Sitelink Extensions & Call-Redirect Tactic (06:15 – 07:15)
* Create sitelinks that redirect mobile users to initiate a direct phone call.
* **Sitelink 1:** Text: `Call Now` | Final URL: `https://youareallyamazing.com/electrician?call=1` (06:25 – 06:35).
* **Sitelink 2:** Text: `Call Right Away` | Final URL: `https://youareallyamazing.com/electrician?call=2` (06:55 – 07:10).
* **Technical Requirement:** Each sitelink must use a unique URL parameter (`?call=1`, `?call=2`) so Google accepts them as unique links, and the destination website must run the creator's **"PPC basic"** plugin to process the redirect into an immediate phone dialer action (06:40 – 07:10).

#### Step 7: Generative AI Image Assets (07:15 – 09:05)
* Under Ad Assets, select **"Images"** and choose **"Generated"** (Google AI Image Generator) (07:18 – 07:25).
* Input prompt to generate lifestyle visual assets (07:30 – 07:40).
* Select multiple aspect ratios (**1:1 Square** and **1.91:1 Landscape**) to maximize ad screen real estate (08:10 – 08:55).

#### Step 8: Lead Forms & Final URL Parameters (09:10 – 09:45)
* **Lead Forms:** Optional for direct search, but recommended for verticals like insurance where lead form completions capture opt-in consent for immediate automated SMS or outbound call follow-ups (09:15 – 09:30).
* **URL Options:** Under **"Ad URL options"**, append URL tracking parameters/suffixes to pass conversion data back to the landing page (09:35 – 09:45).

### 5. WARNINGS

1. **Bulk ZIP Code Location Error (02:40 – 02:50):** When pasting ZIP codes into the Google Ads bulk location box, you **must** type and select "United States" in the restriction dropdown. Failing to do so will cause Google to target identical numerical postal codes in international countries.
2. **Audience "Observation" vs. "Targeting" Pitfall (03:15 – 03:20):** Do not leave Audience Segments set to "Observation". Setting it to "Targeting" prevents waste spend from billing inquiries or customer support calls.
3. **Dynamic Keyword Insertion for Brands (06:00):** Never use dynamic keyword insertion `{KeyWord:...}` when targeting branded keywords, as it can cause trademarked or incorrect brand names to populate in your ad copy.
4. **Website Business Logo Rules (06:10):** Google Ads will reject arbitrary or non-matching logos. The uploaded logo must match the logo hosted directly on the destination landing page.
5. **Call-Redirect Sitelink Plugin Dependency (06:40 – 07:10):** The `?call=1` URL parameter tactic will **not** work unless the landing page has the custom **"PPC basic"** plugin installed.
6. **AI Image Conversion Caveat (08:25 – 08:40):** Large image assets increase ad real estate and overall click volume, but they can lower conversion rates due to accidental clicks or mobile "butt dials."
