## 3. CORE CONTENT

### Step 1: Campaign-Level Setup
* **Navigation:** Open Meta Ads Manager, select the **Campaigns** tab, and click **Create** [00:30].
* **Campaign Objective:** Select **Leads** [00:38].
* **Campaign Naming:** Titled `Bathroom Remodeling` in the demonstration [00:50]. The creator notes that while this example uses bathroom remodeling, the exact setup applies to all service-based contractors working with homeowners [01:00].
* **Daily Budget:** Set to **$50 to $100 AUD per day** (noted as approximately $35–$75 USD or £25–£50 GBP) [01:21]. Shown entering **$50.00 AUD** on screen [01:30].
* Click **Next** [01:35].

### Step 2: Ad Set Level Setup
* **Conversion Location:** Under *Single conversion location*, select **Instant forms** [01:40–02:05]. (Website forms are an option, but Instant Forms keep users within Facebook for faster loading and higher conversion) [01:45].
* **Facebook Page:** Select your business page (demonstration account `Daniel Dimsey` selected) [02:05].
* **Performance Goal:** Select **Maximize number of leads** [02:10]. Do *not* choose "Maximize number of conversion leads" [02:15].
* **Geographic Location & Targeting:**
  1. Click the edit pencil under *Locations*, type in your primary town or suburb (e.g., `Chadstone`), and click **Drop Pin** on your target center point [03:20–03:30].
  2. Set your service radius (e.g., 10 to 20 miles) around the pin [03:35].
  3. **Exclusion Pin Tactic:** Click **Drop Pin**, place pins just outside your targeted radius circle, and change the dropdown selection from *Include* to **Exclude** [03:40–04:05]. Repeat this all around the perimeter of your service circle to block Facebook from wasting budget in unserviced areas [04:30–04:45].
* **Audience Selection:** Skip custom audience suggestions or detailed interest targeting unless setting up a dedicated retargeting campaign [04:50]. Click **Next** [04:58].

### Step 3: Ad Level & Creative Setup
* **Ad Format:** Select **Single image or video** [05:15]. Single image ads are recommended for fast mass-testing without video editing overhead [05:20].
* **Image Creative Tactic:** Use personal photos of the business owner or crew rather than stock photos or before/after images [05:50–06:10]. On screen, an image of two crew members with an overlaid Google rating badge (`4.9 ★★★★★ (97)`) is selected [05:50].
* **Advantage+ Enhancements (06:54–07:08):**
  * **Keep ON:** *Add overlays*, *Visual touch-ups*, and *Add music* [06:54–07:08].
  * **Turn OFF:** *Text improvements*, *Add animation*, *Enhance CTA*, and *Flex media* [06:58–07:04].
* **Text & Positioning Strategy:** 
  * The image stops the user's scroll [07:20].
  * Primary text builds trust via long-form storytelling structured like a friendly community noticeboard post starting with *"Hello Neighbour's"* [07:35].
  * Headline acts as a clear call to action [07:20].
* **Headline:** Enter `Get Your Free Quote` [09:15].
* **Description:** Add social proof right below the call to action, e.g., `Over 350+ Completed Jobs` [09:35–09:40].
* **Call to Action (CTA) Button:** Select `Get quote` from the dropdown list [10:04].

### Step 4: Instant Form Creation Setup
* Scroll to Destination and click **Create form** [10:30].
* **Form Type & Sharing:** Select **More volume** and set Sharing settings to **Open** [10:40].
* **Intro:** Turn off / toggle off the **Greeting** section to remove unnecessary steps [10:45].
* **Questions:** Add **3 to 4 custom questions** (using multiple choice or short answer) to filter for lead intent (e.g., preferred contact method: phone/email/SMS, project urgency, or scope) [10:50–11:10].
* **Contact Information:** Request **Full name**, **Email address**, **Phone number**, and **Street address** [11:15–11:25].
* **Privacy Policy:** Add a link to your website privacy policy or a hosted Google Doc privacy policy statement [11:25–11:35].
* **Ending Page ("Message for leads"):**
  * **Headline:** `Thanks, you're all set.` [11:40].
  * **Description:** Inform prospects when to expect a call/quote (e.g., within 24 to 48 hours) [11:45].
  * **Additional Action:** Select **Call business** to enable high-intent leads to call immediately [11:55].

## 5. WARNINGS

1. **Do Not Use "Maximize Conversion Leads" Optimization:** The creator warns against choosing "Maximize number of conversion leads" in performance goals [02:15]. Based on his ad spend testing, it increases lead acquisition costs without improving lead quality. Higher quality comes from adding multi-question fields to the form itself [02:35–02:50].
2. **Prevent Location Bleed / Wasted Ad Spend:** Meta frequently pushes ad impressions outside specified geographic radii [03:40]. Failing to manually place exclusion pins around your service boundary results in paying for leads outside your service territory [03:45–04:00].
3. **Avoid Generic Corporate / Discount Ads:** Avoid short, generic ad blurbs, stock imagery, before/after photos, or leading with discount prices (e.g., "20% off") [08:25–09:20]. Discount offers attract low-budget, price-sensitive leads ("cheaper people") rather than premium clients [09:20].
4. **Remove Form Intros:** Avoid leaving the optional greeting/intro page enabled on Instant Forms; it creates unnecessary friction and drops conversion rates [10:45].
