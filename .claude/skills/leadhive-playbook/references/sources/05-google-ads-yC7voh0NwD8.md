#### B. Mechanics of Google Local Services Ads (LSA) (06:00 – 14:50)
* **Placement & Format:** Positioned at the very top of Google search results on desktop and mobile, above PPC ads, map packs, and organic SEO results (06:15, 09:20).
* **Google Guaranteed Badge:** Features a green checkmark badge signifying consumer protection up to $2,000 back from Google if the contractor fails to deliver satisfactory work (06:25, 07:10).
* **Pay-Per-Lead (PPL) Model:** Charges contractors per qualified lead generated through Google-tracked phone numbers rather than per click (06:55).
* **Background Check Requirement:** Requires business owners and all field technicians entering customers' homes to pass background checks (originally via Pinkerton, now handled by third-party consulting vendors) (10:01 – 11:10).
* **Cost Per Lead (CPL) & Budgeting (11:31 – 13:00):**
  * Average CPL ranges between **$25 and $45 per lead** (varies by trade and market competitiveness) (11:31).
  * Budget calculation example: A $2,000/month budget breaks down to $500/week or $71/day spend limit (12:20).
  * Lead calculation example: At a $35 average CPL with a $500/week budget, a company receives ~57 leads/month, ~14 leads/week, or ~2 leads/day (12:45).
  * Cap mechanic: Once the set weekly budget cap is reached, the profile automatically goes offline until the next week (12:55).
* **Disputable Lead Criteria (13:45 – 14:40):** Contractors are only charged for valid service inquiries within their specified service areas and job categories. Credits are issued for:
  1. Solicitations and sales pitches (13:55).
  2. Spam / pre-recorded robotic calls (14:15).
  3. Inquiries for locations outside designated service areas (14:18).
  4. Inquiries for services not offered by the business (14:20).

#### C. LSA Algorithm Ranking Factors (20:00 – 24:15)
1. **Proximity to Customer:** Distance between the customer’s IP address/mobile device location and the business location (20:10).
2. **Review Rating & Verified Review Volume:** Overall Google rating combined with the volume of reviews requested directly through the native LSA dashboard (20:50).
3. **Responsiveness / Call Answer Rate:** Google AI tracks if incoming call tracking numbers are answered by a live representative (targeting a **95%–100% answer rate**). Unanswered calls lower profile rotation even if a large budget remains (21:45 – 22:45).
4. **Designated Working / Office Hours:** Listings only display during hours set as active/open (22:50).
5. **Customer Complaints:** High volume of complaints or guarantee refund requests submitted to Google negatively impacts ranking (23:35).

#### D. The 5-Step Process to Win with LSA (24:20 – 26:45)
1. **Dial in the Profile, Service Area, and Budget (24:25).**
2. **Mark Incoming Jobs as Booked (24:55).**
3. **Audit and Dispute Invalid Leads (25:10).**
4. **Drive Verified Native Reviews (25:25).**
5. **Target an 85% Booked Job Benchmark Rate (26:10).**

#### E. Campaign & Profile Optimization Settings (27:00 – 31:30)
* **Budget Strategy:** Set high spending caps (e.g., set a $20,000/month budget even if actual spend is expected to be $10,000) so budget restrictions never throttle lead delivery (27:30 – 28:15).
* **Ad Schedule & Hours:** Extend active working hours into evenings and weekends. After-hours calls feature lower contractor competition and higher urgency leads (28:25 – 29:10).
* **Business Bio:** Complete all bio fields to serve as a sales letter highlighting unique selling propositions (USPs) and core services (29:11).
* **Job Types:** Select all congruent job categories, including diagnostic/symptomatic repair jobs that lead to high-ticket system replacements (29:26, 41:30).
* **Service Areas:** Select zip codes and cities up to 25+ miles out, prioritizing high-affluence geographic areas (29:41 – 30:20).
* **Authentic Photos:** Avoid generic stock photos. Upload authentic photos showing company owners, uniformed technicians, branded service vans, and technicians interacting with clients in homes (30:30 – 31:10).

#### F. Dashboard Feedback Loop & KPI Benchmarks (31:35 – 40:00)
* **Marking Outcomes:** Log into the LSA portal daily to mark incoming calls as "Booked" or "Scheduled," entering customer details (name, email address, job value) (31:40, 32:50).
* **Archiving:** Mark and archive non-converted or lost opportunities to maintain clean dashboard data (33:00).
* **Lead Disputes:** Listen to call recordings and submit disputes on non-qualified leads to recover budget spend (33:45).
* **Verified Reviews:** Enter completed customer emails directly into the LSA dashboard to trigger native verified review requests (34:30 – 37:10).
* **Target KPI Benchmark:** Maintain an **85% booking conversion rate** benchmark within the LSA dashboard metrics (37:30 – 39:40).

#### G. Tactics to Increase Lead Volume When Tapped Out (40:15 – 43:25)
1. Increase weekly/monthly budget caps (e.g., move from $2,000/month to $5,000/month+) (40:50).
2. Expand service area radii, add repair job categories, and broaden office/ad schedule hours (41:10 – 42:30).
3. Consistently update and close out all lead records (mark booked or archived) (42:40).
4. Systematically request native verified reviews on 100% of completed jobs (42:55).

#### H. Full Accelerated Growth Framework Overview (53:05 – 56:30)
* **Drive Leads:** SEO, Google Maps, PPC ads, LSA, and database reactivation offers to past clients (53:15 – 53:50).
* **Maximize Conversion:** High-converting website design, multi-platform review management (Google, Yelp, Angie), and automated two-way SMS/email follow-up (53:55 – 54:40).
* **Optimize Results:** Align marketing spend with revenue goals, optimize average CPL per trade/service, and target a **5:1 ROI** ($5 in revenue returned for every $1 invested) (54:45 – 56:05).

### 5. WARNINGS

* **Setting 24/7 Hours Without Coverage:** Setting ad schedules to 24/7 without a live representative to answer calls damages answer rate metrics, causing Google AI to lower search visibility (28:35).
* **Leaving Call Outcomes Unmarked:** Leaving incoming leads unclassified inside the dashboard defaults the recorded booking rate to 0%, signaling to Google AI that calls are not resulting in booked jobs (38:40 – 38:55).
* **Relying Solely on External Automated Review Software:** Using third-party CRM text/email review software (such as ServiceTitan, Nearby Now, or Review Buzz) misses the LSA verified review designation; LSA verified reviews must be sent natively through the LSA dashboard (25:00 – 26:00, 36:00 – 36:15).
* **Failing to Audit & Dispute Leads:** Allowing non-qualified calls (out of area, spam, unoffered services) to remain un-disputed wastes ad spend at $25–$75+ per lead (14:25, 33:45 – 34:15).
* **Using Generic Stock Photos:** Uploading generic stock images instead of real team, truck, and job site photos decreases profile conversion (30:50).
