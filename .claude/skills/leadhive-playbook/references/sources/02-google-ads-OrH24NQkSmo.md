## CORE CONTENT

### 1. Initial Campaign Settings (00:22 - 04:30)
*   **Campaign Objective:** Select "Create a campaign without guidance" to maintain full control over the account and avoid Google’s generalized recommendations.
*   **Campaign Type:** Search.
*   **Conversion Focus:** While the ultimate goal is leads (form submissions or phone calls), Google lacks sufficient data at the start to optimize for conversions efficiently.
*   **Bidding Strategy:** Start with **Clicks**. Set a Maximum CPC (Cost-Per-Click) bid limit. The benchmark for plumbing is suggested at **$15.00** per click to remain competitive without overspending.
*   **Networks:**
    *   **Search Network:** Turn OFF Google Search Partners.
    *   **Display Network:** Turn OFF Google Display Network. These are typically low-quality traffic sources for local services.
*   **Location Options:**
    *   **Targeting:** Select "Presence: People in or regularly in your included locations."
    *   **Mistake to avoid:** Do not use the default "Presence or interest" setting, as it will attract clicks from users outside the service area.
*   **Location Targeting:** Input the specific city (e.g., St. Louis) or set a radius (e.g., 20 miles) around the central business location.
*   **Ad Schedule:** Run ads only during business hours (e.g., 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM) initially to ensure the phone can be answered immediately. Only expand to 24/7 if the budget allows and a nighttime answering service is in place.

### 2. Ad Group and Keyword Strategy (04:30 - 08:30)
*   **Keyword Match Types:**
    *   **Broad Match:** Avoid entirely. It results in poor quality and wasted spend.
    *   **Exact Match:** Preferred for specific high-intent terms (e.g., `[plumbers in St. Louis]`).
    *   **Phrase Match:** Useful for slightly broader intent (e.g., `"plumbing services near me"`).
*   **Ad Group Segmentation:** The creator recommends copying a general ad group and creating six distinct, service-specific ad groups:
    1.  General Plumbing.
    2.  Emergency 24-Hour Plumbing.
    3.  Drain Cleaning/Clogged Drains.
    4.  Water Heater Service.
    5.  Leak Detection and Repair.
    6.  Toilet Fixture Repair and Installation.

### 3. Ad Copy and Asset Creation (08:30 - 16:30)
*   **Headline Structure:**
    *   **Headline 1:** Focus on relevance. Use **Dynamic Keyword Insertion** (syntax: `{KeyWord:Plumbers in St. Louis}`) so the headline matches the user’s search term exactly.
    *   **Headline 2:** Focus on the offer (e.g., "$100 Off Emergency Plumbing").
    *   **Headline 3:** Focus on the Call to Action (e.g., "Get an Estimate," "Call Now").
*   **Display Path:** Customize to increase relevance (e.g., `max-conversion.com/Plumber/St-Louis`).
*   **Ad Assets (Extensions):**
    *   **Business Name/Logo:** Requires Google verification but is mandatory for trust.
    *   **Sitelinks:** Link to specific pages like "About Us," "Service Area," "Contact Us," or "Commercial Services." Use the `?s=1` parameter to differentiate duplicate URLs if they point to the same landing page.
    *   **Callouts:** Non-clickable text highlights like "500+ Five Star Reviews," "24/7 Emergency Service," or "Licensed and Insured."
    *   **Structured Snippets:** Use the "Service Catalog" type to list services (e.g., Water Heater, Drain Cleaning).
    *   **Call Asset:** Mandatory. Add the business phone number.
    *   **Price Assets:** Highly recommended. Add at least three (e.g., "Water Heater Replacement starting at $1,500"). This pre-qualifies traffic by filtering out price-sensitive users before they click.
    *   **Promotion Assets:** Highlight seasonal or specific discounts (e.g., "Up to $100 off Water Heater Service").

### 4. Audience and Demographics (21:13 - 22:20)
*   **Audience Segments:** Use **Observation Mode**. Add segments related to "Plumbing Services," "Home Improvement," and "Shoppers." Collect data first, then adjust bids based on performance.
*   **Age Exclusions:** The creator recommends excluding the **18-24** age bracket as they are rarely homeowners.
*   **Audience Exclusions:** Consider excluding "Renters" and "DIYers" (Do-It-Yourselfers) to focus on homeowners needing professional intervention.

### 5. Negative Keyword List (19:50 - 21:10)
*   **AI Generation:** Use an AI tool (Claude/ChatGPT) to generate a list of negatives based on high-volume, low-intent terms.
*   **General Exclusions:** terms like "DIY," "How to," "YouTube," "cheap," "jobs," "career," "salary," "parts," "Home Depot," "Lowe's," and "Amazon."
*   **Competitor Exclusions:** Search for top competitors on Google Maps and add their names to the negative list to avoid paying for clicks for users seeking specific companies.

### 6. Landing Page and Conversion Optimization (14:15 - 19:40)
*   **Landing Page vs. Website:** General websites perform poorly. Specific landing pages for each service (e.g., a dedicated Water Heater page) are essential for high conversion rates.
*   **Speed Index:** The landing page must load in **under 2 seconds** on mobile. If it is slower, you will be charged for clicks from users who bounce before the page loads. Tools recommended: Google PageSpeed Insights and DebugBear.
*   **Landing Page Design:**
    *   Floating "Call Now" and "Get Estimate" buttons.
    *   Trust signals: Google Review count, Better Business Bureau logos.
    *   Service area maps.
    *   Simplified lead forms (Name, Email, Phone, Service needed).

## WARNINGS
*   **Broad Match Pitfall:** Avoid using broad match keywords; they will exhaust your budget on unrelated searches like "plumbing salary" or "how to fix a faucet."
*   **The "Interest" Trap:** In Location settings, never leave the default "Presence or Interest" selected. Users from other states searching for "St. Louis plumbers" will trigger your ads, wasting budget.
*   **Search Partners and Display:** Always turn these off for local service campaigns. They provide low-intent traffic that rarely converts for urgent plumbing needs.
*   **Landing Page Speed:** A slow page is a wasted budget. You pay for the click even if the user bounces because the page took 5 seconds to load.
*   **Conversion Data Warning:** Do not switch to "Maximize Conversions" bidding until the account has at least 30 days of data and consistent lead tracking.
