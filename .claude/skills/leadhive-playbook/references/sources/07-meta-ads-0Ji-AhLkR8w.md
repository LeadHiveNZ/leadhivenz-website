### 2. SUMMARY
This video teaches digital marketers, agency owners, and business owners how to leverage eight new Meta lead-generation features and ad set/form settings across Facebook and Instagram. Ben Heath walks viewers through Meta Ads Manager to demonstrate new optimization settings, instant form controls, AI dynamic form delivery, direct lead messaging, and post-submission actions. ### 3. CORE CONTENT

#### Update 1: Dual Conversion Location — Website and Instant Forms (00:32 – 01:37)
* **Location in Ads Manager:** Ad Set level $\rightarrow$ Conversion $\rightarrow$ Conversion location (00:32).
* **Option Details:** 
  * *HEARD:* Ben explains that advertisers previously had to choose strictly between sending traffic to a website (higher lead quality, lower volume) or using instant forms (higher lead volume, lower quality, cheaper CPL).
  * *SEEN:* Meta UI option titled **"Website and instant forms: Generate leads through both your website and instant forms"** (00:50).
* **Tactical Application:** Select this dual setting to allow Meta’s machine learning to allocate impressions dynamically based on whether individual users are more likely to convert on an external landing page or directly inside an instant form (01:15). Ben recommends testing this immediately if an ad account has struggled to choose between website landing pages and instant lead forms (01:28).

#### Update 2: SMS Verification via One-Time Passcode (01:37 – 03:59)
* **Location in Ads Manager:** Ad level $\rightarrow$ Create form $\rightarrow$ Form type (01:44).
* **Option Details:** 
  * *SEEN:* Under "Form type", selecting **"Higher intent"** reveals a checkbox: **"Require leads to verify their phone number with a one-time passcode"** (01:54). 
  * *SEEN (Preview):* Displays a mobile mock-up titled "Phone Verification" where a prospect receives a 6-digit SMS passcode (valid for 10 minutes) and must type it into an "Enter the code" field before submitting (02:00, 02:58).
* **Tactical Application:** 
  * *HEARD:* Forces prospects to submit real phone numbers rather than fake or unmonitored numbers (02:35).
  * *HEARD:* Prevents automated spam/bot lead submissions and filters out low-intent users who refuse to undergo SMS verification (02:25, 03:00).
  * *HEARD:* Drastically increases lead quality for sales teams, though advertisers must expect a significant increase in cost per lead (CPL) and a reduction in raw volume (03:15).

#### Update 3: Turning Off Autofill for Contact Fields (04:37 – 06:01)
* **Location in Ads Manager:** Form Creation $\rightarrow$ Questions / Contact Information (05:42).
* **Option Details:** 
  * *SEEN:* UI box titled "Choose the type of information you need" displaying field items for Email and Phone number, each accompanied by an **"Autofill"** toggle checkbox (05:42).
* **Tactical Application:** 
  * *HEARD:* Standard Meta instant forms autofill user details from profile data created years prior, often yielding outdated email addresses or old phone numbers (05:20). 
  * *HEARD:* Unchecking "Autofill" forces users to type contact details manually (05:40). This acts as a mild friction layer—less severe than SMS verification—to verify current contact details and ensure prospects consciously remember submitting the form (05:50).

#### Update 4: Flexible Form Delivery (AI Form Optimization) (06:01 – 08:23)
* **Location in Ads Manager:** Form Creation window $\rightarrow$ Flexible form delivery (06:10).
* **Option Details:**
  * *SEEN:* Text reads: **"Allow Meta to adjust or remove form elements based on what people are most likely to respond to."** Options include **"Optimized"** (Meta tailors elements per user) and **"Manual"** (06:14).
* **Tactical Application:**
  * *HEARD:* Replaces blanket marketer decisions about form length (e.g., short vs. rich greeting/questions) by allowing Meta’s AI to deliver a shorter form to quick-action users and a longer, detailed form to users requiring more context (07:05, 07:40).
  * *HEARD:* Ben notes this is toggled ON by default for most accounts and recommends leaving it enabled unless operating under strict legal or regulatory requirements (07:58).

#### Update 5: Email Delivery for Leads (08:24 – 09:00)
* **Location in Meta Suite:** Ads Manager / Leads Center $\rightarrow$ Lead delivery settings (08:28, 08:36).
* **Option Details:**
  * *SEEN:* Modal titled "Set up email delivery" allowing selection of recipient email addresses, frequency days (M, Tu, W, Th, F, Sa, Su), daily send time (e.g., 09:00 AM), and file format (.CSV) (08:42).
* **Tactical Application:**
  * *HEARD:* Bypasses manual downloads from Meta Lead Center or complex CRM integration setups for small operations, sending lead notifications directly to inbox workflows to ensure fast follow-up (08:35, 08:48).

#### Update 6: Direct Messaging Follow-Up via Messenger / Instagram (09:00 – 11:08)
* **Location in Ads Manager:** Ad level $\rightarrow$ Follow up with leads (09:06).
* **Option Details:**
  * *SEEN:* Checkbox: **"Chat with leads from instant form. After a lead submits your instant form and agrees to receive messages from your business, a chat that includes their contact information will start on Messenger or Instagram."** (09:06, 10:02).
* **Tactical Application:**
  * *HEARD:* Opens an immediate chat thread on Facebook Messenger or Instagram right after submission (09:15). Converts standard cold lead forms into live chat conversations, bypassing email drop-off or unmonitored phone calls (09:25).

#### Update 7: Additional Action — Chat via WhatsApp (11:56 – 13:51)
* **Location in Ads Manager:** Form Creation $\rightarrow$ Ending $\rightarrow$ Additional action (12:02, 12:38).
* **Option Details:**
  * *SEEN:* Radio button option titled **"Chat on WhatsApp: Let people message you on WhatsApp."** Requires connecting a WhatsApp Business App Account (12:38).
* **Tactical Application:**
  * *HEARD:* Unlike Update 6 (which initiates an automatic chat if opted in), WhatsApp requires an explicit user tap on the thank-you screen (13:06).
  * *HEARD:* Highly effective in international markets like the UK, where WhatsApp is the dominant communication channel, though less widely adopted in the US (13:20).

#### Update 8: Additional Action — Redeem Promo Code (13:52 – 15:15)
* **Location in Ads Manager:** Form Creation $\rightarrow$ Ending $\rightarrow$ Additional action (13:54).
* **Option Details:**
  * *SEEN:* Radio button: **"Redeem promo code: Reward people with a promo code they can redeem on your website."** (13:54).
* **Tactical Application:**
  * *HEARD:* Presents an instant discount code on the form completion screen (13:58). Useful for warming up cold prospects who are not yet ready for a direct purchase by offering an incentive that transitions them smoothly into a website store checkout (14:15, 14:30).

### 5. WARNINGS & CAVEATS

1. **SMS Verification CPL Spike (03:10 – 03:29):** Enabling phone passcode verification substantially decreases total lead volume and increases cost per lead (CPL) due to the added friction. Only use this if your sales team is wasting significant time on uncontactable or low-quality leads.
2. **Stale Profile Contact Data (05:20 – 05:40):** Relying on default autofill forms often feeds outdated email addresses and inactive phone numbers registered to old Facebook/Instagram accounts into your CRM.
3. **Regulated Industry Risks with Flexible Form Delivery (08:00 – 08:20):** Turning on Meta's automatic AI form optimization ("Flexible form delivery") can cause Meta to drop certain form elements. If your business operates in a strictly regulated field (e.g., finance, legal, healthcare) requiring specific legal disclaimers or mandatory field disclosures, set this option to **Manual** instead of **Optimized**.
4. **Staffing and Infrastructure Overhead for Chat Features (10:40 – 11:00):** Enabling Messenger, Instagram, or WhatsApp chat follow-ups requires active sales reps or integrated AI chatbots. If your business cannot handle instant chat responses, do not activate these options as delayed responses harm conversion rates.
5. **Redundant Friction on Direct Promo Offers (14:38 – 14:52):** If your primary goal is driving immediate e-commerce sales via a promo code for cold traffic, running a lead ad form just to deliver a discount code adds unnecessary friction. Run a direct **Sales campaign** instead.
