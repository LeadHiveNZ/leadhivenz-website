## 1. Get more clients: the Core Four (Hormozi, $100M Leads)

Every lead comes from one of four sources, each done by you or leveraged through others:

| Source | You do it | Others do it (leverage) |
|---|---|---|
| Warm outreach | Your network, past Scrub & Shine / Tipsy Tui contacts, tradies you know | **Referrals** from happy clients, affiliates (accountants, trade wholesalers, vehicle sign-writers) |
| Cold outreach | Your calls/DMs to tradies | A hired appointment setter |
| Content | Your social content (you built 15k followers before; reuse that skill) | Editors, clients' testimonials, collabs |
| Paid ads | LeadHive's own ads to tradies | An agency/contractor |

Rules:
- **More stuff × better stuff = more leads.** Give value first: free audits ("here's where your Google listing ranks 10 km away"), free test leads, SERP teardowns.
- **Engaged leads beat raw contacts.** Warm prospects up before pitching.
- **100 → 10 → 1** for cold outreach, with triple specificity (one person, one pain, one solution).
- Messaging = **Problem → Agitate → Solution → Offer**.
- **Plateaus happen when one source is maxed** (common at ~$100k, $1M, $10M). Add the next source rather than pushing harder on the current one.
- Referrals come when you make clients look like heroes to their mates, not mainly from paying commission. Still offer a referral bonus to tradies (they refer other trades in other regions).

**LeadHive priority order now:** (1) free-leads-first outreach in new regions, (2) referrals from your 12 NZ clients (each knows tradies in other trades and towns), (3) Joe-led content showing real results, (4) paid ads to tradies once the offer converts predictably.

## 2. Keep clients: retention beats acquisition

$100M Leads: a 10% lift in retention can beat a 50% cut in acquisition cost for lifetime value. In a retainer business this is the biggest lever.
- Prove value monthly: calls, answered %, booked jobs, estimated revenue, reviews gained (the LSA "mark booked" discipline).
- Fix the client's side: answer rate (target 95%+), speed to lead, quoting.
- Give ownership (customer data, recordings, reviews) so the "rented leads" fear fades (`lead-gen-model.md`).
- Track churn by trade, region and package, and treat every cancellation as a post-mortem.

## 3. Raise lifetime value: the value ladder

Hormozi's ladder ($7 → $70 → $700 → $7,000) applied to LeadHive:
- Starter → Growth → Dominator (more leads, more service lines).
- Add-ons: Meta campaigns for planned work, Google Business Profile management, LSA management where available, review generation, a missed-call text-back system, a website/landing page build.
- Multi-trade bundles for businesses that cover plumbing and gas, or electrical and solar.

## 4. Systemise to remove Joe

From the 10-stage roadmap: each stage's constraint appears as headcount grows, across 8 functions (marketing, sales, product/delivery, customer success, finance, people, IT, ops). Stage-by-stage specifics: get the full PDF.

Practical sequence for LeadHive:
1. **Productise delivery:** one campaign template per trade (Google Ads checklist in `google-ads-trades.md`), one landing-page template, one tracking setup, one reporting template.
2. **Automate reporting and lead delivery** (call tracking + CRM + scheduled reports).
3. **Hire for delivery first** (campaign manager/VA following the checklists), so Joe stays on sales.
4. **Then hire sales:** an appointment setter, then a closer, trained on `sales-process.md` and recorded calls.
5. The Faizan course's "Build & Release" idea (consult for 3–6 months, then hand the system over) is *not* a fit for LeadHive's recurring model. Keep done-for-you retainers; recurring revenue is the asset.

## 5. Expansion rules (NZ → AU, new trades, small towns)
- Before selling a region/trade, run a 2–3 week test campaign to learn real cost per lead and volume, especially in small populations where search volume may not support 15+ leads a month.
- If volume is thin: widen the service radius, add adjacent services, or offer a smaller package.
- One client per trade per region is a scarcity-based sales asset ("you're the only plumber we'll work with in Hamilton"). Use it in every close.
