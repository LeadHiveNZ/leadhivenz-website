# Sales Process — LeadHive Playbook

Distilled from: Alex Hormozi (CLOSER framework, full 9-min breakdown + 1-min clip), Alex Berman / Experiment 27 (agency discovery call), Alex Beck (closing for Iman Gadzhi), the Faizan lead-gen agency course (sales section), and James Kuck's "free leads first" close. Full briefs in `sources/`.

## 1. The LeadHive sales call (one-call close for tradies)

Tradies are busy, sceptical of "marketing guys", and have probably been burned by Builderscrack/hipages or an agency. Keep it 20–30 minutes, phone or Zoom, on their schedule. Structure, merging the frameworks:

**0. Frame (first 2 minutes).** Be sharp, genuinely interested, and sound like an expert by asking pointed questions about their lead flow rather than small talk (Beck).
> "How we'll do this: I'll ask a few questions to see where the business is at and whether we can actually help. If we can, I'll show you how it works and you can decide. If we can't, I'll tell you straight. Fair enough?" (Faizan / Hormozi's "fair enough")

**1. C — Clarify why they're here.** "What made you reply / take the call?" Push past "just looking for info": "You're not jumping on calls all day for info. What's going on with the work coming in?"

**2. Goals first (Beck's rollercoaster).** Ask about the future state first, so they're on a high: "Where do you want the business in 12 months? More vans, more jobs, more of a certain kind of job, less of the cheap stuff?" Get numbers: jobs per week, average job value, revenue target.

**3. L — Label the problem.** "So what I'm hearing is: you want 10 more good jobs a week, but the work's patchy and too much of it comes from shared-lead sites. That about right?"

**4. O — Overview past attempts (the pain cycle), ending low.** "What have you tried? How did it go? What was good, what was bad? What else?" (Ask about Builderscrack/hipages, Facebook, SEO agencies, word of mouth.) End discovery on the gap: "If nothing changes, do you get to [goal]?" Ending on the struggle makes the pitch the way back up.

**5. S — Sell the vacation (3 points, with stories).** Tie each point to something they said (Problem → Feature → Solution, from Beck):
1. *Exclusive:* "Builderscrack sent your last lead to four other sparkies. Ours go to you only. You're the only [trade] we work with in [region]."
2. *Emergency-intent Google leads:* "People searching 'emergency plumber [suburb]' at 9pm. We track every call so you see exactly what came in."
3. *Proof:* a short case study. Client problem → what we did → result in leads/jobs/revenue (Berman's 3-part case study). Better still: "Remember the two calls you got last week with the LeadHive whisper? That's what this is" (Kuck).

**6. E — Explain away concerns before price.** Clear obstacles *before* the number (Hormozi): service area, which job types, how leads arrive, answer-rate expectations, contract length, and "do I own anything?" (see `lead-gen-model.md`).

**7. Alignment, then price.** "How do you feel about this getting you to [their goal]?" Let them sell themselves (Beck). Then anchor: "Given you want 25+ exclusive jobs a month, I'll be upfront: this isn't the cheap option." Then state it: "$500 setup and $1,500 a month for 15–25 exclusive leads." **Then stay silent.** Never say "it's only…" (Hormozi). Do the math with them: "Your average job is $X. If you close half of 20 leads, that's $Y for $1,500."

**8. Close.** Neutral/negative questions: "Fair enough?" / "Would it be crazy to get you live next week?" (Hormozi, after Chris Voss). Assume the close and **take payment on the call**: open Stripe and send the link while they're still on the phone, or take the card details. Don't email a link later (Beck). Book the onboarding call before you hang up (Berman: set the next meeting during this call).

## 2. Objection handling (Hormozi's three buckets)

Peel the layers in order:
1. **Circumstances:** time, money, fit.
   - *Money:* go back to the job-value math. Offer Starter rather than discounting.
   - *"Tried leads before, rubbish":* Hormozi's "burn you twice" reframe: "If you'd sworn off dating after your first break-up… Don't let a shared-lead site burn you twice." Then contrast exclusive vs. shared.
   - *"Can't take more work":* qualify. Offer a smaller package or a waitlist for the region. Busy tradies are your best future clients.
2. **Other people:** "need to talk to the wife / business partner." → "Do they know work's been patchy? If it was the other way round, would you back them?" Shift from asking permission to asking for support. Better: get the partner on the call.
3. **Self:** fear they won't follow through (answering calls, quoting fast). Reassure with process: lead alerts, speed-to-lead coaching, monthly review.

Don't argue or agree with complaints. Summarise and label them ("Sounds like they took your money and you never knew what you got") to stay consultative (Beck).

## 3. Discovery-call rules (Berman)
- Small talk under 2 minutes. No slide deck.
- Lead with a case study, then ask "Have you had similar issues with [X]?" If yes, go to process and proposal. If no, ask about goals, then tell a second matching case study.
- Mention price range at the *end* of discovery if you're doing a two-call process: "Typically clients in your trade invest $1,500–$2,200 a month. Does that ballpark work?"

## 4. Outbound to book calls

- **Free leads first (best for LeadHive):** route test-campaign leads to a target tradie with a whisper, then call (`lead-gen-model.md` §2).
- **Prospect lists:** tradies running Google Ads (budget proven), sponsored on directories (frustrated with shared leads), on Google pages 2–5, or with broken landing pages.
- **Hormozi's numbers:** 100 contacts → 10 responses → 1 meeting. Use triple specificity: one person, one pain, one solution.
- **Cold email/DM (Faizan):** short subject ("Quick question, [Name]"), a personalised first line, a result-based offer ("I help [trade] in [city] get 20+ exclusive jobs a month from Google"), a soft CTA ("Open to a chat?"). Max 3 follow-ups. If you scale cold email, warm up separate domains first (10–14 days) and verify lists. Never risk the main LeadHive domain.
- **Speed to lead applies to you too:** reply and call prospects within minutes.

## 5. Hand-off to onboarding
Before hanging up: payment taken, onboarding call booked, service area and job types confirmed, and their answer-rate commitment stated. Use `anthropic-skills:client-onboarding` for the onboarding process itself.
