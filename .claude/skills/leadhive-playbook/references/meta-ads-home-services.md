# Meta Ads for Home Services — LeadHive Playbook

Distilled from: Ben Heath (8 Meta lead-gen features), Spencer / Holiday Lights Northwest (video messaging ads), Daniel Dimsey (contractor instant-form setup — Melbourne example, AUD). Full briefs in `sources/`.

**Where Meta fits for LeadHive:** Meta creates demand; Google captures it. Nobody with a burst pipe is scrolling Instagram. Use Meta for *planned* jobs (hot water upgrades, bathroom/kitchen renos, solar, roofing, gutter/roof cleaning, landscaping, permanent lighting) and for upsell packages, not as the main emergency-lead engine.

## 1. Two proven campaign setups

### A. Instant-form lead campaign (Daniel Dimsey) — best default for quote-based trades
- Objective **Leads**. Budget **AUD $50–$100/day**.
- Conversion location **Instant forms**; performance goal **Maximize number of leads**. He says "Maximize conversion leads" raised cost without improving quality in his testing; get quality from form questions instead.
- Location: drop a pin on the suburb, 10–20 mile radius, then **drop Exclude pins all around the edge** — Meta bleeds outside radii.
- No detailed interests (broad), except dedicated retargeting.
- Creative: **real photo of the owner/crew** with a Google rating badge overlay (e.g. "4.9 ★ (97)"). Not stock, not before/after.
- Advantage+ creative: keep overlays, visual touch-ups, music; turn **off** text improvements, animation, enhance CTA, flex media.
- Headline `Get Your Free Quote`, description = social proof (`Over 350+ Completed Jobs`), CTA button `Get quote`.
- Form: **More volume**, sharing Open, **greeting off**, **3–4 qualifying questions** (urgency, scope, contact preference), name/email/phone/address, privacy policy link, thank-you screen telling them when you'll call + **"Call business"** button.
- Avoid leading with discounts ("20% off") — attracts price shoppers.

Copy template ("Hello Neighbours" community-post style):
> Hello Neighbours 👋 I'm [Name] with [Business], your local [trade] team right here in [City].
> For [X] years we've helped [City] homeowners [outcome]. [Personal line about loving the work.]
> One of the first questions we always get is… how much will [job] cost? Well… it really depends on [the key variable].
> Do you want [basic option] or [premium option]? Not all quotes are created equal.
> That's why we offer a complimentary [consultation/quote] so you can see your options and be confident you're getting exactly what you want without overspending.
> Just click "Get Quote" and I'll reach out right away. Talk soon, [Name]

### B. Video messaging campaign (Spencer) — cheap conversations for visual trades
- Objective **Engagement → Messaging apps** (Messenger/IG/WhatsApp).
- Budget start **$10/day**, scale to $20–30/day across several creatives.
- Pin on the town, ~14–15 mile radius, age **30–31 to 65+** (homeowners), interests empty.
- Single video, 45–60s of **raw job-site footage** + copyright-free music. No high production needed.
- Upload **5–7 video variations** under the same copy; let Meta pick the winner. Testing 1–2 creatives = failure.
- Benchmarks: **$7–$13 per message**; home-service leads should stay **under $20**; over $15 means refresh creative. Hook rate ~38%.
- Write copy outside Ads Manager (it glitches), paste in.

Script framework: **Hook / call out the town or pain → problem & solution → process (benefits, ease, safety) → simple offer with a believable reason → CTA "message/text us for a free quote" → scarcity (limited spots).** Drone shot of the local town as the opening frame grabs locals.

Example hook: "What's going on [Town] — quick question: why do Christmas lights only get used once a year?"

## 2. Lead-quality levers (Ben Heath)

Use these when clients complain about junk Meta leads:
1. **Conversion location "Website and instant forms"** — let Meta choose per user. Test it if you can't decide between landing page and form.
2. **Higher intent form + SMS one-time passcode** — kills fake numbers and bots. CPL goes up, volume down; use when clients waste time on uncontactable leads.
3. **Turn off autofill** on email/phone — forces typing, fixes stale profile data, mild friction.
4. **Flexible form delivery: Optimized** — Meta shortens/lengthens the form per person. Leave on unless compliance needs fixed fields.
5. **Email delivery of leads** (Leads Center → scheduled CSV email) — simple delivery without a CRM.
6. **Chat with leads** after submission (Messenger/IG) — only if someone answers instantly.
7. **WhatsApp additional action** on the thank-you screen — strong in markets where WhatsApp dominates.
8. **Promo code ending** — mainly e-commerce; skip for trades.

## 3. Creative research
Meta Ad Library (`facebook.com/ads/library`) → All ads → search the niche ("roof cleaning", "hot water", "bathroom renovation") → look for **video ads with pain hooks** that have run a long time → adapt the angle (not the asset).

## 4. LeadHive application
- Offer Meta as an **add-on for planned-work trades** or on the Growth/Dominator packages, not the core emergency product.
- Default build = Setup A for quote trades. For visual trades (roof wash, gutters, lighting, landscaping) test Setup B.
- Always: exclusion pins, 3–4 qualifying questions, autofill off. Escalate to SMS verification if junk persists.
- Get clients to supply 5–10 phone videos from jobs each month. Creative volume drives Meta results, so make it part of onboarding.
- Speed to lead: Meta leads go cold in minutes. Deliver by SMS/email instantly and coach clients to call within 5 minutes.
