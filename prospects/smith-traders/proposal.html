Proposal

More sellers for Smith Traders

A Facebook and Instagram campaign that finds NZ operators with trucks and machinery parked up, and puts them on your phone within minutes. With a seller page built to catch them.

What I saw

Your edge is real. Nobody with surplus gear is hearing about it.

You broker trucks and earthmoving gear on behalf of the owner, and the machine keeps working on contract until it sells. You do the viewings, the independent inspections and the negotiation. You have 76 units listed, buyers across NZ and the Pacific Islands, and names like McLeod Cranes, Regal Haulage and RJ Lincoln on your client list.

Every channel you use today is where buyers look: Deals on Wheels, Trade Me, MarketBook. Sellers find you by word of mouth. Your website was last updated in 2022, has no "sell your machine" page, no way to send you photos or machine details, and no tracking pixel, so even if someone did find you there, neither of us would know.

You are a supply business. Every listing you win is money you already know how to make. This proposal is about winning more listings.

The plan

From a scroll to your phone in three steps

1

Ads, NZ-wide

Facebook and Instagram ads built around one message: parked up? Keep it earning while Smith Traders sells it. Real footage of your listings and you to camera, no stock photos. Targeted at owner-operators, small fleets and civil, forestry and transport businesses, with your past buyer and seller list used to find more people like them.

2

A short form that qualifies

Machine type, make and model, year, hours or kms, region, finance owing, when they want it sold, and photos. The "just curious" option is deliberate. You see who is serious before you pick up the phone.

3

Straight to you

Every enquiry lands on your phone by text and in your inbox within minutes, plus a weekly sheet. You call, you appraise, you list. I report cost per lead, appraisals booked and listings won every month.

Where the ads land

A seller page built for one job

An ad from Facebook cannot land on your current site. There is no sell-your-machine page, no form, and no tracking, so neither of us would know what happened. Instead of rebuilding your website, I build and host a dedicated Smith Traders seller page on its own domain. Your site stays exactly as it is.

  • Your branding, your name, your phone number. It looks and reads like Smith Traders. It links back to smithtraders.co.nz and your stock feed.
  • One page, one job. A headline that matches the ad they tapped, the intake form, how it works, why not just auction it, who you sell for, you and Nicky, and a tap-to-call bar on mobile.
  • Tracked properly. Meta Pixel and analytics on the page, so we see cost per enquiry and can retarget people who read it but did not send their details.
  • Nothing for you to maintain. I host it, I update it, and when we learn which headline works I change it the same day.

I am not proposing a marketplace and I am not touching your website. Your Deals on Wheels feed already does the marketplace job and buyers already use it. Spend the money on sellers.

Recent pages I have built: southmaintenance.co.nz and leadhivenz.co.

The numbers

One extra listing sold a month covers it

I will not promise you a lead count on a niche nobody has advertised in yet. Here is how I think about it. Published Meta benchmarks for industrial and commercial leads sit between about NZ$40 and $150 per enquiry. Not every enquiry is a listing. Some will be a $6k ute. The form sorts most of those out before you call.

What we aim for

  • 15–25 seller enquiries a month once the campaign is out of its first two weeks
  • 3–5 appraisals booked
  • 1–2 listings won

What that is worth to you

You know your commission better than I do. On a $40,000 machine at a typical broker rate that is somewhere between $2,000 and $4,000 to Smith Traders. One extra unit sold a month pays for the whole campaign. Everything after that is margin.

We run it as a 90-day pilot. You see every lead. If it is not paying for itself by then, you stop.
