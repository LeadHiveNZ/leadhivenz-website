# Smith Traders (Tauranga) — prospect research

Compiled 23 Oct 2026 from the live site, the Companies Office register, the Deals on Wheels stock feed and public news. Items marked [inference] are a reading, not a sourced fact.

## 1. What Smith Traders actually does

Commission-style truck and machinery broker. The machine stays with the owner and keeps working until it sells. No yard stock, no buy-and-resell, no marketplace build.

- Home headline: "TRUCK & MACHINERY BROKERS — Let Smith Traders market your product while it continues on contract" (https://www.smithtraders.co.nz)
- Sales & Marketing page (https://www.smithtraders.co.nz/sales-marketing): pre-purchase inspections via independent mechanics; listed on smithtraders.co.nz, Deals on Wheels, Trade Me, MarketBook and Facebook; arranges viewings incl. airport shuttles; finance advice; market appraisals; "reduce the time and money spent marketing and negotiating for you".
- About page (https://www.smithtraders.co.nz/about): mission is to "connect our commercial Truck and Machinery clients nationwide"; "Working alongside our clients keeps both seller and purchaser maintaining their contracts during negotiations"; Mitch "on the ground to facilitate viewings and negotiations"; named clients RJ Lincoln, Pro Floors, JD Fieldservices, Regal Haulage, McLeod Cranes; sectors forestry, mining, haulage, agriculture, council, concrete, landscaping, civil, drainage; export relationships in Solomon Islands, Fiji, Vanuatu, Tonga.
- Revenue claim: "average of $1.9 million in sales over the last 3 years". [inference] That is gross value of goods sold, not fee income. At a 5–10% commission that is roughly $95k–$190k of fees a year.
- Fees: not stated anywhere on the site.
- No "sell your machine" page, no partner programme, no evidence of a marketplace being built.

## 7. Seller psychology

- IRD IR265: excavators, dump trucks, dozers, loaders, graders depreciate at 13% diminishing value (15.5-year life). https://www.ird.govt.nz/-/media/project/ir/home/documents/forms-and-guides/ir200---ir299/ir265/ir265-2020.pdf
- Market depreciation (vendor blogs, unaudited): 20–30% in year one, then 10–15% a year.
- Idle gear still costs finance, insurance, depreciation and yard space. RUC is distance-based so an idle truck does not burn RUC; rego can be put on hold free for 3–12 months, so rego is a weak hook. Finance, insurance, depreciation and space are the strong hooks.
- What auction houses sell against: time, tyre-kickers, low price. Turners "money in 48 hours", Lloyds "sell risk free".
- Mitch's best answer: the machine keeps earning on contract until it sells; no fire-sale at auction; he does the viewings and negotiation.
