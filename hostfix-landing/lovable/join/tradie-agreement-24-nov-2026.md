## Tradie agreement

Last updated 24 November 2026

This is the deal between you and HostFix. It is short on purpose. You agree to it once in the app, and we record when you did. If anything here is unclear, ask us before you accept your first job.

## 1. What this is

HostFix sends you fixed-price jobs at holiday homes by text. You choose which to take. You are an independent contractor running your own business: you use your own tools and vehicle, you work for whoever else you like, and you are responsible for your own tax, ACC levies and insurance. You are not our employee.

**The work is yours; we arrange it and collect the money for you.** When you do a job, you are the one supplying the work to the owner. HostFix finds the job, sets the fixed price and collects the owner’s payment as your agent, then pays you your share less the platform fee (section 6). You can always say no to a job.

## 2. What you need before your first job

- **Public liability insurance of at least NZ$1 million** in your own name (or your business’s), with the insurer, policy number and expiry date entered in your profile, and the certificate of currency from your insurer uploaded there (a PDF, JPG or PNG). Only our team and you can open the certificate. When you renew, upload the new one: we keep the earlier ones as a record. We do not approve anyone, or offer them jobs, without current insurance and its certificate on file, and the app stops offering you jobs the day it expires.
- **Current licences** for any plumbing, gas, electrical or drainlaying work, with the number and expiry in your profile. Licensed work is only ever offered to someone whose licence is on file and in date. We check each one against the public register.
- **Your criminal record**, because you will be entering homes that may have guests in them. You request your own copy from the Ministry of Justice (it is free, and can take up to 20 working days, so ask for it early) and send it to us before your first job. We treat it as confidential and keep only a note that we have seen it.
- **Bank details with Stripe**, set up from your profile, so we can pay you.
- **A Licensed Building Practitioner (LBP) number**, if you want building jobs. Building jobs are only offered to, and can only be accepted by, a tradie with an LBP number in their profile. Other trades do not need one.

You can sign in to your own account as soon as you have applied, to fill these in. Only our team can approve you, and nothing is offered to you until we do. We text you when you are approved.

**Our checks, and the record we keep.** A person on our team checks every tradie before approving them: each licence and LBP number against the public register, your insurance certificate, and your criminal record. We keep a record of each check (what was checked, when, and by whom), your licence and insurance details and certificates, and your job history (for the record check, only the note that we saw it), for as long as you work with HostFix and as long after as the law needs. You agree to these checks and records, and to us showing owners your name, business name and mobile number on jobs you accept. Our privacy policy has the detail.

Tell us within 7 days if any of these change or lapse.

## 3. How offers work

A job is offered by text to one tradie at a time. The first choice has **15 minutes** to accept, then it moves to the backup, then to everyone who does that trade. A job is only offered in the trade it is for: when an owner is not sure which trade a job needs, our team reads it and chooses the trade first. Open the link and press a button to accept; opening the link alone does nothing. On a normal job you accept by choosing the 2-hour window you will arrive in, inside the 24 hours; the owner is told that window. Pass whenever you cannot do it.

**Declining a job never leads to removal or to fewer offers.** Passing, or letting an offer run out, is not counted against you anywhere. You run your own business and choose your own work.

If you are the first choice or backup for your trade and cannot make the promised time, you can suggest a later time instead, up to 7 days ahead. The owner chooses whether to wait for you or have it sent to another tradie. If they wait, the job is yours at that time (and an urgent job is no longer urgent, so there is no urgent share). If they do not, we tell you, and that is not counted against you either.

Before you accept you see the job, the suburb, what it pays, the time you must be on site by, whether a guest is staying, and any hazards the owner or an earlier tradie has noted for the property. **After** you accept you see the address, the access notes and the owner’s number.

## 4. What accepting commits you to

- Being on site by the end of the window you chose for a normal job, or by the deadline for an urgent one (within 4 hours).
- Taking at least one **before** photo in the app and tapping **I have arrived** at the door, with your phone’s location on. That tap is how we measure the time, and it decides whether the urgent and after-hours shares are paid. The owner can tell us if you are not there.
- Doing the job described, to a tradesperson’s standard, and tidying up after yourself.
- Taking at least one **after** photo of the finished work in the app, writing what you did, and tapping **Job done** at the property, with your location on. The app will not let you arrive without a before photo or finish without an after photo.
- **The checks.** Your location at Arrived and at Job done is compared with the property’s map pin. A tap with no location, or more than 300 m from the pin, still works (phones get it wrong), but the job is flagged. So is a job finished less than 10 minutes after you arrived (a diagnosis, a visit to look and price, and “could not get in” are never flagged for time), and a photo whose own date is from before the job was logged. A flagged job is not an accusation: it only means the owner is asked to confirm it (section 6).
- Treating the house, the access code and the owner’s details as confidential. Never share a code, and never leave a door unsecured.
- If something you fixed fails within 30 days because of your work, coming back and putting it right at no charge.

## 5. The price is the price

Every menu job has a fixed price, and your share is shown in the offer before you accept. **By accepting, you accept that amount for that job.** You cannot ask the owner for more, charge them directly, or renegotiate on site.

**Extra work on a menu job** is quoted with **Found more to do? Send a quote**. That quote is only for work beyond the job described: you still do the job described, at its price. Up to the owner’s limit it goes ahead and they are told; above it, nothing starts until they say yes. Anything the owner has not approved is at your own cost.

**Not the job described.** If what you find is not the job described at all (the dripping tap is a burst pipe in the wall), tap **Not the job described** on a menu job, with at least one photo, a one-line reason and a quote. The menu price comes off and the call-out goes on. You are paid the call-out (NZ$190) once the owner approves your quote, or if they have not objected within 24 hours. If the owner says it was the job they described, our team decides from your photos: call-out paid, or no call-out. On a job that was already a look-and-price job, you simply quote.

**How quotes are priced.** Work up to NZ$1,000 is quoted in hours, in quarter hours, at HostFix’s fixed hourly rate for your trade (shown on the quote screen; it is what the owner pays, GST included). You cannot use a different rate. Above NZ$1,000 you price the work your own way. Only plumber, electrician, gasfitter and handyman work has a fixed hourly rate. **Every other trade (cleaning, gardening, building and the rest) has none: you quote extra work your own way, as one price for the work including GST, and the owner approves it before it starts** (their yes, or inside the limit they set for the property). The platform fee comes off the work, as on every job: 15% of the first NZ$1,000 of the job’s price and 3% of the part above it (5% below the line for your own clients, section 10; section 6 has the detail). Parts are added on their own, below. **If you need to come back, include the extra visit in your quote.** There is no second call-out on the same job.

**Work that takes a job over NZ$1,000** always waits for the owner’s yes, even inside their limit. When they approve it, 50% of the approved amount (less any of it the hold on their card already covers) is taken from them as a deposit, **and you do not start until the deposit has cleared.** The owner pays it from their bank account by direct debit (what we recommend: no card fees), which usually takes 3-4 working days to clear, or by card, which is charged straight away. We text you “approved, deposit clearing” and then “deposit cleared, go ahead”; paid by card, you get the approval as soon as it goes through. The other 50% is taken from the owner the same way when you tap Job done. If the deposit does not go through, or comes back, the quote is not approved: we text you not to start, and ask the owner to sort it. If the work then does not go ahead, the owner is refunded.

**The call-out credit.** On a look-and-price job, if the owner approves your quote, the NZ$225 call-out comes off the quote for them. It comes off your share in the same proportion: you keep the NZ$190 call-out pay, plus 85% of the quote less the call-out. A NZ$500 quote: the owner pays NZ$500 in all, and you get NZ$190 plus 85% of NZ$275 (NZ$233.75), NZ$423.75. If the owner says no, the call-out stands and you are paid it.

**Parts** can be added on any job, with **Add parts**. Small parts up to NZ$20 are in the job’s price (a part, times how many, that comes to that or less is not added). For anything else, enter what it is, how many, and your own price for one, including GST: your cost plus your own usual markup. The owner pays that price, and you get it less 3%, which covers the card fee. No receipt is needed. The price list in the app is only a quick way to fill in a name and a price; you can change both. A part you pick from the list and price at more than 1.5 times its list price is looked at by our team; nothing is held for that.

Up to NZ$150 of parts on a job (and within the property’s overall limit) are added straight away and the owner is told. Above that the owner is asked in the app: do not buy them until they say yes. **Keep your receipts.** Our team can ask for the receipt for any part, as a spot check: your payment for that job waits for it, and if it has not come within 7 days the part is refunded to the owner out of your payment and we text you. Only our team sees a receipt. A receipt dated more than 90 days before the job, or one already sent for a part on another job, holds your payment until our team has looked at it. Parts you added before 23 November 2026 stay on the terms you agreed then.

If a part you fitted is faulty, you deal with the supplier’s warranty; your labour is covered by the 30-day callback as usual. A part the owner supplied is not yours to put right if it fails. If a job is cancelled after the owner approved work, parts you had already bought for it and cannot return are paid at the receipt’s cost.

## 6. Pay

- **The platform fee is 15% of the first NZ$1,000 of a job’s price and 3% of the part above it.** The price is everything the owner pays for the job (the menu price or call-out, surcharges, quotes and hourly work) except parts, which have their own 3%. For your own clients it is 5% below the line and 3% above it (section 10). A NZ$15,000 job: 15% of NZ$1,000 plus 3% of NZ$14,000 is NZ$570, so you get NZ$14,430. Your job page, the offer and your Pay page show the fee.
- Your share is the amount in the offer. On an urgent or after-hours job, your share of the surcharge is in that amount, like the rest of the price: the surcharge less the 15% platform fee (5% for your own clients). Parts are paid as in section 5: your price less 3% (parts added before 23 November 2026: the list price less 3%, or what you paid plus 17%).
- **The job’s price is held on the owner’s card when the job is sent out**, and the charge is taken from that hold when you finish. Anything approved on top (parts, more work) is charged then too.
- **If the owner cancels before you tap I have arrived**, even after you tapped On my way, they pay nothing and you are not paid: the travel is yours.** If they cancel after you tap I have arrived**, they are charged the call-out and you are paid your share of it, as for a call-out (NZ$190, plus your share of any urgent or after-hours surcharge).
- **The urgent share is paid only if you tap I have arrived by the urgent deadline** shown in the offer. After the deadline the urgent surcharge is not charged to the owner at all, so there is no share of it to pay. The rest of the job is paid as normal.
- **The after-hours share is paid only if you arrive outside working hours** (before 7am, after 4:30pm, or at the weekend). If you come within working hours, the after-hours surcharge is not charged, and the rest of the job is paid as normal.
- Your payment is sent 24 hours after the job finishes and reaches your bank about 5 to 6 working days after the job. We will never promise you faster than Stripe actually pays.
- Your Pay page in the app lists every job and every payment, with the platform fee on each. Statements are there for your accountant.
- **Invoices and GST: we do the paperwork.** You agree that HostFix issues the owner’s invoice for each job on your behalf, in your name, and you do not issue your own invoice for it. If you are registered for GST and your GST number is in your profile, it is a tax invoice showing your GST number and the GST in the price; if you are not registered, it shows no GST. The prices are the same either way, so tell us within 7 days if you register or deregister. You account to Inland Revenue for any GST on your jobs; HostFix accounts for its own. The platform fee is our charge to you for finding the job, collecting the payment and the paperwork, and it is shown on every job and on your Pay page. HostFix is not registered for GST yet, so there is no GST in the fee; when that changes we will tell you and update this agreement.
- **Once you finish a job the owner approved, you’re paid even if their card or bank payment fails, for up to NZ$1,000 of the job’s price. Chasing is our job.** Your payment goes out at the usual time whatever the payment does; the owner owes the money to HostFix, not to you. On a bigger job, if more than NZ$1,000 of its price has not been paid by the owner when your payment is due (for example the second half of big work, debited from their bank at Job done and not yet cleared), your share of the part above NZ$1,000 waits, and is sent to you as soon as it is paid.
- If you could not get in through no fault of yours, tap **Could not get in** with a photo, and you are paid the call-out.
- **A flagged job’s payment waits for the owner.** They are asked to look at your before and after photos and confirm it is fixed. When they do, you are paid as normal. If they say something is wrong, section 7 applies. If they have not answered within 48 hours, you are paid if you are trusted (10 or more finished jobs, and no other flagged job in the last 90 days); otherwise our team looks at the photos and either pays you or holds the payment while they talk to you and the owner.

## 7. If the owner raises a problem, or their bank disputes the charge

An owner can raise a problem from their job page. If they do within a day of the job, your payment is held until it is sorted. Our team looks at the photos, the notes and both sides, then decides: the work stands and you are paid in full, or part or all of the job is refunded and your payment is reduced in proportion. If you have already been paid, we take your share of the refund back from that payment through Stripe; if that cannot be done, you agree to repay it. We will always tell you why.

**Chargebacks and direct-debit disputes.** If the owner’s bank disputes the charge for a job, your payment for it is held if it has not been sent yet, and we contest the dispute with the job’s photos, times, locations, receipts and messages. If we win, you are paid as normal. If we lose, our team decides whether it was your fault (bad work or a no-show). If it was, a payment not yet sent for that job is cancelled, and a payment already sent becomes a balance you owe us, taken back from your later payments, never more than 50% of any one payment; we text you when this happens, and your Pay page shows what is left. If it was not your fault, HostFix carries the loss and you are paid as normal. Any balance left when you leave HostFix must be repaid.

## 8. Your record, and who is offered jobs first

The app keeps one record that matters: **whether you arrive on time to the jobs you accept**. On time means by the end of the window you chose, or by the urgent deadline. Passing on offers is never part of it.
- **Your on-time record on jobs you accept decides who is offered network jobs first.** Once you have arrived at 10 jobs, if fewer than 90% of your last 20 were on time, you are offered jobs that go to everyone in your trade after the others have had their turn. You still get the offers, a little later. Get back to 90% and you are back in the first group.
- Our team chooses the first choice and backup for each trade, looking at the same on-time record over the last 30 days (80% or better). They can be swapped, paused or removed on that record.
- **A no-show** is a job you accepted where you are not on site by the promised time. We will ring you. Repeated no-shows on accepted jobs (3 in 90 days) can lead to removal from the network, but only after a conversation with you. Nobody is ever removed automatically.
- **Flagged jobs.** 3 flagged jobs in 90 days put you in front of our team for a conversation. Nobody is removed automatically for this either, and declining a job can never flag anything: only jobs you accepted and finished are checked.

If 2 or more jobs you accepted for the same owner in 90 days end without Job done (cancelled after On my way, or nobody there by the promised time), or 3 or more jobs you accepted in 90 days are cancelled after On my way, our team may look at them and talk to you. That is never a removal by itself, and it is never counted as declining.

You can pause offers yourself at any time with the Pause switch in your profile, for a holiday or a busy week, with no penalty.

## 9. Working alone in holiday homes, safety and damage

Many of these houses are empty when you arrive, and some have guests in them. The job screen tells you when a guest is staying (or the owner is not sure), and whether the owner said you may let yourself in if nobody answers. **Guest staying: knock and wait. Don’t let yourself in unless nobody answers after 5 minutes and the owner said that’s OK.** Entering an occupied property without knocking is a serious breach of this agreement. If there is a guest in the house, introduce yourself and say who sent you.

You manage the safety of your own work: follow your own health and safety practices, and do not enter or carry on if something feels wrong. Owners tell us the hazards they know of, and we show them to you before you accept. If you find a hazard at a property (gas, exposed wiring, water near power, a rotten deck, a dog), make it safe or stop, and report it from the job screen with** Found a hazard at this property?**: it is saved to the property for the next tradie and the owner is texted.

If an owner says you damaged something, the claim goes to your public liability insurer. HostFix supplies the job’s photos and times.

**Your staff.** You can send an employee or someone who works for you to a job you accepted. Your business is responsible for them as if you did the job yourself: their work, their conduct, the access codes they see, and anything they damage. They must hold any licence the work needs, be covered by your insurance, and follow this agreement (knocking when a guest is staying, the photos, the taps). Anyone else, such as another business, may not do a job you accepted.

**Claims caused by your work.** If HostFix has to pay a claim, a refund beyond your share or costs because of something you or your staff did or did not do on a job (damage, injury, bad work, or breaking this agreement), you agree to cover it. That is what your insurance is for. This does not apply to anything HostFix caused.

## 10. Your own clients, and app jobs stay in the app

**Your own clients.** You can bring owners you already work for to HostFix: send them an invite from the app, or they can add you from their property page with “Bring your own tradie” (you still sign up and are approved as normal). Once they accept for a property, jobs between you and that property take a **5% platform fee** instead of 15% on the first NZ$1,000 of the job’s price, on everything the 15% applies to: the menu price, the surcharges, the call-out, quotes and hourly work. Above NZ$1,000 it is 3%, as for every job. Parts are as in section 5. 5% covers the card fees (about 3%), the automatic texts and payment, and chasing late payers. You are also the first choice for that property in your trade. Every other tradie and trade at that property stays at 15%. This does not apply to a property where HostFix has already offered you a job, or you have done one: we check that by the property’s address and map pin, not by the owner’s account.

**Owners HostFix introduces** stay at the normal fee (15% to NZ$1,000, 3% above) for every job through the app, however long you have known them. There is no time limit and no buyout.

**One rule: a job booked in the app is finished and paid through the app.** Finishing an app job outside the app (taking the owner’s payment for it directly, or agreeing to do it off the app once it is booked) can lead to removal from the network. A person decides, after talking to you, as with everything else here.

## 11. Ending this

You can stop at any time: pause offers, or email us to close your account. A person on our team can remove you from the network, and will tell you why; the app never does it by itself, and declining jobs is never a reason. Jobs you have accepted are finished and paid under this agreement either way.

**A job reported as done that was not done, including a faked location or faked photos, means removal from HostFix, and any money paid to you for it must be repaid.**

## 12. If we disagree

Talk to us first: ring or email us and a person will look at it with you. If that does not sort it within 20 working days, either of us can suggest mediation with an independent mediator, and either of us, you or HostFix, can take it to the Disputes Tribunal (for claims inside its limit) or a court. Nothing here stops either of us doing that. While a disagreement is open, jobs you have already accepted carry on as normal.

## 13. Changes

If we change this agreement, the date at the top moves and you are asked to agree to the new version in the app (on your home page) before your next offer. We give you 30 days’ notice by text before a change to the platform fee applies to you. New Zealand law applies.
