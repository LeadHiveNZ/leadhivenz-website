## What this means for the empty campaigns

- Hawke's Bay plumber and Christchurch plumber are the strongest resale stories: 21/mo average each,
  with best months of 28 and 35. Both are inside the Starter promise (15–25) without any re-tuning.
- Christchurch electrician produced 18 calls in the first half of September alone before B Electric
  left; that slot should be sold as "25+/mo potential" with the caveat it only had two months.
- Tauranga plumber (18/mo) and Auckland plumber (14/mo in one month) are sellable at Starter but need
  the campaign re-warmed; expect month one to be at the low end.
- Auckland electrician (10/mo, $78/lead) is the weakest live slot. Worth a keyword/radius review.
- Missed-call rate of 16% is the biggest free lever: lifting answer rate to 95% ≈ +19% leads at $0.
