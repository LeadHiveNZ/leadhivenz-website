## Why tradies join

- **No tyre-kickers.** The owner's card is on file before you get the job. No quotes that go nowhere.
- **No paperwork.** No invoices, no statements, no chasing. We handle the money.
- **Work that fits around your day.** Short jobs, close together, at homes that are often empty between guests.
- **First pick of the jobs.** Each trade has a first-choice tradie who is offered every job first. What counts is turning up on time to the jobs you accept. Passing never counts against you.

## How it works

1. **We text you a job.** You see what it is, where it is and what you'll be paid.
2. **You have 15 minutes to take it.** Pick when you'll be there and tap to accept, or pass. If you pass, it goes to the next tradie, and that never counts against you.
3. **Do the job, with a photo before and after.** Take them in the app at the property, with your phone's location on. That's your proof of work.
4. **Get paid.** Your payment is sent within two days of the job and is in your bank about {{payout_days}} after the job. No invoice. If the app can't confirm a job (no location, too far from the house, or finished very quickly), the owner is asked to check your photos first.

## Questions

### What does it cost me?

Nothing to join, and no lead fees. We keep a fee out of each job, and the payout you see in the text is what you get.

### Do I have to take every job?

No. Passing on a job never leads to fewer offers or to being removed. What counts is your on-time record on the jobs you do accept: it decides who is offered network jobs first.

### How do I get paid?

By bank transfer, automatically. You set up your bank details once. Your payment is sent within two days of each job and takes a few working days to reach your bank. A job the app couldn't confirm waits for the owner to check the before and after photos, or for our team if the owner doesn't answer. Once you finish a job the owner approved, you're paid even if their card fails. Chasing is our job.

### What about parts?

Small parts up to {{parts_included}} are in the job's price. Common parts are on the price list at a fixed price: you get the list price less 3%, no receipt needed. Anything else is charged at the merchant's price plus 20% for sourcing it, and you get what you paid plus 17%: upload the receipt as a photo, a screenshot or the PDF. Above {{parts_limit}} of parts on a job, the owner says yes in the app before you buy.

### When do you start?

We're signing up one tradie per trade first. Put your name down and we'll give you a call.
