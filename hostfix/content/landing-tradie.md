# More paid jobs in {{market}}. No quoting. No invoicing. No chasing money.

We send you fixed-price maintenance jobs at holiday homes, straight to your phone. Tap to accept, do the job with a photo before and after, and you are paid automatically. No invoice to write, nobody to chase.

- Paid automatically, in your bank about {{payout_days}} after the job
- You see what you'll be paid before you accept
- Urgent adds {{urgent_share}} to your pay when you're on site by the deadline, and after hours adds {{after_hours_share}} when you come outside working hours: the surcharge less our {{platform_fee}}, like the rest of the price

## Why tradies join

- **No tyre-kickers.** The job's price is held on the owner's card before you get the job. No quotes that go nowhere.
- **No paperwork.** No invoices, no statements, no chasing. We handle the money.
- **Work that fits around your day.** Short jobs, close together, at homes that are often empty between guests.
- **First pick of the jobs.** Each trade has a first-choice tradie who is offered every job first. What counts is turning up on time to the jobs you accept. Passing never counts against you.

## How it works

1. **We text you a job.** You see what it is, where it is and what you'll be paid.
2. **You have 15 minutes to take it.** Pick when you'll be there and tap to accept, or pass. If you pass, it goes to the next tradie, and that never counts against you.
3. **Do the job, with a photo before and after.** Take them in the app at the property, with your phone's location on. That's your proof of work.
4. **Get paid.** Your payment is sent within two days of the job and is in your bank about {{payout_days}} after the job. No invoice. If the app can't confirm a job (no location, too far from the house, or finished very quickly), the owner is asked to check your photos first.

## Who we're looking for

- Cleaners, handymen, plumbers, electricians, gasfitters, drainlayers, appliance and heat pump techs, builders, painters, roofers, gardeners, window and carpet cleaners, pest control and spa pool techs working in and around {{market}}

## Questions

### What does it cost me?

Nothing to join, and no lead fees. We keep a fee out of each job: {{platform_fee}} of the first {{tier_threshold}} of the job's price and {{above_tier_fee}} of the rest, so on big jobs you keep nearly all of it. The payout you see in the text is what you get.

### Do I have to take every job?

No. Passing on a job never leads to fewer offers or to being removed. What counts is your on-time record on the jobs you do accept: it decides who is offered network jobs first.

### How do I get paid?

By bank transfer, automatically. You set up your bank details once. Your payment is sent within two days of each job and takes a few working days to reach your bank. A job the app couldn't confirm waits for the owner to check the before and after photos, or for our team if the owner doesn't answer. Once you finish a job the owner approved, you're paid even if their card fails, for up to {{payout_guarantee}} of the job's price. Chasing is our job. When approved work takes a job over {{payout_guarantee}}, the owner pays half as a deposit when they approve it, from their bank or by card, and you start once it has cleared (usually 3-4 working days from a bank, straight away by card). The other half is taken when you tap Job done.

### What about parts?

Small parts up to {{parts_included}} are in the job's price. For anything else you enter the part and your own price for it, including GST: your cost plus your usual markup. The owner pays that, and you get it less 3%, which covers the card fee. No receipt is needed, but keep it: we sometimes ask for one. Above {{parts_limit}} of parts on a job, the owner says yes in the app before you buy.

### What if the owner cancels?

Before you tap "I have arrived", even when you're on the way, the owner can cancel for free, and the travel is yours. Once you've tapped Arrived, cancelling charges the owner the call-out, and you're paid your share of it.

### Can I bring my own clients?

Yes. Send them an invite from the app. Jobs for a client you bring are {{own_client_fee}}, not {{platform_fee}}, on the first {{tier_threshold}} of the job's price ({{above_tier_fee}} above it, as on every job): that covers the card fees (about 3%), the automatic texts and payment, and chasing late payers. Owners HostFix introduces stay at {{platform_fee}} for jobs through the app.
