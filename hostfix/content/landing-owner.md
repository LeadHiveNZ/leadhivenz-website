## Sound familiar?

- **The 9pm text.** "The shower's leaking." You're hours away, and your usual guy isn't picking up.
- **The quote lottery.** Every small job starts with a call-out fee and a price you can't check.
- **The review.** A guest found it before anyone fixed it, and now it's on your listing.

## How it works

1. **Anyone can report it.** You, your cleaner from their own link (one link for every house you give them, signed with their name), or your guest from the QR card in the house. A photo and a minute.
2. **We find the tradie, not you.** Plumber, electrician, cleaner, gardener or builder: it goes straight to our first-choice tradie for that trade. If they can't take it, it goes to the next one. The tradie who takes it picks a two-hour window to arrive in, and you get a text with it.
3. **They get in without you.** Your lockbox code and access notes are shown only to the tradie who accepts the job.
4. **You get before and after photos and a fixed price.** When it goes out we hold the price on your card; you're only charged once it's fixed. One statement a month. If the app can't confirm the tradie's visit (where they were, or how long they stayed), we may ask you to check the photos before they're paid.

## You only pay for what you got

> Urgent, and the tradie gets there after the 4 hours? The urgent fee isn't charged. Emergency, and they take longer than the hour? No emergency fee. After-hours rate, but they came in working hours? Not charged either. Nobody has taken your job after 3 hours? We text you and ring round, and you pay nothing unless a tradie does the job.

## Who this is not for

If you pay a full-service manager, maintenance is already in their fee and you're covered.

If you only want emergency cover for your own home, a home emergency plan from your insurer is the cheaper buy.

This is for owners who look after their own holiday home, often from another city, and want one place to send every job.

## Questions

### I already have a guy.

Keep him. We're who you call when he doesn't pick up on a Saturday in July.

### What does it cost to join?

Nothing. You add your property and a card, and you only pay the fixed price when a job is done.

### Are jobs dearer through you?

No. The menu is priced from what {{market}} tradies charge for a call-out and the first hour, and the price already includes the call-out. Our share comes out of that price, not on top of it.

### Who are your tradies?

Local tradies and cleaners we've checked and approved. Plumbing, gas, electrical and drainlaying work only ever goes to a tradie whose licence is on file and in date. Building work only goes to a Licensed Building Practitioner (LBP).

### Can you do the cleaning too?

Yes. Turnover, entry and exit cleans at a fixed price by the size of the house, deep cleans, oven cleans and a clean-up after a party. Need it done before today's check-in? Book it as urgent for {{urgent}} more and a cleaner is on site within 4 hours. We also send window and carpet cleaners, gardeners, roofers, painters, builders, pest control, spa pool techs and drainlayers. Anything not on the price list yet is booked as a job not on the menu.

### Not sure who you need?

Choose "Not sure" when you tell us what's wrong. Someone on our team reads it and sends it to the right tradie.

### What about my door code?

It's shown only to the tradie who accepts a job at your place, and to our small team. We suggest a lockbox or smart lock you can re-code.

### What if the job turns out to be big?

Anything over {{approval_limit}} stops and comes back to you as a quote before any work starts. You say yes or no. If you say yes to work that takes the job over {{payout_guarantee}}, half is paid as a deposit then and the rest when it's done: from your bank account (no card fees, a few working days to clear) or by card if it's urgent.

### When do you start?

We open {{market}} once our tradies are signed up. Leave your details and we'll call you before then.

### I'm in Wānaka or Arrowtown.

Leave your details anyway. We'll let you know when we open there.
