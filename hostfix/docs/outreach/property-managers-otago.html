The 90-second call

Who
The owner or operations manager. In a business under 100 properties they answer their own phone.
Open
"Hi, it's Joe. I run a maintenance network for holiday homes called HostFix. I'm working with a Queenstown manager who has 63 properties, and I'm ringing the other managers in town before we open. Have you got two minutes?"
The problem
"When a shower leaks at 9 at night, who do you ring? And how many calls does it take?" Let them answer. They will tell you the pain.
What it is
"Your cleaner, your guest or you reports it from a phone. It goes to a vetted local tradie at a fixed price, they get the lockbox code, do it, send photos, and the card is charged automatically. You keep billing your owners however you do now, your margin on top. It costs you nothing: we take 15% inside the fixed price."
The two objections
"We have our own guys": great, sign them up, they get first choice on your properties and we chase nothing. "Our owners won't pay fixed prices": the prices are set at local call-out plus first hour, lower than most quotes; send me one of last month's invoices and I'll show you side by side.
The ask
"Can I put five of your properties on it for 60 days? If it doesn't save you time, you turn it off and you've paid nothing."
Close
Book the setup call before you hang up. Send the walkthrough video and the owner price list straight after.
