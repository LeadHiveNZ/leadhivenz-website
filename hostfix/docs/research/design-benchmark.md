## Recommendations

### A. Landing page structure

Owner page:
1. Hero with the promise, the monthly price and one postcode field: the best consumer pages ask one thing first.
2. Three proof numbers: six of the eight pages put ratings or counts at the hero.
3. The 24-hour-or-credited guarantee as its own block, as Honey Homes, TaskRabbit and Urban Company do with theirs.
4. Three numbered steps with phone mock-ups.
5. Fixed prices for common jobs as "from" cards.
6. One membership card with a property-count stepper, because the price is per property.
7. Tradespeople with photos and vetting chips.
8. With/without table.
9. Host testimonials with town and property count.
10. FAQ accordion, then the form again.

Tradesperson page:
1. Hero in the Lula pattern with one field.
2. Three stat tiles: hours to payout, typical job price, jobs nearby this month.
3. Live feed of recent jobs with prices, to prove the work exists.
4. Phone mock-up of the text message and accept screen.
5. Table against lead sites: no lead fees, no bidding, no subscription.
6. Four numbered steps and the requirements.
7. Testimonials labelled by trade.
8. FAQ and form again.

Headline formulas that recur: three short beats ("Real jobs. Fast pay. No bidding."), the result stated as already done ("Your Home, on Autopilot"), a "#1 place to" claim, and a direct question.

### B. Ten practices
1. One field in the hero, the rest in later steps.
2. Repeat the form at the foot.
3. Keep a CTA pill in the mobile header.
4. Add a reassurance line under the button ("No commitment").
5. Put three numbers at the hero.
6. Show a "from" price in the hero, anchored per property.
7. Badge one option "Most popular".
8. Lay vetting chips over a real tradesperson photo.
9. Answer price and cancellation in an FAQ accordion.
10. Keep the headline clear on load: TaskRabbit's discount modal, Tapi's region picker and Lula's cookie banner each covered it on mobile.

### C. Logo
The strongest marks use one flat colour, hold at 32 px and double as the app icon. The memorable ones are not tools: Thumbtack's pin, TaskRabbit's rabbit, Fergus's dog. Seven of the seventeen put a house or roof in the mark, so avoid houses, roofs, spanners, hammers, ticks and map pins.

Each concept sits in a 64 × 64 box, plum #3B1E3F with one lime #D4F23A detail.
1. **Key tag.** A plum rounded rectangle 28 × 52 (radius 8), tilted 12°. A hole of radius 5 near the top, and one lime bar 14 × 5 across the lower third, like a written job line.
2. **Door ajar.** A plum upright rectangle 24 × 52 (radius 3). From its right edge, a lime four-sided leaf 14 wide whose far edge is inset 6 at top and bottom. A plum dot of radius 2.5 for the handle.
3. **24-hour ring.** A plum circle of radius 24 with an 8 stroke and a 60° gap between 12 and 2 o'clock, with a lime dot of radius 5 in the gap.

### D. App structure
- Owner tabs: Home, Jobs, Properties, Account, plus a raised "Report a problem" button.
- Tradesperson tabs: Offers, My jobs, Pay, Profile.
- Job card: status chip first, property and suburb, job title, fixed price top right, time left on the 24-hour clock, tradesperson, photo count. Before accepting, the tradesperson sees suburb and distance only.
- Statuses: "Finding a tradesperson" amber #D98E04; "Booked", "On the way" and "On site" blue #2563EB; "Done" green #1F9D7A; "Paid" grey #6B7280; "Running late" and "No one accepted" red #D92D20.
- Dispatcher exceptions: one queue sorted by time left to the 24-hour mark. Each row gives the reason in plain words, the property, the clock and one main action (reassign, call, credit the month). Groups: no one accepted, accepted but not moving, on site too long, done without photos, payout past 48 hours. The target is an empty list.

### E. Colour and type
- Plum #3B1E3F: text, mark, main buttons.
- Paper #F7F2EA: page background.
- Lime #D4F23A: one highlight per screen, and buttons on dark sections.
- Lilac tint #EBE3F0: cards and selected states.

No product studied leads with plum: five lead with blue, six with green or teal, and hipages and Tradify with orange. Plum also sits outside the status colours, so the brand never reads as a status. Lime appears elsewhere only as a minor accent. Set headlines in a heavy grotesque and the rest in a neutral sans with tabular figures for prices and clocks; skip the serif that Honey Homes and Humming Homes share.
