Compiled 15 Nov 2026. "States" means the source says it; "inference" is my reading; "extract" means a search-result extract, because the site could not be opened directly (AirDNA, Bachcare, ACMA, WorkSafe, hipages).

## 2. Who looks after them now (Queenstown)

- AirROI states [5] (5 Nov 2026): 50 companies run five or more Queenstown listings. Largest: Hello Queenstown 143, Luxe Stay Queenstown 141, Relaxaway 75, Luxury Stays Queenstown 61; Bachcare 25.
- District listings by host size (my count) [2]: one listing 2,282; 2 to 9 listings 2,289; 10 or more 2,310.
- Commission: Tāhuna Hideaway, Staysouth and Relaxaway publish no rate [6][7][8]. AirROI states that of 180 companies checked (Sept 2026) "17 publish a rate, from 10% to 30% of booking revenue with a median starting rate of 17%, and 102 quote only on request", with "maintenance call-outs" among extras billed on top [5]. Elsewhere in NZ, Hanmer Holiday Homes charges "20% + GST" [9]; Bachcare Host starts at 15% (extract) [10].
- Maintenance, in their words: Relaxaway "We'll arrange professional, qualified and licenced local tradespeople to maintain your property" and "we're on call 24-7 during their stay to handle any emergencies" [8]. Tāhuna Hideaway "Our team is available 24/7 to address any emergencies" [6].
- Trades: Builderscrack shows 48 plumbers and over 230 handymen serving Queenstown Lakes (15 Nov 2026), none listing rates or targeting holiday homes [11]. AA Home's 24/7 call-out service lists 22 regions; Queenstown is not one [12]. I found no maintenance business aimed at holiday-home owners (not conclusive).

## 4. Fixed prices for small jobs

- NZ (MoneyHub guides) [13]: blocked toilet $150-$250; tap or leaking pipe $150-$250; toilet repair $150-$300; power socket $150-$250 including GST.
- NZ firms: toilet or drain unblocking from $260 + GST [14]; heat pump service $150-$159 + GST, $65-$78 per extra unit (three firms outside Otago; extracts) [16].
- Australia (A$; Yellow Pages, Apr-May 2026) [17]: plumber $80-$200 an hour; blocked drain $150-$600; tap replacement $80-$350; electrician $80-$140 an hour; power point $140-$180; hard-wired smoke alarm about $150.
- Not found: cistern mechanism, shower re-siliconing, gas hot water service, NZ smoke alarm or light fitting.

## 5. Licensing

New Zealand
- Plumbing, gas, drains (PGDB) [18]: "For any plumbing, gasfitting or drainlaying work, make sure the person undertaking the work is licensed to do so." Allowed without a licence: "replace or repair taps, ball cocks and plugs", appliance hook-ups, and "Clear blocked drains, but only if this does not involve altering or repairing the inspection pipe". Barred: "any work involved in fixing or unfixing any pipe, plumbing fixture or appliance" and "any work involved in installing, altering or repairing gas appliances". That page addresses DIY; a 2021 government notice (extract, not checked against the Act) puts tap, ball valve, washer and plug work outside "sanitary plumbing" [19].
- Electrical: "Only registered and licensed electrical workers can carry out or supervise prescribed electrical work (PEW)" (EWRB) [20]. WorkSafe (extract) [21]: for DIY you must "own and live in the property", and no one may do electrical work in "any rented property unless they are registered electrical workers and hold current practising licences".
- Building (LBP) [22]: "RBW is work that is essential to make a home structurally sound and weathertight"; only licensed building practitioners may do or supervise it.

Australia
- Queensland (QBCC, last reviewed Aug 2021) [23]: licence needed for building work "valued over $3,300", and at any value for plumbing, drainage or gas fitting; a handyman is exempt "as long as the value is no more than $3,300, isn't electrical or plumbing work and doesn't require an occupational licence".
- NSW [24]: "A licence is required for: residential building work valued at more than $5,000 (incl GST) in labour and materials" and "all specialist work regardless of its cost", which "includes electrical wiring, plumbing, drainage and gasfitting work".
- Victoria (Dec 2025) [25]: registration is needed "to carry out work worth more than $10,000, except for work involving only one" listed trade; plumbers "are registered or licensed with the BPC"; Energy Safe Victoria: "Electrical work isn't a DIY job, even for small jobs such as changing power points or light switches."

## 6. Text messages and privacy

- NZ Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007 (Internal Affairs) [26]: commercial texts need express, inferred or deemed consent; "Commercial messages must always clearly identify the business responsible for sending the message" and "must contain a functioning unsubscribe facility"; "You must honour a request to unsubscribe within five working days."
- NZ Privacy Act 2020 (Privacy Commissioner) [27]: IPP 3 requires reasonable steps, "before the information is collected", to tell people what is collected, why, who receives it, and their access and correction rights. Since 1 May 2026, IPP 3A applies the same duty when the information comes from someone else, such as a manager passing on a tradesperson's number.
- Australia Spam Act 2003 (ACMA, extract) [28]: provable consent, clear sender identification, and a free unsubscribe actioned within 5 working days.
- SMS Sender ID Register (ACMA, extract) [28]: since 1 July 2026 an unregistered branded sender ID shows as "Unverified"; register through each SMS provider.

## 7. Short-term rental regulation

- Queenstown Lakes [29][1]: operators must register; "STVA activity operating for up to 90 nights per year does not require resource consent"; 3,196 properties are registered to operate beyond 90 nights. Letting under 180 days is rated Mixed Use (rates up 25-35%); 181-365 days Accommodation (up 50-80%).
- NZ 2025-26: no national register. Councils paused the Otago Central Lakes Regional Deal on 27 Aug 2026 over "the commitment to explore an accommodation levy policy in 2027" [30]; a National Party policy (8 Sep 2026) would instead share the international visitor levy [31].
- NSW (Feb 2025) [32]: mandatory register ($65, $25 renewal); 180-day cap on non-hosted lets in Greater Sydney and some regions; 60 days in most of Byron Shire since 23 Sep 2024.
- Victoria: short stay levy "From 1 January 2025" [33] (7.5%, secondary sources). Mornington Peninsula requires annual registration and "a designated contact person to respond to neighbour complaints within two hours, day or night" [34].
- Queensland: no statewide register found. Noosa (2026/27) [35]: annual renewal $800 per house; a contact person "available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week", within 20 km, "responding to each complaint within 30 minutes". Gold Coast: nothing reliable found.

## So what for a founder

1. Size: about 5,400 short-stay properties in Queenstown Lakes, roughly 4,000 on the Queenstown side. Other NZ areas are under 2,200 listings; Sunshine Coast with Noosa (7,300), Gold Coast (4,200-7,100) and Mornington Peninsula (4,400) are larger.
2. Channel: listings split into near-equal thirds (solo hosts, 2 to 9, 10 or more). Big managers already promise 24/7 cover, arrange trades and bill maintenance on top of about 17-20% commission, so they are both channel and incumbent (inference).
3. Pricing: labour $90-$120 an hour plus GST; all-in first hour about $160-$260 scheduled, $240-$600 after hours; small jobs $150-$300. A fixed menu in that band with stated after-hours multipliers matches published prices (inference).
4. Menu: tap, ball cock and plug repairs, appliance hook-ups and drain clearing appear licence-free; pipework, fixtures, all gas and any electrical work in a rented property need licensed trades.
5. Australia: Queensland and NSW licence rules apply to whoever contracts for or advertises the work, so marketplace-versus-contractor structure needs legal advice (inference).
6. Response-time rules create demand: 30 minutes in Noosa, two hours on the Mornington Peninsula. Byron's cap and Victoria's levy reduce it.
7. Queenstown gap: AA Home does not serve it and I found no local trade publishing prices (inference; search incomplete).
