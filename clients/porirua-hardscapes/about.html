Our story

A tradesman's company, not a sales office.

Adam Martin has spent more than three decades hands-on in concrete, excavation and landscape construction around Wellington. Driveways, retaining walls, site works, section clear-outs: if it involves a digger, boxing or a concrete truck, he's done it, most of it on the hills between Porirua and the south coast.

Porirua Hardscapes Ltd is the Whitby-based company he runs today. The way it works is simple. Adam comes to your place, measures up and gives you a written quote. Then the same crew turns up to do the work: digging out, boxing, steel, drainage, the pour and the finish. Nobody hands your job to a subbie you've never met.

We own our diggers and trucks, so we don't wait on hire companies and you don't pay a middleman's margin. It also means we can take on the awkward jobs, like steep driveways, tight access and walls that need a bank cut back first.

About the business

What people ask before they book us.

Are you insured?

Yes — ask for our certificate of currency.

Do you do commercial work?

Yes. Alongside homes we take on commercial and light civil jobs: yards, car parks, slabs, site prep and earthworks. Call Adam to talk through scope and timing.

How many crews do you run?

One. It's a deliberate choice. Adam quotes every job and the same crew does the work, so the person who promised you a finish is the person delivering it.

Do you subcontract the work?

No. Digging, cartage, boxing, steel, pouring and finishing are all done by our own crew with our own gear. If a job needs a specialist, like an engineer for a consented wall or a licensed asbestos remover for an older building, we arrange it and show it on your quote.
