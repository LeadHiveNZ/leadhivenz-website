Good to know

Landscaping questions, answered.

Can you do the whole section, not just one part?

Yes. That's the point of one crew. We can clear it, dig it, build the walls, paths and patio, then lay the lawn and plant the beds, on one quote and one programme. You can also do it in stages if that suits the budget.

Do you clear overgrown sections?

Yes. We use our own digger and trucks to clear scrub, stumps, old sheds, rubble and rubbish, then regrade the ground so it's ready to build on. Clean soil and green waste are carted separately so tip fees stay down.

Do I need a consent for landscaping work?

Most landscaping doesn't. The usual exceptions are retaining walls holding more than 1.5 m of ground or carrying a surcharge, decks where a fall of more than 1.5 m is possible, fences over 2.5 m, and pool fences. We flag anything that needs council sign-off before you commit.

How is a landscaping job priced?

Every section is different, so we price it on site after walking it with you. The quote is itemised by stage (clearing, earthworks, walls, paving, lawns and planting), so you can see where the money goes and choose what to do now and what to leave for later.
