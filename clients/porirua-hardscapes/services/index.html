Good to know

Questions about how we work.

Can you do several services on the one job?

Yes, and that's where we save people the most hassle. A typical job might be demolition of an old slab, excavation, a retaining wall and a new driveway. We quote it as one job with each stage as a line, and the same crew does the lot.

Do you charge for quotes?

No. On-site quotes are free and there's no obligation. We quote 7 days a week, and you usually get an itemised written quote within 48 hours of the site visit.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Whitby and most of our jobs are within 30 minutes of Porirua, but we work across Wellington City, Lower Hutt, Upper Hutt and the Kāpiti Coast too.

Do you handle consents?

We tell you up front whether your retaining wall, deck, fence, vehicle crossing or demolition needs council sign-off, before you spend a dollar. For work that needs an engineer, we build to the engineer's design.

How quickly can you start?

Quotes within 48 hours of the visit. Start dates depend on the season and the size of the job; smaller jobs often slot in within a couple of weeks. Ask Adam for the current lead time when you call.
