Good to know

Straight answers before you book.

Consents, concrete, walls, diggers and demolition. If your question isn't here, call Adam.

Quotes & timing

Are your quotes really free?

Yes. Adam comes to your property, measures up and talks through options, and you get a written, itemised quote, usually within 48 hours. No obligation and no call-out fee.

How quickly can you start?

Start dates depend on the season and the size of the job. Smaller jobs often slot in within a couple of weeks. Ask Adam for the current lead time when you call.

How long does a driveway take?

A standard driveway is usually 1 to 3 days on site, depending on size, access and whether an old slab has to come out first, plus curing time before you drive on it.

Consents

Do I need a building consent for a retaining wall?

Not if the wall retains 1.5 m of ground or less and has no surcharge: no driveway, building, pool, another wall or sloping ground above it. It must still meet the Building Code and be drained. Taller walls, or any wall carrying a load, need building consent and normally an engineer.

Wellington City Council notes a resource consent may also be needed if a wall is near a boundary or over 2.5 m.

Do I need consent for a deck or fence?

A deck is exempt if it's not possible to fall more than 1.5 m, even if the deck collapses. The test is the fall height, not the deck height, so many hillside decks need consent. You need a barrier where the fall is 1 m or more.

Fences up to 2.5 m are exempt, but pool fences are not. The district plan may limit boundary fences to around 2 m, and the Fencing Act covers sharing the cost with your neighbour.

Does a new driveway need council approval?

A driveway on your own land usually doesn't need building consent. A new or altered vehicle crossing onto the road does. In Porirua that's a Corridor Access Request through Porirua City Council, with the crossing up to 3 m wide for a single or 4.5 m for a double garage, at least 1 m from street furniture, and heavy-duty if more than two properties share it.

Concrete

How thick should a concrete driveway be?

At least 100 mm for cars, with 20 MPa or stronger concrete and steel mesh in the top third. Go to 125–150 mm with heavier reinforcement if a boat, campervan or truck will use it.

When can I drive on my new driveway?

Walk on it after 24 to 48 hours. Keep cars off for at least 7 days and heavy vehicles off longer. Concrete reaches its design strength at around 28 days. Keep it moist-cured for at least the first 4 days.

Why does concrete crack, and can you stop it?

Concrete shrinks as it cures, so some cracking is normal. We saw-cut control joints roughly every 3 to 4.5 m so it cracks in a straight line inside the joint, where you want it, instead of across the middle.

Retaining & decks

Why does drainage matter behind a retaining wall?

Water pressure building up behind a wall is the main reason walls fail. Every wall we build gets a drainage coil at the base, free-draining backfill such as scoria, and filter cloth, with the drain taken to an approved outlet.

Timber, block or crib wall?

Timber (H5 posts, H4 rails) is the most affordable. Block or concrete costs more but lasts longer and looks tidier. Crib walls suit tall or engineered situations. We'll recommend one at the site visit.

Excavation & demolition

What does "wet hire" mean?

The machine comes with an experienced operator. Expect a minimum charge, plus travel or cartage, plus tip fees for anything that leaves site.

What happens with the soil and rubble you dig out?

We cart it away in our own trucks. Clean earth goes to cleanfill. Concrete, rubble and demolition waste go to the right facility, and tip fees are passed through at cost.

Do you check for underground services before digging?

Yes. We lodge a free beforeUdig request to locate power, gas, water and telecommunications lines before any digger goes in.

Do I need consent to demolish a house or garage?

Fully demolishing a detached building of up to three storeys is exempt from building consent. Partial demolition may need consent. Buildings from before 2000 need an asbestos survey before work starts, and any asbestos must be removed by a licensed remover.
