Residential Demolition Services in Wellington, NZ

When it comes to preparing your site for a fresh start, safe and efficient demolition is essential. At Porirua Hardscapes, we provide professional residential demolition services across Wellington, handling everything from small-scale removals to full-site clear-outs. With decades of experience, modern equipment, and a commitment to safety, we deliver results you can rely on. Our team works throughout Porirua, Wellington City, Lower Hutt, Upper Hutt, and nearby suburbs—helping homeowners clear space for rebuilds, renovations, or new construction projects. Our Residential Demolition Services

We understand that every property and every project is unique. That’s why we offer a wide range of demolition solutions tailored to your specific needs.

Full House Demolition

Safe, controlled demolition of entire homes, including utility disconnection and complete debris removal.

Partial Demolition

Removal of extensions, walls, or interior areas while preserving the rest of the structure for renovations or upgrades.

Garage, Shed & Outbuilding Removal

Quick and efficient demolition of smaller structures such as garages, carports, decks, or sheds.

Site Clearance & Preparation

Once structures are removed, we can clear and level the site to prepare it for construction, landscaping, or future use.

Waste Removal & Recycling

We responsibly manage demolition waste, recycling timber, metal, and concrete wherever possible to reduce environmental impact.

FAQ – Residential Demolition Wellington

  • How long does a residential demolition take?

    The timeframe depends on the size and type of structure. A small shed may be cleared in a day, while a full house demolition can take several days to a week, including debris removal.

  • Do I need a permit for residential demolition?

    In most cases, yes. Porirua Hardscapes can assist with the permit process and ensure all council requirements are met before work begins.

  • What happens to the waste materials?

    We sort and recycle as much material as possible, including timber, concrete, and metals. Non-recyclable waste is disposed of responsibly at approved facilities.

  • Can you remove asbestos during demolition?

    If asbestos is present, we work with licensed asbestos removal specialists to ensure it is safely managed and disposed of in compliance with regulations.

  • How much does residential demolition cost?

    Costs vary depending on the size of the structure, site conditions, and waste disposal needs. We provide detailed, competitive quotes upfront, so you know exactly what to expect.
