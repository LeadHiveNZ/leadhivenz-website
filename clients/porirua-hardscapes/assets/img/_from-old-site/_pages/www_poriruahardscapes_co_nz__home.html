Your Local Concrete & Excavation Specialists

At Porirua Hardscapes, we specialise in concrete, excavation, and landscape construction across Porirua, Wellington City, Lower Hutt, Upper Hutt, and surrounding suburbs. With over 36 years of experience, our locally owned and operated team delivers high-quality craftsmanship and long-lasting results for residential and commercial projects. From driveways and patios to site excavation and landscaping, we provide end-to-end services, ensuring every project is completed safely, efficiently, and to the highest standard. Whether it’s a new build, renovation, or outdoor upgrade, Porirua Hardscapes is your trusted partner for hardscape construction that stands the test of time.

Our Services

Concrete Structures & Driveways

Durable, clean-finished concrete work for driveways, patios, footpaths, slabs, and foundations, built to withstand Wellington’s conditions.

Excavator & Truck Services

Reliable excavator and truck hire with experienced operators for site preparation, material removal, trenching, and bulk earthworks.

Landscape Construction

Design and build practical, attractive outdoor spaces—from garden edging and paving to full section clear-outs and soft landscaping.

Decking & Fencing

Custom timber decks and fences to suit your section, style, and budget, including design, construction, and compliance.

Retaining Walls

Strong, well-drained retaining walls using timber, concrete, or block—perfect for levelling, soil retention, and maximising usable space.

Residential Demolition

Safe and efficient residential demolition services, from small sheds and garages to full house clear-outs, with responsible waste removal.
