Exterior painting

The prep is what makes it last.

An exterior repaint is only as good as the prep underneath it. Weatherboards are washed down, loose and flaking paint is scraped back, bare timber is sanded and primed, and gaps are filled before a topcoat goes anywhere near them. Plaster, brick, fascias, windows and doors get the same treatment. Sun, wind and coastal weather are hard on paint around here, so we recommend the right product for each surface and how exposed it is. You get a clear written quote and a timeframe up front, and we keep you posted if rain pushes things out. Darren or Rich is on site for the whole job.

How we do it

Four steps, done properly.

You know what's happening, when, and what it costs before a brush comes out.

  1. Look and quote

    We walk around the house with you, check the boards and the existing paint, and give you a written quote and timeframe.

  2. Wash and prep

    The house is washed down, then loose paint scraped back, edges sanded, gaps filled and bare timber primed.

  3. Topcoats

    Paint goes on once the surface is sound and dry. We work around the weather and keep you posted if rain moves things.

  4. Final walkthrough

    We walk the job with you, touch up anything you spot and leave the section tidy.
