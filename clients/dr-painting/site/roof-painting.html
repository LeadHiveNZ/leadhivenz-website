Roof painting

More years from the roof you have.

Roof painting is one of the best-value jobs on a house. An iron or tile roof that has gone chalky, faded or started to rust can get years more life from a proper recoat. We wash the roof, treat moss and lichen, deal with rust spots and prime bare or treated areas, then put on two full topcoats. We look over the fixings and flashings as we go and tell you about anything that needs attention. Roof work depends on dry weather more than anything else, so we book it around the forecast and keep you in the loop from quote to finish.

How we do it

Three steps, done properly.

You know what's happening, when, and what it costs before a brush comes out.

  1. Inspect and quote

    We look over the roof, check rust, fixings and flashings, and give you a straight answer and a written quote.

  2. Wash and treat

    The roof is washed, moss and lichen treated, and rust spots wire-brushed and treated.

  3. Prime and topcoat

    Primer on bare and treated areas, then two full topcoats in dry, settled weather.
