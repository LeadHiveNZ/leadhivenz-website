Where we work

Three regions, one local team.

Same people, same prep and the same free written quote, wherever you are.

Whanganui

Home base

Whanganui is home base. Most of our work is in and around the city and the towns close by, including Marton, Bulls and Hunterville. Being local means quick site visits for quotes, and when the weather turns we’re never far from the job.

Whanganui · Marton · Bulls · Hunterville

Manawatū

We travel

We travel anywhere in Manawatū, from Palmerston North, Feilding and Ashhurst to Sanson, Foxton and Levin. Same team, same prep and the same free written quote. Exterior repaints, roofs and full interiors are all worth the trip, and smaller jobs are still worth a call.

Palmerston North · Feilding · Ashhurst · Sanson · Foxton · Levin

South Taranaki

We travel

Up the coast we cover South Taranaki, including Waverley, Pātea, Hāwera, Eltham, Manaia, Ōpunake and Stratford. Coastal wind and salt are hard on weatherboards and roofs out this way, so the prep and the right products matter even more.

Waverley · Pātea · Hāwera · Eltham · Manaia · Ōpunake · Stratford
