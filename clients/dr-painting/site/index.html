How it works

Four steps. No surprises.

You know what's happening, when, and what it costs before a brush comes out.

  1. Free quote on site

    We come to you, look over the job and talk it through. You get a clear written quote, free.

    Free, no obligation

  2. Colour & plan

    A colour consult if you want one, the right products for each surface, and a start date that suits you.

  4. Final walkthrough
