Handyman & property maintenance

The small jobs, sorted with the paint.

Most houses have a list of small jobs that never quite get done. A rotten board, gutters full of leaves, a path that needs water blasting, a few things to fix before the painters arrive. We take care of those as part of a paint job or on their own. It makes sense to sort the repairs before the paint goes on, and having one team do both means one call, one quote and one tidy job. It works well for landlords and property managers between tenants, too. If it’s on your list and it’s around the house, it’s worth asking.
