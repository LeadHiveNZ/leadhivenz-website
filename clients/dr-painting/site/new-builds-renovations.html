New builds & renovations

Painting that fits the build programme.

On a new build or renovation, the painter has to fit in with everyone else on site. We work to your builder’s schedule, stay in touch so we’re ready when the plasterboard is stopped, and get the sealer, undercoats and topcoats on in the right order. Interiors, exteriors, joinery and trim are all part of it. Once the other trades are finished we come back for the final touch-ups, so the house is handed over clean. Whether you’re the builder or the homeowner running the project, you get one clear written quote and one team to deal with from start to finish.

How we do it

Four steps, done properly.

You know what's happening, when, and what it costs before a brush comes out.

  1. Quote from plans or on site

    Send us the plans or have us walk the site. You get a written quote that lines up with the build programme.

  2. Fit in with the build

    We stay in touch with the builder so we’re ready when stopping is done, and work around the other trades.

  3. Seal, undercoat, topcoat

    Each surface gets the right paint system, applied in order and given time to dry between coats.

  4. Touch-ups and handover

    When the other trades are finished, we come back for the final touch-ups so the house is ready to hand over.
