Will plantation shutters keep my house cooler in a Brisbane summer?

Yes. Closed shutters block direct sun and hold an insulating layer of air between the glass and the room, so west-facing rooms heat up far less in the afternoon. Tilt the blades up and you can bounce soft light off the ceiling while keeping the glare and heat out.

PVC or basswood: which is better for Queensland?

For bathrooms, laundries, kitchens and anywhere near moisture, PVC every time. It won't swell, warp or peel in Brisbane humidity, and it wipes clean. Basswood is a real timber that is lighter, suits wide living-area windows and takes paint or stain beautifully, but it should be kept away from wet areas. Many homes end up with both. For patios and alfresco areas, aluminium is the answer.

What warranty do I get?

Two layers of cover. Your products carry the manufacturer's warranty, and SR backs the installation with its own workmanship guarantee. Shane sets out the exact terms for every product in writing with your quote, so you know precisely what's covered before you decide.

How long does it take from quote to install?

Everything is custom made to Shane's measurements, so timing depends on the product and the size of the job. Shane will give you a clear timeline with your quote, and most homes are fully installed in a single day.
