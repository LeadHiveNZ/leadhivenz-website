Privacy · SR Shutters & ScreensPrivacy policy.

Short and plain: what the quote form collects, what we use it for, and how to reach us about it.

Who we are

SR Shutters & Screens is a family-owned business run by Shane Bartolo, installing shutters, screens and blinds across South East Queensland. In this policy, "we" and "us" means SR Shutters & Screens.

What the quote form collects

When you request a free measure and quote, the form asks for:

  • your name and mobile number
  • your suburb
  • the products you're interested in
  • anything else you choose to tell us in the message box

If you call or email instead, we keep your number or email address and what you tell us about the job.

What we use it for

We use your details only to contact you, arrange your measure and quote, and look after the job if you go ahead. We don't sell, rent or trade your details, and we don't add you to a mailing list.

Who sees it

Your enquiry goes to Shane. The website's form service delivers it to his inbox, and it isn't shared with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Seeing, fixing or deleting your details

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it at any time. Email srshuttersnscreens@gmail.com or call Shane on 0411 885 227.

Questions

If you have a question or a concern about your privacy, email srshuttersnscreens@gmail.com and Shane will get back to you.
