How long does it take from quote to install?

Everything is custom made to Shane's measurements, so timing depends on the product and the size of the job. Shane will give you a clear timeline with your quote, and most homes are fully installed in a single day.

What warranty do I get?

Two layers of cover. Your products carry the manufacturer's warranty, and SR backs the installation with its own workmanship guarantee. Shane sets out the exact terms for every product in writing with your quote, so you know precisely what's covered before you decide.

Are the measure and quote really free? Will I be pressured to sign?

Completely free and obligation-free. There are no sales reps on commission at SR, just Shane. You'll get a clear written quote and can take as long as you like to decide. Plenty of people book a measure just to compare options, and that's fine by us.
