What is a hydro clean?

A deep clean for the parts you can’t reach

Hydro cleaning (also called a hydro pressure clean) is a deep clean of your indoor split system unit.

Luke removes the covers and filters, fits a waterproof cleaning bag under the unit, then washes the coil, fan and drain tray with a pressure washer and a cleaning solution made for air conditioners. The dirty water runs into the bag and away, not down your wall or onto the floor.

It is the only way to properly clean the barrel fan and coil fins, which is where the mould and grime that cause musty smells actually live.

Why it matters

Cleaner air, lower running costs

Air conditioners pull in dust, pet hair, salt and moisture every day. Over time, that gunk becomes a breeding ground inside the unit.

  • Mould

    The coil and fan are dark and damp, exactly where mould grows. Every time the system runs, it can blow spores into the room.

  • Bacteria & allergens

    Dust, pet hair and bacteria collect on the coil and fan. Clearing them out helps if anyone at home has asthma or allergies.

  • Efficiency

    A clogged coil can’t move heat properly, so the system works harder and longer to reach the same temperature.

  • Power bills

    A system that works harder uses more electricity. A clean unit runs more efficiently, which helps keep running costs down.

Who it’s for

Worth doing for most homes, essential for some

  • Rental properties

    A clean between tenancies or before an inspection keeps units healthy and tenants happy.

  • Allergy & asthma households

    Less dust, mould and bacteria blowing around the rooms you sleep and live in.

  • Beachside homes

    Salt air, sand and humidity mean faster build-up in coastal suburbs like Mudjimba, Marcoola, Twin Waters and Peregian.

  • Homes with pets

    Pet hair and dander clog filters and coils faster than you would expect.

How often?

Once a year is a good rule of thumb for most homes. Clean more often if you are beachside, have pets, run the system most days, or anyone at home has allergies or asthma.

Do you need one? Check in 30 seconds

Turn the unit off. Gently open the front louvres (the flaps) and shine a torch inside at the barrel-shaped fan. If you see black spots or a furry build-up, that is mould, and it’s time for a hydro clean.

FAQ

Hydro cleaning questions

How long does a hydro clean take?

About an hour for a standard wall-mounted split system. If you are booking several units, Luke will give you a time estimate for the whole job when you book.

Will it make a mess?

No. A waterproof cleaning bag catches all the dirty water under the unit and the floor is protected. You are left with a clean unit and a clean room.

Is the cleaning solution safe?

Yes. Luke uses non-toxic mould and bacteria treatments designed for air conditioners, and everything is rinsed through before the unit goes back together.

Can’t I just clean the filters myself?

You should rinse the filters every few weeks, and it helps. But the coil, fan and drain tray sit behind the covers and need the unit partly taken apart and washed with the right gear. That is the part a hydro clean does.

Why not just book a cheap clean?

Some budget cleans don’t remove the covers or use the wrong chemicals, which leaves mould behind inside the unit. A proper hydro clean takes the unit apart, treats the mould and washes it out.

Do you clean all brands?

Yes. Luke cleans all the major brands, including Daikin, Panasonic, Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Mitsubishi Electric, Hisense, Hitachi, TCL, Fujitsu and LG.
