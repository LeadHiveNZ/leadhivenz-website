The way I work

When you call Onshore Air, you talk to me. I quote the job, I do the work, and I’m the one you call if you ever need anything afterwards. No call centre, no sales pitch, no being passed around.

I’ve spent more than 15 years working on everything from single bedroom splits to larger multi-room systems, so I know what lasts in Sunshine Coast conditions: the humidity, the salt air and the long, hot summers. I’ll tell you honestly what your home needs, even when that means a smaller job than you expected.

Turn up when I say I will, explain things clearly, do neat work and charge a fair price. That’s the whole idea, and my reviews say it works.
