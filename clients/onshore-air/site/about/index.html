The Onshore way

Air conditioning the way you’d want it done in your own home

Turn up when you say you will. Explain things clearly. Do neat work. Charge a fair price. That is the whole philosophy, and the reviews say it works.

When you call Onshore Air, you talk to Luke. He quotes the job, does the work and is the person you call if you ever need anything afterwards. No call centre, no sales pitch, no being passed around.

After 15+ years working on everything from single bedroom splits to larger multi-room systems, he knows what lasts in Sunshine Coast conditions: the humidity, the salt air and the long, hot summers. He will tell you honestly what your home needs, even when that means a smaller job than you expected.
