Split, multi-head or ducted

One cool bedroom or the whole house: I’ll recommend what suits your home and budget.

  • Split systems

    The go-to for bedrooms, living areas and granny flats. Quiet inverter units sized to the room, so they aren’t running flat out every summer afternoon.

  • Multi-head systems

    Two or more indoor units running off one outdoor unit. Handy when you want several rooms cooled but only have space for one condenser.

  • Ducted systems

    Ceiling vents through the house, zoned so you only cool the rooms you’re using. Suits larger and two-storey homes.

  • New builds and replacements

    I can plan unit locations and pipe runs with you or your builder before the walls close in, or swap an old system for a new one with minimal fuss.

What you get with every install

A good install is the difference between a system that runs quietly for years and one that struggles from day one. This is what I do on every job.

Installation questions

Something else? Call Luke on 0444 541 409.

How much does it cost to install a split system?

It depends on the size of the unit, the brand and how far the pipes need to run between the indoor and outdoor units. Quotes are free, so you’ll know the exact price before you commit to anything.

What size air conditioner do I need?

It depends on the room’s size, ceiling height, insulation, windows and which way it faces. Too small and it runs flat out; too big and it cycles on and off and wastes power. I work it out properly when I quote.

Should I get a split system or ducted?

Split systems are the most affordable way to cool individual rooms. Ducted suits larger homes where you want every room comfortable from one discreet system. Multi-head sits in between. I’ll talk you through the trade-offs for your home.

Which brand is best?

Mitsubishi Electric, Fujitsu, Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, General and Hisense all make reliable systems. The best fit depends on your budget, the room and the features you want, and I’ll give you honest options rather than push one brand.

Can you replace my old air conditioner?

Yes. I can take out the old unit and install the new one, often in the same spot where that makes sense. If the existing pipework isn’t suitable, I’ll explain why before I quote.

Do you install in new builds and renovations?

Yes. I can work with you or your builder to plan unit locations and run pipework and drains before the walls are closed in, then fit and commission the units at the end.

Is the installation guaranteed?

Yes. Every installation comes with a workmanship guarantee, and I’m happy to talk you through what it covers when I quote.
