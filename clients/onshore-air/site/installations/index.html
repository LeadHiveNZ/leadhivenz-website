What we install

The right system for the way you live

Whether you want one cool bedroom or the whole house comfortable, Luke will recommend the system that suits your home and budget, not the one with the biggest margin.

  1. 01

    Split systems

    The go-to for bedrooms, living areas and granny flats. Quiet, efficient inverter units sized to the room, so they are not running flat out every summer afternoon.

  2. 02

    Multi-head systems

    Two or more indoor units running off a single outdoor unit. Handy when you want several rooms cooled but only have space for one condenser.

  3. 03

    Ducted systems

    Discreet ceiling vents throughout the house, with zoning so you only cool the rooms you are using. Ideal for larger and two-storey homes.

  4. 04

    New builds & replacements

    Planning unit locations and pipe runs with you or your builder before the walls close in, or swapping an old system for a new one with minimal fuss.

What’s included

FAQ

Installation questions

How much does it cost to install a split system?

It depends on the size of the unit, the brand and how far the pipes need to run between the indoor and outdoor units. Luke gives free quotes, so you will know the exact price before you commit to anything.

What size air conditioner do I need?

It depends on the room’s size, ceiling height, insulation, windows and which way it faces. Too small and it runs flat out; too big and it cycles on and off and wastes power. Luke works it out properly when he quotes.

Should I get a split system or ducted?

Split systems are the most affordable way to cool individual rooms. Ducted suits larger homes where you want every room comfortable from one discreet system. Multi-head sits in between. Luke will talk you through the trade-offs for your home.

Which brand is best?

Mitsubishi Electric, Fujitsu, Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, General and Hisense all make reliable systems. The best fit depends on your budget, the room and the features you want, and Luke will give you honest options rather than push one brand.

Can you replace my old air conditioner?

Yes. Luke can take out the old unit and install the new one, often in the same spot where that makes sense. If the existing pipework isn’t suitable, he will explain why before he quotes.

Do you install in new builds and renovations?

Yes. Luke can work with you or your builder to plan unit locations and run pipework and drains before the walls are closed in, then fit and commission the units at the end.

Is the installation guaranteed?

Yes. Luke backs every installation with a workmanship guarantee, and he is happy to talk you through what it covers when he quotes.
