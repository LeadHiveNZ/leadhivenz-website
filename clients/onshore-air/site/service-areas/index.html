Built for coastal conditions

Salt air and humidity are tough on air conditioners. Outdoor units corrode faster near the beach, and coils clog sooner. Luke recommends the right placement and protection on new installs, and regular servicing and hydro cleans to keep coastal systems healthy.

Hinterland heat, sorted

Up the range in Nambour, Palmwoods, Montville and Maleny, summers are still and sticky and winter nights get properly cool. A reverse-cycle system handles both, and Luke can size one to suit older Queenslanders and new builds alike.
