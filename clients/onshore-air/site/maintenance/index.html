Annual service

What’s checked on a service

A proper service is more than a quick filter rinse. Here is what Luke works through on every visit.

  • Filters removed, cleaned and checked
  • Indoor coil and fan inspected for dirt and mould
  • Drain line and tray checked and flushed so they don’t overflow
  • Outdoor unit cleared of leaves, sand and debris
  • Refrigerant operation checked
  • Electrical connections and isolator inspected
  • Remote, modes and thermostat tested
  • Airflow and outlet temperature measured
  • Corrosion checked, especially on beachside units
  • A plain-English rundown of anything that needs attention

Why service it

Small job now, fewer problems later

  • Lower running costs

    Clean filters and coils let the system move heat efficiently, so it uses less power to keep you comfortable.

  • Fewer breakdowns

    Worn parts and blocked drains get picked up early, before they fail on the hottest day of the year.

  • Longer life

    Regular care helps your system cope with salt air, humidity and long Queensland summers.

  • Healthier air

    Less dust, mould and bacteria circulating through the rooms you live and sleep in.

Who books a service

Homeowners

FAQ

Servicing questions

How often should my air con be serviced?

Once a year suits most homes. Beachside homes, rentals and systems that run most days can benefit from a check every six months.

What’s the difference between a service and a hydro clean?

A service is a health check of the whole system, indoors and out. A hydro clean is a deep wash of the indoor unit’s coil, fan and drain tray. Many people book both at the same visit.

Do you service all brands?

Yes. Luke services all major brands, including Mitsubishi Electric, Fujitsu, Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, General, Hisense, Daikin and Panasonic.

What happens if you find a problem?

Luke will explain what he has found, what it means and what it will cost to fix, before doing any extra work. Nothing happens without your go-ahead.

Can you service my rental or holiday let?

Yes. Just let Luke know who to contact for access when you book, and he can work around tenants or guests.
