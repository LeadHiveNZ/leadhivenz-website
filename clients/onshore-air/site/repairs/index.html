Sound familiar?

The most common faults I get called out for. If yours isn’t here, call anyway.

  • Not cooling (or heating)

    Blowing warm air, taking forever to cool down, or one room that never gets comfortable. Often low refrigerant, a blocked coil or a failing part.

  • Leaking water

    Water dripping from the indoor unit or running down the wall usually means a blocked drain, a dirty coil or a unit that isn’t sitting level.

  • Noisy or rattling

    Buzzing, squealing, rattling or a loud outdoor unit. Noises tend to get worse, and cheaper to fix the earlier they’re caught.

  • Error codes and flashing lights

    A code or flashing light on the display is the system telling you something’s wrong. Note it down or take a photo before you call.

  • Won’t turn on

    No response from the remote, or it starts and then cuts out. It could be power, the remote, a sensor or a control board.

  • Bad smells

    A musty or mouldy smell when it starts up is usually a dirty coil. A hydro clean is often the fix.

Repair questions

Something else? Call Luke on 0444 541 409.

How much does an air con repair cost?

It depends on the fault and the parts needed. I diagnose the problem first and quote the repair before any work starts, so you can decide with the full picture.

Is it worth repairing an old air conditioner?

Often, yes. Many faults are minor and well worth fixing. But if a unit is old and needs a major part such as a compressor, replacing it can make more sense. I’ll give you both options and an honest opinion.

Why is my air conditioner leaking water?

Most leaks come from a blocked drain line or drain tray, a dirty coil that’s icing up, or a unit that isn’t sitting level. It’s usually a straightforward fix, but leave it too long and the water can damage plaster and paint.

Why is my air con blowing warm air?

First check it’s set to Cool and the filters are clean. If it still blows warm, it could be low refrigerant, a faulty sensor or a problem with the outdoor unit. Give me a call and I’ll talk you through it.

What does the error code on my unit mean?

Codes differ between brands and models. Note the code (or take a photo of the display) and the model number, and tell me when you call. I can often tell you what it means over the phone.

Do you do same-day repairs?

Where possible, yes. It depends on the day’s bookings, so call early and I’ll tell you honestly when I can get there.
