Common problems

Sound familiar?

These are the most common air con faults. If yours isn’t on the list, call anyway.

  • Not cooling (or heating)

    Blowing warm air, taking forever to cool down, or one room that never gets comfortable. Often low refrigerant, a blocked coil or a failing part.

  • Leaking water

    Water dripping from the indoor unit or running down the wall usually means a blocked drain, a dirty coil or a unit that isn’t sitting level.

  • Noisy or rattling

    Buzzing, squealing, rattling or a loud outdoor unit. Noises tend to get worse, and cheaper to fix the earlier they are caught.

  • Error codes and flashing lights

    A code or flashing light on the display is the system telling you something is wrong. Note it down or take a photo before you call.

  • Won’t turn on

    No response from the remote, or it starts and then cuts out. It could be power, the remote, a sensor or a control board.

  • Bad smells

    A musty or mouldy smell when it starts up is usually a dirty coil. A hydro clean is often the fix.

FAQ

Repair questions

How much does an air con repair cost?

It depends on the fault and the parts needed. Luke diagnoses the problem first and quotes the repair before any work starts, so you can decide with the full picture.

Is it worth repairing an old air conditioner?

Often, yes. Many faults are minor and well worth fixing. But if a unit is old and needs a major part such as a compressor, replacing it can make more sense. Luke will give you both options and an honest opinion.

Why is my air conditioner leaking water?

Most leaks come from a blocked drain line or drain tray, a dirty coil that is icing up, or a unit that isn’t sitting level. It is usually a straightforward fix, but leave it too long and the water can damage plaster and paint.

Why is my air con blowing warm air?

First check it is set to Cool and the filters are clean. If it still blows warm, it could be low refrigerant, a faulty sensor or a problem with the outdoor unit. Give Luke a call and he will talk you through it.

What does the error code on my unit mean?

Codes differ between brands and models. Note the code (or take a photo of the display) and the model number, and tell Luke when you call. He can often tell you what it means over the phone.

Do you do same-day repairs?

Where possible, yes. It depends on the day’s bookings, so call early and Luke will tell you honestly when he can get there.
