**Patterns**
1. **Blue owns the category.** 13 of the 15 brands are blue-led. Red is the "OEM energy" accent (Mitsubishi, Alliance, Crown, Metro, Static). Yellow mostly shows up as a CTA colour and rarely as an identity colour.
2. **Type is stock geometric sans.** The local tier runs on Montserrat and Poppins. Nobody local has a crafted, kerned wordmark.
3. **Trust lives beside the logo, never inside it.** It shows up as ARCtick and QBCC numbers, review counts, "since 19xx", workmanship guarantees and a strip of Daikin / Mitsubishi Electric / Airstage dealer badges. Onshore's logo has to sit in that strip without looking small-time, so it needs to be compact, flat and symmetrical.
4. **Marks split into two groups.** OEMs use abstract geometry (delta, diamonds). Local installers default to snowflakes, swooshes, thermometers, roofs and peaks, or have no mark at all.

## Clichés to avoid
- Snowflake + airflow swoosh, a snowflake inside a sun, thermometers, roof outlines with an air tail.
- Gradient spheres or orbs, glass, chrome, bevels, drop shadows.
- Outlined, inline or condensed "surf-shop" lettering. This is the current logo's biggest weakness.
- Electric tech-blue + red. It reads as national franchise or wholesaler.
- Bundles of thin parallel stripes. They fill in at 16 px and cannot be embroidered.

## White space Onshore Air can own
- **Warm-to-cool, coastal.** No one in the set tells "hot outside, cool inside" in a single geometric symbol. Onshore's sun-meets-sea sphere already does, so refine it rather than replace it.
- **Navy + sun-gold + ocean blue.** A deep navy lead with a warm gold (not lemon) and a clear ocean blue separates Onshore from the tech-blue/red pack and from Smarter Air's `#FFD700`.
- **Premium-local restraint.** The Noosa premium brands look expensive through space, one face and few colours. Local HVAC hasn't caught up.
- **A real system.** Most locals ship one PNG. Onshore gets stacked, horizontal, icon, mono and reversed versions with clear-space rules.

## Strategy
1. Keep the circular sun-meets-sea symbol and its diagonal warm/cool split. It is the brand's only equity, and it literally describes the product.
2. Rebuild it as flat geometry: 3–5 solid bands, no gradients, no hairlines. That way it embroiders, engraves and reads at 16 px.
3. Lead with deep navy, make sun-gold the signature and ocean blue the cool counterpoint. No red, no lemon yellow.
4. Replace the outlined display face with a heavy, kerned geometric sans, delivered as outlines.
5. Ship a full system that sits confidently beside Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric and Airstage badges, from a favicon up to a 6 m van.
