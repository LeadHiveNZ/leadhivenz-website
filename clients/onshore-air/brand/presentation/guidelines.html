Guidelines

Clear space

Leave a gap of x on every side, where x is the height of the capital letters in ONSHORE. Nothing else (text, photos, the edge of a van panel) goes inside the dashed line. The supplied files already include half of that gap.

Guidelines

Which file do I use?

The file names are the same in every concept pack.

Van wrap or signSend the signwriter horizontal.svg. It is a vector file and scales to any size; never enlarge a PNG. Use horizontal-mono-navy.svg for one-colour cut vinyl.
Polos and caps (embroidery)Send the embroiderer mono-white.svg for navy shirts, or mono-navy.svg for white or light shirts. Ask for a sew-out sample first.
Google, Facebook, Instagram pictureUpload png/icon-app-512.png. The platforms crop it to a circle and it is designed for that.
Websitehorizontal-on-navy.svg in the header on a computer, horizontal-compact-on-navy.svg on a phone. Your web person will know what to do with these.
Invoices, quotes and email signaturehorizontal.svg, or png/horizontal@2x.png where the software will not take SVG.
Browser tab (favicon)favicon.svg, plus png/icon-32.png as a fallback and png/icon-app-180.png for iPhones.
Business cardsprimary-stacked.svg on the front, icon-on-navy.svg on a navy back.
One-colour jobs (stamps, engraving)The mono- and icon-mono- files, in navy or white.
