FAQ

Good to know

Still have a question?

Our manager Zafar and the team are happy to help with bookings, groups and car hire.

(04) 939 1747
What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00pm and check-out is by 10:00am. Self check-in is available, so a late arrival is no problem. Just let us know your arrival time.

Is there parking?

Yes. Free on-street parking is available right outside the motel.

How far is the airport?

Wellington International Airport is 3 km away, about 5 minutes by taxi or Uber. There is a bus stop at the door on the Kilbirnie bus route, and the city centre is about 10 minutes by car.

Can I hire a car at the motel?

Yes. 747 Car Hire runs right here on site. Collect your car when you check in, with one-way options to Wellington Airport, Picton, Christchurch, Auckland and Queenstown. Ask when you book, or request a quote.

Do the rooms have kitchenettes?

Yes, every studio does. Each kitchenette has a fridge, microwave, kettle, toaster and kitchenware, plus complimentary tea and coffee. Kilbirnie's supermarkets are a 5-minute walk away.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard and EFTPOS. We're also Halal-friendly.
