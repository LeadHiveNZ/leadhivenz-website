For your gold, three things determine the price: weight, purity and the current market gold price.

If you have bullion and coins, Gold Exchange will pay you 98% to 100% of the gold spot price. For gold jewellery, we will pay you 85% to 100% of the gold spot price. The higher the purity, the more your gold items are worth.

If your jewellery or gold item is not 24ct pure gold, it also contains other metals (otherwise known as “alloys”). This guide shows how much gold each carat normally contains, and the hallmark on the jewellery:

9ct: 37.5% gold, hallmark 375
14ct: 58.5% gold, hallmark 585
18ct: 75% gold, hallmark 750
22ct: about 91.7% gold, hallmark 22ct
24ct: 99.9% gold, hallmark 24ct

For your diamonds, prices vary according to the 4 C's: clarity, colour, carat and cut. Other factors, such as shape and current market trends, also affect the price.

Gold Exchange will price your diamond according to the international wholesale diamond market to give you a fair value and the most cash. If you have a diamond you wish to sell, have as much information at hand as possible, including any international grading laboratory certificate, as this will help you get a better deal.
