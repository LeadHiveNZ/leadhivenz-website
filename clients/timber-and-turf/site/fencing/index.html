Common questions

Do I need council consent for a new fence?

Most standard residential fences up to 2.0 metres high don't need building consent. Fences over 2.0 metres generally do, and there are extra rules for pool fencing, some zones, and fences built on top of retaining walls. We'll advise on your site when we quote, and Auckland Council can confirm if you're unsure.

Who pays for a boundary fence?

Usually it's shared. Under the Fencing Act 1978, neighbours generally split the cost of an adequate boundary fence 50/50. It's best to talk to your neighbour before work starts. We can give you a quote to share with them.

How long does a new fence take?

It depends on the length, the ground and the style. We'll give you a realistic timeframe with your quote, and we'll turn up when we say we will.

Can you take away my old fence?

Yes. We can pull out the old fence, take the timber away and build the new one in its place.

Rules change and every site's different, so treat these as a general guide. We'll advise on your job when we quote.
