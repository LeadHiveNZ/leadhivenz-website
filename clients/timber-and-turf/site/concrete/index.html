Common questions

How long before I can walk or drive on new concrete?

As a rough guide, you can usually walk on it after a day or two and drive on it after about a week. Concrete keeps curing and gets close to full strength at around 28 days. Weather and mix make a difference, so we'll tell you exactly what to expect for your job.

Do I need consent for a concrete path or driveway?

Most paths and pads on your own property don't need building consent. A new or changed vehicle crossing needs approval from Auckland Transport. We'll advise on your job when we quote.

Why does base prep matter so much?

A slab sits on whatever's underneath it. We dig out soft ground, put down and compact a proper base, and pour to the right thickness so your concrete stays where it should.

Can you do the digging as well?

Yes. We've got our own small digger for digging out, levelling and trenching, so the whole job is handled by one crew.

Rules change and every site's different, so treat these as a general guide. We'll advise on your job when we quote.
