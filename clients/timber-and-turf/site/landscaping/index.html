Common questions

Can you clear a really overgrown section?

Yes, that's bread-and-butter work. We'll come and have a look, tell you what's involved and give you a clear price.

Do you take the green waste away?

We can include removal in your quote, so you're not left with a pile in the driveway.

Ready lawn or seed?

Ready lawn gives you an instant result and is usually usable within a few weeks. Seed is cheaper but takes longer and depends on the season. We'll talk you through what suits your section and budget.

Can you get a digger into my section?

Usually, yes. A small digger fits through most side access. We check access when we come out to quote.

Rules change and every site's different, so treat these as a general guide. We'll advise on your job when we quote.
