Who we are

A local operator who does the job properly

Timber & Turf is run by Chris, an owner-operator based in Manukau.

We do timber fencing, concrete, landscaping, decks, steps and retaining walls, plus small digger work for site prep and earthworks. It's the kind of work that usually needs three or four different trades. We do it with one crew.

Because it's owner-operated, Chris is the one you talk to when you call, the one who comes out to quote, and the one on site doing the work. No call centre, no salesperson, no being handed off.

The way we work is simple: turn up when we say we will, give you a clear price before we start, do the job properly, and leave the site tidy.
