Common questions

Do I need consent for a retaining wall?

Generally, retaining walls over 1.5 metres high need building consent, and so do lower walls that hold up extra load above them, like a driveway, a building or another slope (called surcharge). We'll look at your site and advise what applies.

Do I need consent for a deck?

Lower decks often don't need building consent, but higher decks (generally over 1.5 metres off the ground) do, and every deck still has to meet the Building Code. We'll advise on your job when we quote.

What timber do you use?

Treated pine with the right treatment level for each part. In-ground posts and poles need a higher rating than decking boards and framing. We'll talk you through the options when we quote.

How long will a timber retaining wall last?

A properly built timber wall with good drainage behind it will last a long time. Most failures come down to poor drainage or undersized posts, which is exactly where we don't cut corners.

Rules change and every site's different, so treat these as a general guide. We'll advise on your job when we quote.
