## His Quick Feaso, as built in Excel (for the in-app version)

Revenue: lots × price per lot (example 30 × $300,000 = $9,000,000). Less agency 2.5%, legal
$3,000 per lot, and GST under the margin scheme = (revenue − land purchase price) ÷ 11.
Net revenue = revenue − agency − legal − margin GST.

Costs:
- Land: purchase price, stamp duty (5% in his sheet), legals ($5,000). = Acquisition.
- Construction: electrics and comms per lot ($18,000), civils per lot ($100,000), planting /
  landscaping per lot ($5,000), sub-total, contingency 5%. = Total construction.
- Consultants: electrical design, engineer, surveyor (per lot $3,000), planning, other.
- Fees: marketing, project management ($5,000 × 18 months), bond (1% of civils), accounting and
  admin, holding, council CC approval fee, s94 / s7.11 contributions per lot ($20,000; his sheet
  charges 29 lots for a 30-lot job, i.e. a credit for the existing lot).
Total cost; gross profit before finance = net revenue − total cost.
Finance: equity; funds to complete = total cost − equity; land debt at 8%; construction debt at 8%
on half the balance (average draw); establishment fee 1.5%. Interest costs. Total cost including
finance. Net profit post finance; margin on cost = net profit ÷ total cost inc finance.
Per-lot column for revenue, acquisition, construction, consultants, total cost and profit.

Quirks to fix in the app version: his sheet hard-codes 30 lots in three per-lot formulas (legal
quantity, total cost per lot, profit per lot) and 29 for s94; in the app these follow the lot
count, with the existing-lot credit as its own input.
