# Software build agreement

**Between:** J Ingram T/A LeadHive AU, ABN 78 612 053 288 ("LeadHive")
**And:** Wellco Developments Pty Ltd, ABN 81 096 401 400 ("Wellco")
**Date:** [date]

## 1. What LeadHive will do

1.1 Connect the Wellco project app, as shown in the demo Wellco has reviewed, to a database and file
storage in Wellco's own Supabase account, with a login for Dan Wellman.
1.2 Publish the app on Wellco's own Netlify account so it works on phone, iPad and computer.
1.3 Load Wellco's current projects, companies and contacts from details Wellco provides.
1.4 Set up a folder and instructions so Wellco can make its own changes using its own Claude account.
1.5 Run one handover session (up to 1 hour) showing how to use the app, back it up and make changes.
1.6 Fix faults in LeadHive's work reported within 30 days of go-live, at no charge.

## 2. Price and payment

2.1 The fixed price is **A$2,000**.
2.2 50% (A$1,000) is due before work starts. The balance is due at go-live.
2.3 The A$350 setup session already paid is separate and is not part of this price.
2.4 Wellco pays Supabase and Netlify directly. Supabase Pro is about US$25 a month at go-live.
Netlify's free plan is expected to be enough.
2.5 Changes outside clause 1, or after the 30-day fix period, are quoted before any work starts.

## 3. Ownership and accounts

3.1 Wellco owns its data, documents and its copy of the app.
3.2 The accounts are Wellco's. LeadHive's access is by invitation and Wellco can remove it at any time.
3.3 LeadHive keeps the right to reuse the general software, without any of Wellco's data, names or
documents, for other clients.

## 4. What the app does and does not do

4.1 The app records projects, quotes, bills, invoices, GST treatments and BAS figures. It does not pay
bills, move money, lodge BAS or file anything with the ATO or any authority.
4.2 The app is a record-keeping tool. It is not tax, accounting, legal or financial advice. Wellco's
accountant remains responsible for checking and lodging BAS and tax returns.
4.3 For the first BAS quarter after go-live, Wellco agrees to keep its existing records as well and
compare the figures before relying on the app.

## 5. Changes Wellco makes

5.1 Once Wellco, or anyone acting for Wellco including an AI assistant, changes the app's code or
database, fixing those changes is outside the 30-day fix period and is quoted as paid work.

## 6. Backups

6.1 Wellco is responsible for keeping Supabase on a plan with daily backups once it holds live data,
and for exporting its records at least each quarter (the app's Excel export) to its own storage.

## 7. Liability

7.1 The app is supplied as is after the 30-day fix period.
7.2 To the extent the law allows, LeadHive's total liability under this agreement is limited to the
fees Wellco has paid under it. LeadHive is not liable for indirect or consequential loss, including
lost profits or tax penalties.
7.3 Nothing in this agreement excludes rights Wellco has under the Australian Consumer Law that cannot
be excluded.

## 8. Ending

8.1 Either party can end this agreement by email. Wellco pays for work done to that date, and keeps
everything delivered so far.
