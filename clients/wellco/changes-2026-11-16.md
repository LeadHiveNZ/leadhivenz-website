# Wellco: changes from the setup session (16 Nov 2026)

Source: Zoom setup session, Dan Wellman and Joe Ingram, 16 Nov 2026 (Otter transcript).
Dan's verdict on the demo: "the layout and system works for me, I can get my head around it."
Next step agreed on the call: Joe builds a final draft, Dan uses it for about a week, then a
10 to 15 minute call to set up hosting on Dan's own account.

## Changes Dan asked for

1. **New stage: Certification & Registration**, between Construction and Sales.
   Dan: "that's a very crucial staging point... a lot of people fall over." It covers the sign-offs
   needed before settlements can happen: certifier, surveyor, town planner, NBN, the bank (mortgage
   discharge / consent), council's final sign-off and subdivision certificate, plan registration at
   LRS / Titles Queensland. It is also where the big end-of-job money is pooled: council
   contributions (his example: "council goes, yeah we're ready to sign so you can settle, but hey,
   we need 500 grand"). The stage needs a funds-needed view: a total of the money that must be paid
   before sign-off, what is already paid, and what is still to find.
   New default stage list (9 stages): 1 Sourcing, 2 Feasibility, 3 Acquisition & DD, 4 Consultants,
   5 Development Approval, 6 Construction, 7 Certification & Registration, 8 Sales & Settlements,
   9 Bonds & Wind-up.

2. **Stages and wording are adaptable.** Dan asked "can another stage be added easily?" and wants
   "the ability to add some extra items or change the wording on the item just for my own personal".
   He should be able to rename a stage, add a stage, and add or rename tasks, on a project and on
   the default template, without asking Joe.

3. **New project type: Residential build.** "I do do my own projects occasionally." Types are now
   Land subdivision, Commercial / industrial, Residential build. Each type has its own default stage
   template; residential build swaps Consultants / DA for Design & approvals and has Occupation
   certificate inside Certification.

4. **Contact roles and categories are adaptable.** Dan wants to add a new role "as I go" without it
   being "done in the back house", add a new category (branch), choose which category a role sits in,
   and put a contact in more than one category ("sometimes I have consultants that sit in two
   categories"). His example split: Civil contractors and Building contractors as separate groups.

5. **Cost category per bill line.** One bill can have lines with different GST treatment AND
   different cost categories, "a bit like Xero". His example: a council invoice with GST-free
   contributions (Council & Gov) and GST-inclusive works on their infrastructure (Construction) or
   final certification inspections. Budget vs actual, BAS and every export must add up by line,
   not by bill. Dan confirmed the existing "Add line" and per-line tax rate already do the GST part.

6. **Documents at the deal stage, filed by stage and type.** When an agent sends a contract, plans
   or area sales stats for a site, Dan wants to drop them on the deal straight away, instead of
   making a folder on OneDrive first. When the deal becomes a project, those files come with it.
   Inside a project, documents are organised by stage and by document type. Files are not just PDFs:
   "a lot of Excel" and Word too.

## Confirmed as fine, no change

- Quotes, engagements, Bills / Invoices tabs, Awaiting approval / Awaiting payment, Mark paid with
  bank reference and date, the deal-to-project flow, the overall layout.
- OneDrive stays his backup. He has a business and a personal OneDrive plus a Google Drive copy and
  a hard copy at home. He finds Google Drive's layout confusing, so OneDrive is the target for the
  automated backup.

## Still open

  His screening process from the call suggests: zoning (and how much of the site it covers), lot
  size / minimum lot size, likely yield, asking price vs estimated end value, GST status of the
  sale (from the contract), easements or services on title (his example: a water main through the
  middle), agent and source. Confirm with Dan.
- Hosting account set-up: on the follow-up call, on Dan's own account.
- Dan: "I don't mind paying extra and developing this further if it needs." Scope beyond the
