Best times to call

Ian is often out on the river during the day, where the phone signal can be patchy. If he doesn’t pick up, leave a message or send a text with your name and number, and he will call you back.

Early mornings and evenings are usually the easiest time to catch him.

When you call

It helps to have a rough idea of:

  • When you would like to come, and how many nights
  • How many of you there are, and the ages of any kids
  • Whether you have panned for gold before
  • Anything Ian should know about, such as mobility or dietary needs

Location

Murchison, Tasman, New Zealand

Ian’s place is in Murchison, at the top of the South Island, on the main road between Nelson and the West Coast. He will give you directions when you book.
