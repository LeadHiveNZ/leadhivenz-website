Our services

Three licences, one team, no runaround

  • Plumbing

Most jobs don't stay in one lane. A bathroom renovation needs new waste pipes and a drain connection. A gas hot water upgrade needs a gasfitter and a plumber. Because GJS does plumbing, drainlaying and gasfitting in-house, you deal with one crew from the first look to the final certificate.

Whether it's a villa in New Plymouth, a dairy shed out past Kaponga or a café kitchen in Hāwera, we quote upfront, turn up when we said we would and leave the place tidy.

How we quote

A clear price before we pick up a tool

    Tell us about the job
