Hot water in Taranaki

Replace it, upgrade it, or make it cheaper to run

Hot water is one of the biggest power costs in a Kiwi home, so when it's time for a new system it pays to get the right one. We'll look at your household, your power or gas supply and where the cylinder lives, then give you straight advice and an upfront quote.

We replace tired electric cylinders, install heat-pump hot water systems like the Ecospring, fit continuous-flow gas units and convert older low-pressure homes to mains pressure. As licensed plumbers and gasfitters, we handle the whole install ourselves — from the valves to the gas line — and leave you with the paperwork.

No hot water?

Check the switchboard first — the hot water switch or fuse may have tripped. If the cylinder is leaking, switch it off at the switchboard and close the cold water valve feeding it, then call us. We're on call 24/7.

Your options

Which hot water system suits you?

Four common setups we install across Taranaki. Not sure? Tell us how your household uses hot water and we'll recommend one.

Electric cylinder

Best forLike-for-like replacements and homes without gas.

  • Simple, reliable and familiar
  • Works with controlled-power plans
  • Sizes to suit one person or a big family

Heat-pump hot water

Best forHouseholds wanting lower power bills over the long run.

  • Uses far less power than a standard element
  • Needs a well-ventilated spot, usually outside
  • We install Ecospring and other systems

Gas continuous flow

Best forBusy homes with back-to-back showers and a gas supply.

  • Hot water on demand — it doesn't run out
  • No cylinder taking up cupboard space
  • Runs on natural gas or LPG bottles

Mains-pressure upgrade

Best forOlder homes with weak, low-pressure showers.

  • Strong, even pressure at every tap
  • Pairs with a new electric or heat-pump cylinder
  • We check your pipework can handle it first

FAQ

Hot water questions

How long does a hot water cylinder last?

Many cylinders give well over a decade of service, but water quality, pressure and how well the valves are looked after all play a part. Signs yours is on the way out: rusty or discoloured hot water, water pooling around the base, a relief valve that never stops dripping, or running out of hot water sooner than you used to.

What affects the price of a new system?

Mainly the type of system and its size, whether you're staying on low pressure or moving to mains pressure, and how easy the site is to get to. Changes to pipework, a new gas line, moving the cylinder or adding valves and seismic restraints also come into it. We'll look at the job and give you a clear, upfront quote before any work starts.

Heat pump or gas — which is better?

It depends on your house. A heat-pump cylinder suits most homes on electricity and keeps running costs down, but it needs space outside with good airflow. Continuous-flow gas is great for big households and back-to-back showers, and makes most sense if you already have natural gas or LPG. We'll talk you through both and recommend the one that fits.

Can you replace my cylinder the same day?

Often, yes — when the right size is available from our suppliers and access is straightforward. Call as early as you can. If a straight swap can't happen that day, we'll make everything safe and tell you exactly when you'll be back in hot water.
