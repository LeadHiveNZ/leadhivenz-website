Common jobs

What to expect when we call round

The jobs we're asked about most, what usually causes them, and how we sort them out.

Leaking tap

A steady drip adds up to a lot of wasted water. It's usually a worn washer, cartridge or O-ring, sometimes a mixer that's simply worn out.

What to expect

We isolate the water, replace the worn parts (or the whole tapware if it's past saving) and test it under pressure. Most taps are sorted in one short visit.

Toilet keeps running

A cistern that won't stop filling, or trickles into the pan, is usually a tired inlet valve or a perished flush seal. It's quiet, but it costs you.

What to expect

We find the faulty part, replace it with a quality valve or seal, and check the pan connector and stop tap while we're there so nothing else is about to fail.

Low water pressure

Weak showers can come from a blocked filter, a failing pressure-limiting valve, corroded galvanised pipe or an old low-pressure hot water system.

What to expect

We test the pressure at the toby and at your taps to find where it's dropping, then give you clear options, from a simple valve swap to a re-pipe or mains-pressure upgrade.

Burst pipe

A burst can do serious damage fast. Turn the water off at the toby straight away, keep clear of wet electrics, then call us on 06 765 8423, day or night.

What to expect

We find the break, repair or replace the damaged section and check for hidden water damage. We'll note what we found to help with your insurance claim.

FAQ

Plumbing questions

Can you give me a price over the phone?

For straightforward jobs like a tap washer or a toilet valve we can usually give you a good idea on the phone, especially if you send a photo. For anything bigger we'll come and look first, then give you a clear quote before any work starts.

Where is my water toby?

In town it's usually in a small box near your front boundary, often by the footpath or driveway. On rural properties the shut-off may be at the pump, tank or where the supply enters the house. It's worth finding before you need it. Ask us next time we're out and we'll show you.

Do you work on rental properties?

Yes. We work with landlords and property managers around Taranaki, can arrange access directly with your tenants, and send the invoice to whoever is paying. Repairs are explained in plain English so you know what was done and why.

Do you do commercial plumbing?

We do. Shops, offices, cafés and commercial kitchens, plus farm buildings, dairy sheds and workshops. Where we can, we'll work around your opening hours to keep disruption to a minimum.
