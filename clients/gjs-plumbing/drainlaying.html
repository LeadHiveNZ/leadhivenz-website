Drainlaying & drainage

What's underground matters

Drains stay out of sight until they fail. Then it's sewage in the yard, water ponding under the house or a garage that floods every time it buckets down. With the rainfall we get in Taranaki, good stormwater and the right fall matter more than most places.

As licensed drainlayers we plan, lay and repair drainage for houses, new builds, subdivisions and farms. We book the council inspection and supply the as-built plan, so the job is signed off and on record for the life of the property.

Know the signs

Signs you have a drain problem

Drains rarely fail without warning. If you notice any of these, get it looked at early. It's almost always cheaper than waiting for it to overflow.

Blocked drain right now?

Sewage or water backing up into the house is an emergency. Stop using toilets, showers and the washing machine, and call us on 06 765 8423, any hour.

Call now Emergency help

  • Gurgling plugholes or toilets

    Air being forced back up the pipe usually means a partial blockage or a venting fault further down the line.

  • Slow-draining sinks & showers

    One slow fixture is often a local clog. Several at once points to a problem in the main drain.

  • Bad smells inside or out

    Sewer smells can mean a cracked pipe, a failed joint or a trap that has dried out.

  • Soggy ground or extra-green grass

    A leaking drain waters the lawn from below, and over time it can undermine paths, driveways and foundations.

  • Water backing up

    An overflowing gully trap, or a toilet that rises when you flush, means the line is blocked. Call us before it gets worse.

FAQ

Drainage questions

Is a blocked drain my problem or the council's?

Generally you're responsible for the private drains on your property, and the council looks after the public main. Exactly where the line sits varies a little between councils. When we investigate, we'll tell you which side of it the problem is on before any work goes ahead.

Do new drains need council consent?

Most new drainage work needs a building consent and must be inspected by the council before it's backfilled. We book the inspection, and once it's passed we supply the as-built plan showing where everything runs.

Can you connect my septic tank or move me to town sewer?

Yes. We lay and repair the drains running to septic tanks on rural and lifestyle properties. Where a town sewer connection is available, we can lay the new line and connect you up.

What usually causes blocked drains?

Tree roots getting into old earthenware pipes, fat and food scraps down the kitchen sink, wipes flushed down the toilet, or a pipe that has cracked or sagged over time. We clear the blockage, then find the cause so it doesn't keep coming back.
