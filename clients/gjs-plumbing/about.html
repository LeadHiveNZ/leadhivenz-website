Our story

A local business with its name on the ute

GJS Plumbing, Drainage & Gasfitting is a family-owned business based in Stratford. Graham and Scott lead the team, so when you ring, you're dealing with the people who actually do the work.

We hold all three licensed trades under one roof — plumbing, drainlaying and gasfitting. One call covers a dripping tap, a blocked drain, a new gas hob or the services for a new build, and one crew is responsible for the whole job, from the quote through to sign-off.

We're members of Master Plumbers, and our work is right here in Taranaki: homes in town, farms and lifestyle blocks, builders and local businesses. Stratford based, Taranaki wide.

What we stand for

Four things we don't compromise on

Nothing fancy — just the way we'd want a tradesperson to treat our own place.

Turn up when we say

If we've booked you in, we're there. If something changes on the day, we ring you before you have to ring us.

Quote upfront

You'll know the price before we start. Find something unexpected? We stop and talk it through with you first.

Do it once, do it right

Good materials, proper falls and fixings, and everything tested before we leave — so you're not calling us back for the same job.

Leave it tidy

Drop sheets down, offcuts and old fittings taken away, and your place left the way we found it — minus the leak.
