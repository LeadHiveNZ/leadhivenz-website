FAQ

Trade questions

Something else? Call 09 215 9544, 7 days, 8am–8pm.

Is there a minimum order?

Not to open an account. We quote each job on its own, from a single unit for a renovation to every unit in a development.

Multi-unit and repeat work is priced to match the volume, so send us the full schedule if you have it.

What are your lead times?

It depends on the model and the quantity. Tell us your site programme and we’ll confirm availability on the quote, then lock in supply and install dates that match your stages.

Do you provide commissioning paperwork?

Yes. Every system we install is commissioned before we hand it over, and you get the invoice plus model and serial numbers for each unit for your handover or tenancy file.

Installs are completed to NZ electrical and refrigerant requirements. If your project needs specific documentation for sign-off, tell us when you apply and we’ll confirm what we supply on the quote.
