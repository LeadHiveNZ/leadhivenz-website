Privacy policy

What we collect when you ask us for a quote, why we need it, who sees it and how to get a copy. In plain English, under the Privacy Act 2020.

Last updated October 2026

This policy explains how Aircon Warehouse Limited (“Aircon Warehouse”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores and shares personal information. It applies to our website, our showroom and warehouse at 7/51 Ash Road, Wiri, and any time you deal with us by phone, email, Facebook or in person. We follow the Privacy Act 2020 and its information privacy principles.

What we collect

We only collect what we need to quote, install and look after your heat pump. That usually means:

  • your name, phone number and email address
  • your suburb and, if you go ahead, the install address
  • job details you give us, such as room sizes, photos of the space, the type of property and what you’re after
  • for installs, the make, model and serial numbers of the units we fit and the date we fitted them
  • for trade accounts, your company name, trade type and business contact details, plus anything you provide in a credit application
  • a record of quotes, invoices and payments

Most of this comes straight from you, through our website forms, phone calls, emails and messages. We don’t collect card or bank details through this website.

Why we collect it

We use your information to:

  • prepare your quote and answer your questions
  • book and carry out your installation
  • register your manufacturer warranty and handle any warranty claim
  • send after-care reminders, such as when filters need a clean
  • set up and run trade accounts
  • keep accurate business and tax records, as the law requires

We don’t sell your information, and we won’t send you marketing emails unless you’ve agreed to them. You can opt out at any time.

Who we share it with

We only share your information when it’s needed for your job or required by law:

  • Our installers and trades who carry out your installation, so they know where to go and what to fit.
  • A finance lender, only if you choose to apply for finance. The lender’s own privacy policy then applies to the information it holds.
  • Manufacturers and their New Zealand distributors (Daikin, Fujitsu (GENERAL Airstage) and Haier) to register your warranty and process warranty claims.
  • Service providers we use to run the business, such as email, accounting and website hosting. They may only use your information to provide that service to us.

Some of these providers store information on servers outside New Zealand. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure your information is protected in a way that is comparable to the Privacy Act 2020.

How we keep it safe

We store information securely and only people who need it to do their job can access it. If a privacy breach ever happens that is likely to cause you serious harm, we’ll tell you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner, as the Act requires.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it. Enquiries that don’t go ahead are deleted after a reasonable period. Job and warranty records are kept while your warranty and our after-care apply, and business and tax records are kept for at least seven years, as New Zealand tax law requires. After that, we securely delete or destroy them.

Seeing and correcting your information

You have the right to ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and to ask us to correct it if it’s wrong. Email info@airconwarehouse.co.nz and tell us what you need. We may ask you to confirm who you are first.

We’ll respond as soon as we can, and within 20 working days. If we can’t make a correction you ask for, we’ll explain why and add your request to our records.

Cookies and website analytics

Our website uses basic analytics to see which pages are visited and how people find us. This information is grouped together and isn’t used to identify you. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings and the site will still work.

When you follow a link to Google or Facebook, their own privacy policies apply.

Questions and complaints

If you have a question or a concern about how we’ve handled your information, please talk to us first and we’ll do our best to put it right. If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz (opens in a new tab).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated at the top.
