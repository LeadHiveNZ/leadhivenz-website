What’s in the price

What a standard install includes

Our installed prices cover a standard back-to-back install. Here’s exactly what that means, and what can change it.

Included as standard

  • Back-to-back install, indoor and outdoor units on the same wall
  • Up to 3 m of pipe
  • Standard wall bracket or ground pad
  • Condensate drain
  • Electrical connection to an existing suitable circuit
  • Commissioning and testing
  • Packaging and rubbish taken away
  • Remote and app walk-through

What can add cost

  • Longer pipe runs
    When the outdoor unit can’t sit directly behind the indoor one.
  • Two-storey outdoor placement
    Working at height takes more time and access gear.
  • Switchboard work or a new circuit
    If there’s no suitable circuit, or the board is full or old.
  • Wall type
    Brick, block and concrete take longer to drill than weatherboard.
  • Crane or scaffold
    Some apartments and high positions need hired access.
Anything extra is on your quote before we start, never added on install day.

FAQ

Package questions

Still unsure? Call 09 215 9544, 7 days, 8am–8pm.

Can I supply my own unit?

Ask us. We focus on units we supply, so the warranty is in one place and there’s one phone number to call if anything needs attention. Buying through us also means warehouse pricing on the unit itself.

What if my switchboard is old?

We check the switchboard when we quote. If there’s no room for a new circuit or the board needs work, we tell you up front and price it, so it isn’t a surprise on install day.

Installs are completed to NZ electrical and refrigerant requirements.

Do you remove the old unit?

We can. If you’re replacing an old heat pump, mention it when you ask for a quote and we’ll include taking it out. The refrigerant is recovered properly, not released, and removal is listed on your quote.

How do I pay?

Your quote sets out the payment terms before you commit, so you know exactly what is due and when.
