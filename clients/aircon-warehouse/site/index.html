FAQ

Good questions, straight answers

Still unsure? Call 09 215 9544, 7 days, 8am–8pm.

What size heat pump do I need?

As a guide: a bedroom needs 2.5–3.5 kW, a medium living room 5–6 kW, a large open-plan space 7–9 kW or more, and a ducted whole-home system 10–18 kW.

Insulation, glazing, ceiling height and which way the room faces all change this, so we confirm the size on your free quote.

How much does a heat pump cost to run?

A modern heat pump returns roughly 3–4 units of heat for each unit of electricity (its COP), which is why it is the cheapest form of heating in most Auckland homes.

A 6 kW unit typically costs around 20–50c an hour, depending on your tariff and how hard it’s working. Set it to 18–21°C for the best efficiency.

How long does installation take?

A standard back-to-back high-wall install usually takes 3–5 hours. Ducted systems take 1–3 days, depending on the home. We confirm timing on your quote.

Will you install a unit I bought somewhere else?

Ask us. We focus on units we supply, so the warranty is in one place and there’s one phone number to call if anything needs attention.

Ducted or high wall: which is right for me?

Ducted is out of sight, can be zoned room by room, and suits new builds and homes with roof-space access. High-wall units are the most affordable way to heat and cool individual rooms.

Multi-split sits between the two: one outdoor unit running several indoor units. More on ducted systems.
