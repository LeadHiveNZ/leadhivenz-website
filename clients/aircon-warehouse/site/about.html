Our story

Built around one simple idea

Buy the heat pump at warehouse pricing, and have the same team install it.

That idea keeps everything straightforward. The people who size your unit are the people who fit it. Our prices are on the website before you pick up the phone. And when the warranty needs attention, there’s one number to call, not a retailer and an installer pointing at each other.

The team brings more than 10 years’ experience in the industry, supplying and installing heat pumps across Auckland. We work with three brands we know inside out: Daikin, Fujitsu (GENERAL Airstage) and Haier.

Supply, installation, finance and trade accounts all sit under one roof at our warehouse in Wiri. Whether it’s a bedroom unit for a family home or every unit in a new development, you deal with the same people from quote to commissioning.

How we work

Four things we don’t compromise on

The same standards on every job, whether it’s one bedroom or a whole development.

  • Transparent pricing

    Prices on the website, not hidden behind a site visit. Your quote is a fixed price, and anything that could add cost is spelled out before we start.

  • Right size, not the biggest

    We size the unit to the room, not to the invoice. Bigger isn’t better if it costs more to buy and more to run.

  • Tidy installs

    Pipes planned before we drill, colour-matched capping, outdoor units on proper brackets or a pad, and dust sheets down.

  • One phone number for the warranty

    We supplied it and we installed it, so there’s no back and forth. Manufacturer warranty on every unit, and one number to call.
