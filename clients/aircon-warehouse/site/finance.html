Ways to pay

Pay the way that suits you

A heat pump is an investment in a warmer, drier home. You can pay for it outright or spread the cost.

Interest-free options

Spread the cost of a supply and install package without paying interest. The lender sets the fees and conditions, and we show you exactly what applies to your job before you commit.

Long-term finance

Smaller, regular repayments over a longer term. Useful for bigger jobs, like a ducted whole-home system or several rooms at once. Interest and fees apply and are set out in full.

Pay on completion

Prefer to pay outright? Pay a deposit when you accept your quote and the balance once the install is done, by bank transfer or card.

How it works

From quote to warm house

  1. Get your free quote

    Tell us about the space and we’ll come back with a fixed price for the job, usually within one business day.

  2. Choose how to pay

    Pay outright or ask about finance. We’ll explain the options that suit the size of the job, in plain English.

  3. Approval with the lender

    Your application is handled with the lender, against its own lending criteria. You see the full terms in writing first.

  4. Install day

    Once you’re approved and the quote is accepted, we book the install. Most high-wall installs take 3–5 hours.

Plain language

The fine print, up front

Finance is a loan. It’s worth knowing exactly how it works before you sign anything, so here it is without the jargon.

Before you apply

Finance is provided by a third-party lender. Terms, fees and lending criteria apply. We’ll give you the full terms in writing before you commit.

  • Approval is the lender’s decision, not ours
  • The lender checks the repayments are affordable for you
  • If a term isn’t clear, ask us and we’ll explain it
  • Paying outright is always an option

FAQ

Finance questions

Want to talk it through? Call 09 215 9544, 7 days, 8am–8pm.

Who can apply for finance?

Most homeowners buying a supply and install package can apply. Approval is up to the lender and depends on its lending criteria, including a check that the repayments are affordable, so we can’t promise an outcome.

We’ll tell you what the lender needs from you before you apply.

Does finance change the price?

Your quote is the price of the job. Depending on the option you choose, the lender may charge its own fees or interest on top.

We’ll walk you through those on your quote, so you can compare finance with paying outright before you decide.

Can I pay a deposit and the rest on completion?

Yes. Pay a deposit when you accept your quote and the balance once the install is finished, by bank transfer or card. The deposit amount is shown on your quote. See our terms of trade for the detail.
