# Why the site is built this way — competitor research summary

Researched 20+ "we buy campervans / caravans / backpacker vans" sites in NZ and Australia (Oct 2026).

## What the best sites do (and this site copies)
1. **Speed in the headline** — "today", "instant", "same day". Travelwheels (Sydney, the best backpacker buyer in AU): "Sell My Campervan Today – Instant Valuation Over WhatsApp", with the sub-line "Flights soon? … We can buy it today."
2. **3-step how-it-works** — quote → agree → we collect & pay. Every strong site has it.
3. **"We come to you" + free pickup** as a headline benefit (9 of 11 AU sites).
4. **Payment named explicitly** — "on the spot", "same day", "before we drive it away".
5. **List what the seller does NOT need** — no WOF, no cleaning, no advertising, we do the paperwork.
6. **Any age / any km / any condition** wording.
7. **Private sale vs selling to us** comparison and FAQ answers to scam/no-show fears.
8. **Departure-date logic** — only TREK Campers (NZ) asks for it. Travelwheels builds the whole page around "before your flight". This site asks "when do you want it gone?" and "need an airport handover?" in the form.
9. **WhatsApp as primary CTA** for backpacker buyers (Travelwheels, Travel Cars NZ, Backpacker Car NZ). Phone-first for local cash buyers. This site does both; WhatsApp/Messenger show once configured.
10. **Sticky call bar on mobile** — none of the competitors have one. Easy win.

## Gaps in NZ that Aroha owns
- **No NZ buyer combines**: nationwide + we come to you + airport handover + paid in full on the day + 24/7. Travel Cars NZ: Auckland yard only, "impromptu visits can't be accommodated", "no guarantees" for vans they didn't sell. TREK: pays 10% upfront, balance months later, requires WOF >1 month, <280,000 km, 2000+. Cash For Caravans (Chch): "we don't buy vehicles that have been converted into campervans".
- **Queenstown** has no buyer page at all. Christchurch/Auckland airport handover is unclaimed.
- **Off-season**: Travel Cars won't even list vans left March–July until mid-August. "We buy all year round" is a real differentiator.
- **Competitor sites are sloppy** — broken placeholders, typos, mismatched phone numbers. A clean fast mobile site wins on polish alone.

## Upgrades worth doing next (in order)
1. Real reviews with home country + Google rating.
2. City pages: `/sell-campervan-auckland`, `/sell-campervan-christchurch`, `/sell-campervan-queenstown` (Travel Cars and Used Car Guys rank this way).
3. German + French versions of the landing page (biggest working-holiday groups; only Backpacker Car NZ and Kiwi Road Trips bother).
4. "Recently bought" strip: van, city, date — nobody in NZ shows proof of volume.
5. Guide content for SEO: "How to sell a campervan in NZ before you fly", "NZTA change of ownership for travellers", "Best time of year to sell a campervan in NZ".
