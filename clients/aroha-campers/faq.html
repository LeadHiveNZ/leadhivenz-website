Frequently asked questions

Everything travellers ask us about selling a campervan in New Zealand. Can't find your answer? Call 0800 20 30 60 – we're available 24/7.

How do I get an offer?

Call 0800 20 30 60, send us a message, or fill in the short form with your van's details, a few photos, where it is and when you're leaving. We'll come back to you quickly with an offer. No obligation.

Do you really buy anywhere in New Zealand?

Yes. North Island and South Island. Auckland, Christchurch, Queenstown, Wellington and everywhere in between. We come to you, or we can meet you at the airport.

How do you pay?

Cash or bank transfer, your choice. You're paid when we take the van, not days later.

Can you meet me at the airport?

Yes. We can pick the campervan up at the airport before your flight, and we can drop you off at the airport when we collect the van. Tell us your flight date and time.

My van is old, high-km, or the WOF has run out. Will you still buy it?

Ask us anyway. We buy high-roof, low-roof, self-contained and older or unwanted campervans. Expired WOF, high mileage, DIY conversions and vans that haven't sold privately are all welcome.

What about the change of ownership paperwork?

We handle it with you on the day, so the van is officially off your name before you leave New Zealand.

Can I keep driving until my last day?

That's the idea. Tell us your departure date and we'll arrange the handover for the day you fly, so you can use the van right to the end of your trip.

What if I'm leaving in a day or two?

Call us. We're available 24/7 and we move fast. The sooner you get in touch, the easier it is to line up a handover that suits your flight.

Is there any cost or obligation to get an offer?

No. Getting an offer is free and there's no obligation. If you'd rather hold off or sell privately, no hard feelings.

Do I need to clean or empty the van?

Give it a reasonable tidy-up if you can, but don't stress. Talk to us about camping gear you no longer need as well – we can often take it with the van so you're not left trying to offload it on your last day.

Do you buy during the off-season?

Yes. We buy all year round, including the quieter months when it's hard to find a private buyer.

What should I have ready on the day?

Photo ID (passport or licence), the keys, any service records or manuals you have, and your bank details if you'd like a transfer. We take care of the NZTA change of ownership with you.

Can you pay into an overseas bank account?

Tell us where you bank when you get in touch and we'll confirm the easiest way to pay you before the handover.

What kinds of vehicles do you buy?

Mostly campervans and converted vans: Toyota Hiace, Estima, Nissan Caravan, Mazda Bongo, Ford Transit, Mercedes Sprinter, people movers with a bed in the back, pop-tops, self-contained and non-self-contained, and older vans and motorhomes. If you sleep in it, we're interested.

Will your offer change when you see the van?

We make our offer from the details and photos you send. As long as the van matches what you told us, that's the price. If something major wasn't mentioned, we'll talk it through with you honestly.

How is this different from a buy-back or selling on behalf?

A buy-back only works if you bought the van from that dealer. Selling on behalf means waiting weeks or months for your money and paying commission. We buy your van outright and pay you in full on handover, whoever you bought it from.

Ready to sell your campervan?

Call us any time, day or night. Or send your details and we'll come back to you fast with an offer.
