01 — Kitchens & Splashbacks

Splashbacks that anchor the room.

The splashback is the focal point of a kitchen, and it is unforgiving. We plan the set-out around windows, power outlets and rangehoods so cuts are symmetrical and the pattern or veining runs where it should — with mitred external corners for a solid, monolithic look.

Kitchen floors take more punishment than any other room. We check the substrate for flatness and deflection, match the adhesive to the tile format and the subfloor, and place movement joints where the building needs them, so the floor stays flat and crack-free for years.

  • Set-out planned around windows, outlets and appliances
  • Mitred and polished external corners
  • Large-format panel and natural stone splashbacks
  • Colour-matched grout and silicone finishes

02 — Bathrooms & Showers

Wet areas built from the membrane up.

A beautiful bathroom means nothing if it leaks. Every wet area we tile is waterproofed in line with AS/NZS 4858 — bond breakers and reinforced internal corners first, then membrane applied at the specified thickness and given its full cure time before a tile goes near it. Shower floors are formed with accurate falls to the waste, whether a centre grate or a linear drain.

On that foundation we bring the finish: level-entry showers, niches set out to line up with the grout joints, and feature walls in mosaic or handmade-look tile. We recommend epoxy grout where hygiene and stain resistance matter most, and a premium cement-based grout where a softer, natural joint suits the tile.

  • Waterproofing to AS/NZS 4858
  • Level-entry and linear-drain showers
  • Recessed niches and mosaic feature walls
  • Epoxy or premium cement grout, specified per space

03 — Floors & Large Format

Large format, laid dead flat.

Large-format porcelain — 600 × 1200 mm and beyond — gives a room a calm, seamless quality, but only when it is laid perfectly flat. We grind or screed the substrate to tolerance, back-butter every tile for full adhesive coverage and use a tile levelling system to eliminate lippage between edges.

Transitions, thresholds and movement joints are planned at the outset, not improvised on the day. We can also prepare floors for electric underfloor heating, so the heating mats and the tiles above them work together as one system.

  • Substrate grinding, levelling and screeding
  • Levelling systems for lippage-free joints
  • Full-coverage back-buttered installation
  • Underfloor heating preparation

04 — Natural Stone & Outdoor

Natural stone, treated with respect.

Marble, travertine, limestone and granite each behave differently — in how they are cut, fixed and sealed. We use white or rapid-setting adhesives where staining or moisture is a risk, blend pieces so natural variation is balanced across the room, and finish with a penetrating sealer suited to the stone.

Outdoors, Canterbury’s frosts demand more. Patios, steps and entries are laid with proper falls for drainage, frost-resistant materials and flexible exterior-grade adhesives, so the surface stays sound and safe underfoot through every season.

  • Marble, travertine, limestone and granite
  • Stone sealing and aftercare advice
  • Patios, steps and entranceways
  • Frost-resistant exterior systems
