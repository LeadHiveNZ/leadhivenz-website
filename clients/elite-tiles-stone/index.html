The Elite Standard

Tiling, elevated to a considered craft.

A finished surface is only as good as what lies beneath it. Every Elite project begins with the work you will never see — flattening substrates, setting accurate falls and installing waterproofing systems built to last decades, not seasons.

From there it is a matter of discipline: a set-out planned so cuts land where the eye won’t find them, joints held to a consistent width, crisp mitred edges and premium materials chosen for the space. The result looks effortless because the effort went in long before the first tile was laid.

Featured Project

A new-build kitchen in polished marble-look porcelain.

The brief was a floor with the presence of Carrara marble and none of the upkeep. We flattened the slab to tolerance, back-buttered every tile for full adhesive coverage and used a levelling system to keep each edge perfectly flush — so the veining reads cleanly from the kitchen through to the walk-in pantry.

Why Elite

The details others leave out.

  • 01

    Meticulous preparation

    Substrates are checked for flatness, moisture and movement before a single tile is set. Proper preparation is the difference between a floor that lasts and one that cracks.

  • 02

    Premium adhesives & waterproofing

    We use proven, manufacturer-backed adhesive and membrane systems, installed to AS/NZS 4858 and the data sheet — never mixed and matched to save a few dollars.

  • 03
