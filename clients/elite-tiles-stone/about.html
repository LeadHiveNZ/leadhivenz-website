About Us

Specialists, by choice.

Elite Tiles & Stone is a Christchurch tiling company focused on one thing: tile and stone, installed properly the first time.

Our Story

Obsessed with the work you’ll never see.

We are specialist tilers, not generalists. That focus lets us go deeper on the parts of a job that matter most — the substrate, the waterproofing, the set-out — and deliver a level of finish that is hard to achieve when tiling is one trade among many.

Our work spans residential new builds, full renovations and commercial fit-outs across Christchurch and wider Canterbury. We work directly with homeowners, and alongside builders, designers and architects who need a tiling contractor they can rely on to hold a line and hold a programme.

Whatever the scale, the approach doesn’t change: measure carefully, prepare thoroughly, communicate clearly and leave every site clean.

What We Stand For

Four principles, no exceptions.

  • Precision

    Straight lines, consistent joints and every cut placed with intent.

  • Honesty

    Clear pricing and straight answers, even when it isn’t what you want to hear.

  • Cleanliness

    Protected rooms, controlled dust and a tidy site at the end of every day.

  • Guarantee

    Workmanship we stand behind long after the final invoice.

Our Process

How we work.

Four clear stages, so you always know where your project stands and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Consult & measure

    We visit the site, talk through your tile selection, layout and timing, and take accurate measurements and substrate readings.

  2. 02

    Detailed quote

    You receive a clear, itemised quote covering preparation, waterproofing, materials and labour — no vague allowances or surprises.

  3. 03

    Preparation & waterproofing

    Substrates are levelled and primed, and wet areas are waterproofed to AS/NZS 4858 with full cure times respected.

  4. 04

    Installation & handover

    Tiles are set out, laid, grouted and sealed. We walk you through the finished work and leave care advice for every surface.
