Local conditions shape the job

Every part of the Bay brings its own challenges. Coastal sections at the Mount and Papamoa have sandy, free-draining soil and a lot of wind, which affects fence design, fixings and which plants will thrive. Hillside suburbs like Welcome Bay and Ohauiti often need retaining and careful drainage on clay. Newer subdivisions in Tauriko, Pyes Pa and Omokoroa usually start from bare ground, which suits a full landscaping package of lawn, irrigation, fencing and planting.

Wherever you are, the process is the same: a site visit, a written itemised quote, and Matt running the job.
