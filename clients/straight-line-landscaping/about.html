The person behind the work

Matt has been landscaping since 2012 and has owned and run Straight Line Landscaping for the past four years. The business is based in Tauranga and works across the city and the Western Bay of Plenty.

Being owner-operated keeps things simple. Matt visits the site, measures up and writes the quote himself, so the person you talk to at the start is the one accountable for the result at the end.

What the name stands for

"Straight Line" is the standard the work is held to: retaining walls that sit level, fence posts that run true on a string line, decks that are square, and lawns without dips. Just as important is a straight answer about cost and what is involved, with a written, itemised quote before any work starts.

The process

How Matt works

The same four steps on every job, big or small.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    Call or text Matt on 020 4006 6910, or send the quote form. A few photos of the area help.

  2. 02

    Site visit & itemised quote

    Matt comes out, measures up and talks through options, then sends a written quote broken down by item.

  3. 03

    Build

    Work is done in the right order (earthworks, walls and drainage, irrigation, then turf, decking and planting).

  4. 04

    Tidy handover

    The site is cleaned up and you get a walk-through, plus care tips such as a watering plan for new lawn.
