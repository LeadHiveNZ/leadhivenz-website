# Sauna HQ client quote format

Reproduce this layout and tone. It is Marko's own wording from real quotes. Keep the scope
bullets in build order. Never show cost prices, markup or labour as separate figures.

```
[Date, e.g. 2nd August 2026]

CLIENT:   [Client name]
PROJECT:  Custom Built Sauna: [L] x [W] x [H]mm
          At: [address]

We have pleasure in hereby submitting pricing and specifications for a custom-built Sauna based
on [dimensions and video provided / plans provided] plus our Traditional Finnish Sauna scope.

Sauna Specifications by Sauna HQ:
• [Any site-specific prep: block off doorway, remove plasterboard, new doorway...]
• Install a false ceiling for the sauna at [H]mm for the optimum user experience.
• Fit polyester insulation to all walls and ceiling of the sauna.
• Install Aluminium foil with taped joints followed by timber battens to create an air barrier
  in all walls and ceiling, completing our triple insulated sauna system.
• Install 100mm high black aluminium skirting around the perimeter of the sauna walls.
• Fix Premium Grade [Timber] lining boards [size] to all internal walls and ceiling of the
  sauna. All boards are secretly fixed and run vertical finishing about 60mm off the floor.
• [If glass] Supply and install [x] m² of toughened glazing to the glass wall.
• Install two-tiered sauna benches, built using [plank size] [Timber] planks. All sauna
  benches are built with concealed fixings.
• Supply and hang a custom built sauna door with ½ lite window, timber push/pull handles and
  heavy duty roller catch.
• Mount a Narvi Sauna heater, model [model] ([kW]) with [built-in manual controls / N2014
  control panel] and [kg] of Sauna rocks.
• Supply a wall mounted sauna light fitting with timber shade.
• Supply and install a 2½ rail back rest above bench.
• Supply Sauna Accessory pack, including bucket, ladle and thermometer.

Total Cost $ [ex GST] + GST = $ [inc GST] Inc GST

Alternative Timber Choice;
Replace all [Timber] timber components with [Alternative]. [One sentence on the alternative,
e.g. Alder and Thermo Aspen are the 2 most commonly used timbers for sauna construction in
Europe.]                                              Saving $ [difference] Inc GST

Optional Lighting Upgrade (rated at 125°C);
Fit 6 x 15mm LED downlight for the sauna ceiling                     Add $ 484.00 Inc GST
Fit LED Strip lighting to the ceiling above the top bench with pelmet Add $ 730.00 Inc GST

Optional Heater & Control Upgrades;
[Each priced heater that fits the room, with the price difference]   Add $ [diff]

Other Specifications for the Sauna not supplied by Sauna HQ:
  Electrical Power supply for sauna heater (32amp single phase OR 16amp three phase)
  Electrical supply and switching for the sauna lights. Light switch must be outside the sauna.
  Electrical Fit-off to Sauna Heater and lighting.
  Any work or finishes for the sauna floor
  Exterior painting to the sauna walls

Terms of Payment:
40% deposit with order confirmation and balance due on our completion of works.

Quote Validity
Our quotes remain valid for 90 days after which they are subject to review.

If you have any questions in relation to our proposal, please do not hesitate to get in contact
with me.

Yours sincerely,
Marko Tossavainen
```

Marko usually rounds the final figures slightly. Round the inc GST total to the nearest $10
or so and back-calculate the ex GST figure, the way his quotes read ($22,690 + GST = $24,959).
