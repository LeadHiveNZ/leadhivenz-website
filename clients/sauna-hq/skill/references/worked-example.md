alternative. Labour $7,000. Demolition $650. Other materials (cement sheet to block a doorway)
$450. Narvi NM 600. Standard wall light. Accessory pack.

Marko's own spreadsheet for this job had 85 wall boards, 27 ceiling boards, 13 bench planks,
$279 bracing cleats and $100 trims. Those are the numbers to hit. His sheet had no infill or
skirting line, so a run of the rules lands at about $10,325 materials against his original
$9,868, which he has accepted. Total inc GST with the NM 600 at list price: about $25,540.

## Pricing chain
Materials subtotal → × 1.35 → + labour 7,000 → × 1.10 GST → + heater 1,280 + light 280 +
accessories 300 + demolition 650 = total inc GST. Quote shows total / 1.1 + GST = total.

Heater: 2.01 × 1.32 × 2.1 = 5.57 m³. NM 600 (6–9 m³) is picked because of the 6 kW minimum
and the 0.5 m³ tolerance. NS 600, NC 60, Saana 680 and the pillar heaters list as upgrades.
