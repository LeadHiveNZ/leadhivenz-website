01What we do

Six services. One person accountable.

Spouting, fascia and downpipes work as one system. When one part fails the others usually suffer, so we look at the whole roofline before recommending anything.

01

Spouting replacement

When spouting is rusted through, leaking at the joins or sagging between brackets, patching rarely pays. We strip the old spouting, check the fascia behind it, and fit new steel or uPVC set to the right fall, so water reaches the downpipes instead of the garden.

Includes: removal and disposal of the old spouting, a fascia check, new brackets, spouting and outlets, and downpipes where needed.

Re-spouting explained 02

New spouting & fascia

For new builds, extensions and re-roofs. We work around your builder and roofer so the fascia goes on before the roof iron and the spouting follows once the iron is down. Continuous (longrun) spouting is available.

Includes: fascia, spouting and downpipes supplied and fitted as one package, matched to your roof or joinery colour.

New builds & re-roofs 03

Fascia

Timber fascia usually rots from the top edge, where water from old spouting has been getting in for years. If the timber is still sound, we prep and paint it: sand, prime and repaint. If it isn't, we repair short sections, replace whole runs, or switch to pre-painted steel so there's nothing left to scrape and repaint.

Includes: a rot check, fascia prep and paint, timber or steel fascia, and internal (box) gutter conversions on older homes.

Fascia options 04

Downpipes

A downpipe that's blocked, cracked or emptying onto the ground sends water straight at your foundations. We replace, add and re-route downpipes, and fit rainwater heads and tank connections.

Includes: downpipes, offsets, brackets and rainwater heads, connected to your stormwater outlets or tank.

Downpipes & rainwater heads 05

Spouting repairs

A leaking join, a sagging run, a corner that overflows in every downpour. If the rest of the spouting is sound, a proper repair makes sense. If it isn't, Dylan will tell you straight rather than sell you a patch.

Includes: resealing or replacing joins, re-hanging sagging runs to the right fall, new brackets, stop ends and outlets.

Repairs & leaks 06

Gutter cleaning & guard

Leaves, moss and roof grit settle in spouting and block the outlets. We clear the spouting, flush the downpipes and fit leaf guard where it helps. No guard makes spouting maintenance-free, and we'll be honest about that.

Includes: clearing by hand, flushing downpipes, a check for leaks and damage, and leaf guard where it suits.

Cleaning & leaf guard

02Diagnose it

Not sure which you need?

Start with what you can see from the ground. These are the most common symptoms and where they usually lead. Dylan confirms it on site, and if the fix is smaller than you expected, he'll say so.

Send us a photo
  • Overflowing at the corners Repairs or replacementUsually a blocked outlet, a failed join or a run that has lost its fall. Repairable if the rest is sound. If it isn't, it's time to replace.
  • Rust, flaking paint or pinholes ReplacementCorrosion in steel spouting works from the inside out. Once it's through in a few places, more will follow, and replacement is the honest answer.
  • Soft, rotten or peeling fascia FasciaPaint lifting along the top edge, or timber that gives under a screwdriver, means water has been getting in. Prep and paint it, repair it, replace it or switch to steel.
  • Water pooling at the base of the walls DownpipesA downpipe is blocked, cracked, disconnected or missing. Left alone, it soaks the ground right beside your foundations.
  • Moss, weeds or a blocked downpipe CleaningDebris is holding water in the spouting. A clean usually sorts it, and leaf guard can slow it coming back.
  • Building, extending or re-roofing New workPlan the spouting and fascia in with the roof, rather than adding them as an afterthought.

03Materials & warranty

Good products, honestly explained.

We fit pre-painted steel and Marley uPVC, from brands New Zealand builders know. The right choice depends on your house, your budget and how close you live to the sea.

Pre-painted steel

COLORSTEEL® & ColorCote®

Strong, crisp and made in colours to match New Zealand roofs. The usual choice for re-spouting, new builds and re-roofs.

ColorCote and COLORSTEEL steel rainwater goods carry manufacturer warranties that depend on product and exposure zone; we'll tell you exactly what applies to your home.

uPVC

Marley spouting

Light, won't rust and quick to fit. A cost-effective choice for many homes, sheds and garages.

Marley uPVC spouting carries a 15-year manufacturer guarantee.

Longrun

Continuous spouting

Made in long single lengths for each run, so there are far fewer joins to leak. Suits long, straight eaves.

Available on request. Ask about profiles and colours when you get your quote.

Close to the coast? Homes near the sea, in places like Castlecliff, Foxton Beach and Pātea, sit in a harsher exposure zone. That changes which products suit and what the warranty covers, so we check it before we quote.

05Questions

Common questions, straight answers.

Can't see yours? Call Dylan. He's happy to talk it through, with no obligation.

Do you only do full replacements?

No. Plenty of jobs are repairs, cleaning or a single downpipe. If a repair will give you good years, that's what we'll recommend. If the spouting is past saving, we'll explain why and show you what we found.

Can you quote from photos?

Often, yes. Send photos of each side of the house and any problem areas through the quote form, and tell us whether it's single or two storey. For bigger jobs Dylan will still measure on site before confirming the price.

Can you do the fascia and downpipes in the same job?

Yes. Spouting, fascia and downpipes work as one system, so we quote and fit them together. It's less disruption than booking them separately, and everything matches.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Whanganui and work across Manawatū, Rangitīkei and South Taranaki, including Palmerston North, Feilding, Levin, Marton, Bulls, Taihape and Hāwera. If you're further out, call and ask. See all service areas.
