01Why it matters

Blocked spouting does quiet damage.

Spouting only works when water can flow. Leaves, moss, roof grit and seed build up, and the trouble starts slowly.

  • Overflow. Water spills over the front and runs down the wall, or sits behind the spouting.
  • Rot. Water at the fascia, soffit and top of the wall is how timber starts to soften.
  • Rust and sagging. Wet debris holds moisture against steel and adds weight until brackets let go.
  • Blocked downpipes. Water backs up, or comes out where you didn't plan it.
  • Moss and weeds. Silt in the bottom of the run is a perfect place for them to take root.

Your warranty usually expects it

Manufacturer and installer warranties on spouting commonly come with maintenance conditions, and regular cleaning is often one of them. Check the paperwork for yours. If you ever need to make a claim, being able to show the spouting has been kept clear helps.

03How often

Twice a year is a good rule. Trees change it.

How often depends on what is overhead, not on the calendar.

Most homes

Twice a year

Once after autumn leaf-fall and once in spring covers most houses without much overhanging vegetation.

Getting it done in autumn also readies the spouting before winter.

Under trees

More often

Pine, gum, oak and liquidambar drop needles, leaves, bark and seed in heavy bursts. Spouting under or beside them clogs faster.

We'll suggest a sensible schedule after the first visit.

After a storm

Take a look

High winds put branches, bark and roofing grit in the spouting. If water is overflowing after rain, don't wait for the next clean.

Call us for a clean or a check.

04Leaf guard, honestly

Leaf guard reduces the mess. It doesn't end the job.

No guard makes spouting maintenance-free. Anyone who tells you otherwise is overselling it.

  • What a guard doesA mesh or cover over the spouting keeps most larger leaves and debris out. Less gets in, so there is less to clear and the spouting stays flowing for longer between cleans.
  • What it doesn't doFine debris, seed, roof grit and silt still get through or settle on top, and moss can grow on mesh. Guards still need checking, brushing off or lifting for a clean.
  • Mesh and other typesMesh is the most common: affordable and simple to fit, though fine debris can lodge in it. Solid covers and brush inserts exist, each with trade-offs. Some types can let heavy rain shoot over the front.
  • Where it suitsLarge leaves from deciduous trees, and roofs where the spouting is hard to reach. Pine needles and tiny seed are the hardest to keep out, so a guard helps least there.

Few trees nearby? A guard may not be worth the money, and we'll say so. If you do have trees close to the house, we'll talk through which type suits your roof and your spouting before you spend anything.

05Height & access

Heights are done safely, or not at all.

Single-storey roofs are usually straightforward. Two-storey houses, steep ground, tight access, glass roofs and spouting close to power lines take more time and different equipment, and they are quoted on that basis.

If we can't reach something safely, we'll tell you rather than take a risk. And if you are thinking of doing it yourself: please don't clean spouting from a ladder in the wet, or on your own. Falls from ladders are a common cause of serious injury at home.

How often should spouting be cleaned?

Twice a year suits most homes: once after autumn leaf-fall and once in spring. Houses with trees close by need it more often. After a big storm, check whether water is overflowing, and call us if it is.

Is leaf guard worth it?

It depends on your trees. Guards keep out larger leaves, so they help most with big-leaved deciduous trees. They don't stop fine debris, pine needles or silt, and they don't make spouting maintenance-free. If you have few trees nearby, it may not be worth the money, and we'll tell you so.

Will cleaning keep my spouting warranty valid?

Manufacturer warranties on spouting commonly come with maintenance conditions, and regular cleaning is often one of them. Read the terms for your product and keep a record of your cleans. Marley uPVC spouting carries a 15-year manufacturer guarantee. ColorCote and COLORSTEEL steel rainwater goods carry manufacturer warranties that depend on product and exposure zone; we'll tell you exactly what applies to your home.

Can you clean a two-storey house?

Often yes, but it takes more time and equipment, so it is quoted differently. Send photos of each side of the house, or call Dylan, and we'll tell you what is involved. If we can't do it safely, we'll say so.

What happens if you find a problem while cleaning?

You get photos and a plain explanation of what we found and what it would take to fix. Nothing extra is done without your say-so. A leaking join or loose bracket is a spouting repair; widespread rust or rot may mean new spouting.
