01Privacy statement

How we handle your information

This statement explains how Central Spouting Ltd ("we", "us") collects, uses and looks after personal information. We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and its information privacy principles.

Who we are

Central Spouting Ltd is a New Zealand registered company based in Whanganui. You can reach us at office@centralspouting.co.nz or on 020 470 9967.

Our privacy officer is Dylan, the owner. Any privacy question or request comes straight to him.

What we collect

When you use the quote form, we collect:

  • your name and phone number
  • your email address, if you give it
  • your town or suburb, and any address details you include
  • the services you tick and the message you write
  • a photo, if you choose to attach one

If you phone, text or email us, we keep what you tell us so we can quote and do the job. If you become a customer, we also keep the quote, invoice and job details.

Why we collect it

Only to reply to your enquiry, prepare your quote, arrange a visit, do the work, invoice you and answer any later questions about the job. We won't add you to a mailing list or use your details for marketing.

How your form reaches us

This website is hosted by Netlify, and quote form submissions are handled by Netlify Forms. When you press send, your details and any photo are stored by Netlify and emailed to us at office@centralspouting.co.nz. Netlify is based in the United States, so your submission may be stored on servers outside New Zealand. We use Netlify only to receive your enquiry, not for any other purpose.

Website logs and cookies

Like most web hosts, Netlify keeps basic technical logs when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time. These are used to run and secure the website. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch them.

We don't use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies on this website.

What we don't do

We never sell, rent or trade your information. We only share it when we need to for your job (for example, giving a supplier a delivery address for materials) or when the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only for as long as we need them to deal with your quote. If you go ahead with a job, we keep the job and invoice records for as long as New Zealand tax and business law requires, then delete them securely.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it. You can also ask us to delete it, and we will unless we need to keep it by law. Just email office@centralspouting.co.nz or call 020 470 9967.

If you're not happy with how we've handled a privacy request, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

Changes to this statement

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. This version was last updated on 14 October 2026.
