03What to look for

Six signs of good spouting work.

Use this list on our photos, or on anyone's. Good spouting is mostly in the details you only notice when they're wrong.

  • Straight, even linesFrom the street, the spouting should read as one clean line along the fascia, with no dips, kinks or wavy sections between brackets.
  • The right fallA slight, steady fall towards each downpipe. Too little and water sits and overflows; too much and the spouting looks crooked against the roofline.
  • Tidy corners and stop endsCorners fitted neatly and stop ends sealed. Corners are where most leaks start, so they deserve a close look.
  • Sealed, secure joinsEvery join sealed and fixed properly, with no gaps, no daylight underneath and no messy smears of sealant left on show.
  • Downpipes plumb and fixedDownpipes running straight down the wall, clipped at even spacing, with a clean connection into the drain or tank.
  • A proper clean-upNo offcuts in the garden, no screws on the driveway, old spouting taken away, and a walk-round with you before anyone leaves.
