The profile is the shape of the spouting face. Match it to the age and style of the house, and to how much water your roof sheds.

Quarter round

The everyday Kiwi profile. A smooth curved face that suits most homes, old and new.

How much does new spouting cost?

It depends on the length of spouting, the number of downpipes, the profile and material you choose, access (single or two storey) and whether old spouting or fascia needs removing. Rather than guess, we measure up and give you a free, itemised written quote, so you can see exactly where the money goes.

How long does a spouting replacement take?

Most single-storey homes are done in a day. Two-storey homes, fascia replacement and internal gutter conversions take longer. Your quote tells you how long to expect, and we keep you posted if the weather turns.

Should I choose steel or uPVC spouting?

Both are good choices. Pre-painted steel (COLORSTEEL® or ColorCote®) is strong, crisp and comes in colours to match most roofs. Marley uPVC spouting is light, won't rust and carries a 15-year manufacturer guarantee. Near the coast, product choice matters more: we'll recommend what suits your home's exposure zone and explain the warranty that applies.

My fascia is rotten. Can you fix that too?

Yes. Rot often shows up once the old spouting comes off, especially on older villas and bungalows. We show you what we find and agree the cost before doing any extra work. Timber fascia can be replaced or covered in pre-painted steel, and internal (box) gutters can be converted to external spouting.

Do I need to be home for the quote or the job?

Not usually. Spouting is outside work, so as long as we have safe access we can measure and install while you're at work. Send a few photos through the quote form and we can often price the job before we visit.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Whanganui (some still spell it Wanganui) and work across Manawatū, Rangitīkei and South Taranaki, including Palmerston North, Feilding, Levin, Marton, Bulls, Taihape, Hāwera and through to New Plymouth. If you're further out, call and ask. See all service areas.
