03Before you get in touch

Quick answers, no fine print.

Anything else, just ask. Dylan is happy to talk it through, with no obligation.

020 470 9967
Do I need to be home for the quote or the job?

Not usually. Spouting is outside work, so as long as Dylan has safe access to the roofline (gates unlocked, dogs inside) he can measure up and do the work while you're out. If he finds something that needs your say-so, like rotten fascia behind the old spouting, he'll call you before going any further.

How quickly will you get back to me?

Dylan reads every enquiry himself and replies as soon as he can. He's on the tools most days, so if something is urgent, like spouting hanging loose after a storm, a phone call to 020 470 9967 is the quickest way to reach him.

Can you quote by email or from photos?

Often, yes. For straightforward jobs, a few clear photos of the spouting, the corners and the downpipes, plus your rough address, are usually enough for Dylan to email you a price. For a full re-spout, fascia work or a two-storey home he'll usually want to measure up first so the quote is accurate. Either way, you get it in writing and itemised.

What if I'm outside your service area?

Ask anyway. We're based in Whanganui and work across Manawatū, Rangitīkei and South Taranaki through to New Plymouth. If you're further afield, Dylan will tell you honestly whether he can fit your job in, rather than leave you waiting.
