How far will Dylan travel from Whanganui?

Regularly across Manawatū, Rangitīkei and South Taranaki, and to New Plymouth for larger jobs. If you're outside those areas, or on the edge of them, call and ask.

Can you quote my place without driving out first?

Often, yes. Send a few clear photos through the quote form and we can usually price the job before anyone visits. For older houses or tricky roofs, Dylan may still want a look in person.

Do you work in small towns and on rural properties?

Yes. Marton, Bulls, Hunterville, Taihape, Pātea and Waverley are all part of the patch, along with farms, sheds and lifestyle blocks around them. For out-of-town properties, photos of each roof edge help a lot.
