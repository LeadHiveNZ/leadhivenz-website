01The city

A city with two kinds of roof.

Palmerston North is the biggest place we regularly work in, and the housing is more varied than you might expect. Close to the centre, Terrace End and Hokowhitu have older villas and bungalows with established trees, timber fascia, and spouting that's been patched more than once. Toward the edges of the city, newer subdivisions are going up with fresh roofs and fascia that haven't met a proper downpour yet.

Those two kinds of house need different things from a spouting contractor. The old ones need someone who'll look at what's under the spouting before quoting. The new ones need tidy, well-matched work that's done to suit the build.

We're based in Whanganui, about an hour away, so a Palmerston North job is a planned trip rather than a drop-in. Sending photos first lets Dylan price many jobs before he sets out.

Old villa or new build.

03Wind and weather

Flat, open and often windy.

Palmerston North sits on open plains with little to slow the wind. That matters for spouting.

  • Brackets and fixingsSpouting is only as good as what holds it to the fascia. We pay attention to bracket spacing and fixings so a run doesn't work loose over the years.
  • Wind-driven rainSideways rain gets under roof edges and onto fascia. Properly set spouting and a sound fascia keep that water moving rather than soaking in.
  • Downpipes held firmDownpipes that wobble in a gale are a sign they weren't fixed well. We secure them to the wall at sensible intervals.
  • A look after stormsIf a southerly has been through and your spouting looks different, send a photo. A loose bracket caught early is a small fix.

Do you travel to Palmerston North from Whanganui?

Yes. Dylan is based in Whanganui and Palmerston North is about an hour away, so jobs there are planned trips. Send photos through the quote form and we can often price the work before anyone drives anywhere.

I'm building in a new subdivision. Can you do the spouting and fascia?

Yes. New builds, extensions and re-roofs are a core part of the work: spouting, fascia and downpipes supplied and fitted as one tidy package. Give us a call, or have your builder call, and we'll talk through profiles and colours.

My Terrace End villa has rotten fascia. Do I need to replace both?

Often it makes sense to, because new spouting on soft fascia won't last. We'll show you what we find and agree the cost before any extra work. Timber fascia can be replaced or covered in pre-painted steel.

Is wind a problem for spouting in Palmerston North?

Not for spouting that's fitted well. Wind works on brackets and joins over time, so spacing, fixings and downpipe support matter. If something looks loose after a storm, send us a photo and we'll tell you whether it needs attention.
