01Home base

An old town with wet winters

Central Whanganui's homes are mostly from the 1910s, with some older still. Walk through Durie Hill, Gonville or St Johns Hill and you'll see villas and bungalows with iron roofs and plenty of character.

Houses of that era have timber fascia, and plenty have internal (box) gutters tucked between roof sections. After a century of Whanganui weather, that's where trouble starts: rusted spouting lets water sit against the fascia, the timber softens, and damp works into the rafter ends. Replace the spouting alone and the real problem stays hidden, so we check the fascia on every quote.

The town gets around 900 mm of rain a year, enough that a sagging or blocked gutter shows itself quickly. Whether you say Whanganui or still say Wanganui like plenty of long-time locals, the roofs are the same ones.

03Coast and river

Castlecliff, the river, and why they change the advice.

Spouting corrodes faster near salt water. Under New Zealand's building rules, land within about 500 m of the open coast (and about 100 m of tidal estuaries) is treated as the harshest exposure zone. Much of Castlecliff is close enough that we start every job there with one question: how far are you from the water?

Away from the coast, the river is the other factor. Moss and leaf litter hold moisture against the fascia in damp, shaded spots. Regular cleaning helps, and a leaf guard can cut the work down, though no guard makes spouting maintenance-free.

  • We ask first. Distance from the sea decides which products make sense.
  • uPVC doesn't rust. It's a sensible choice close to the coast. Marley uPVC spouting carries a 15-year manufacturer guarantee.
  • Steel can work too, but its warranty depends on your exposure zone, so we check before we sell.
  • Mind the metals. Copper or lead above pre-painted steel can corrode it, so we check your roof.

Warranty, plainly. ColorCote and COLORSTEEL steel rainwater goods carry manufacturer warranties that depend on product and exposure zone; we'll tell you exactly what applies to your home.

Is coastal spouting different in Castlecliff?

It can. Near the sea, salt speeds up corrosion, so product choice and warranty matter more. We'll ask how close you are to the water and recommend what suits your exposure zone. See spouting replacement for the options.

My Whanganui villa has internal gutters. Can they be replaced?

Often, yes. Internal (box) gutters can usually be converted to external spouting with new fascia, but whether that suits your roof and the look of the house varies. We'll tell you honestly after we've seen it.

Can you quote my Whanganui home from photos?

Often. Send a few clear photos through the quote form and we'll come back with an itemised, written quote. For older houses with suspect fascia, Dylan may want to look up close first.

How long does a re-spout of a Whanganui villa take?

Most single-storey homes take a day. Rotten fascia, two storeys or internal gutter conversions take longer, and your quote says how long.
