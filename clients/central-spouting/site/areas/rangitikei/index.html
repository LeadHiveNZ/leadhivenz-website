02Why it matters

The bit in the middle gets looked after.

Rangitīkei sits between two bigger markets, and a district of this size can easily get treated as somewhere to drive through. That isn't how we see it. A home in Marton or Hunterville gets the same free, itemised, written quote and the same owner-on-the-job attention as one in Whanganui.

Small towns often mean older housing, longer gaps between tradespeople and customers who've put up with a leaking gutter for longer than they'd like. If that sounds like you, a call is a good start. Photos make it even easier.

Honesty about travel matters in small towns. Marton and Bulls are close to our regular routes. Hunterville and Taihape take a bit more planning, and we'll tell you plainly how we'd handle your job.

03Cold country

Taihape is higher, and different.

Taihape sits well above the coast and is noticeably colder than the rest of our patch. Frosts are part of winter. Snow is not an everyday worry, but where it does settle on a roof, it tends to slide, and a sliding load can take spouting with it.

Snow straps (also called snow guards) fixed across the roof hold snow back so it melts and drains instead of coming off in one piece. They aren't needed on every roof. Whether yours would benefit depends on its pitch, cladding and where the spouting sits.

  • Ask about snow straps if your roof sheds snow over a path, door, or the spouting itself.
  • Bracket spacing and fixings deserve extra thought where a snow load is realistic.
  • Keep outlets and downpipes clear. A blocked outlet in a freeze is how spouting fills and fails.
  • Tell us your roof type when you call, and send a photo of the roof edge.

04Rural Rangitīkei

Farms, sheds and roof water.

A lot of Rangitīkei is farm and lifestyle country, and for many of those properties the roof is also the water supply. If your drinking water comes off the roof into a tank, clean spouting and downpipes matter more than they do in town: keeping leaves and debris out of the spouting helps keep them out of the tank.

That's one more reason to get the basics right: a good fall, brackets that hold, outlets that clear and downpipes that connect properly. Gutter cleaning and a leaf guard help too, though no guard removes the need to check.

  • Spouting replacement for homes, sheds and farm buildings.
  • Downpipes and tank connections.
  • Fascia repair and replacement.
  • Spouting repairs where replacement isn't needed.

Do you really come all the way to Taihape?

Taihape is the far end of our Rangitīkei patch, and yes, Dylan quotes there. It's a bigger trip, so call and tell us what you need. We'll be straight about what makes sense.

Do I need snow straps on my roof?

Not every roof does. They matter where snow sliding off could damage spouting or land somewhere unsafe. Tell us your roof type and send a photo, and we'll give you an honest answer.

Do you quote rural properties near Marton and Hunterville?
