About · Locally owned in Whanganui

Meet Dylan, the owner on every job.

Central Spouting Ltd is a Whanganui-based spouting, fascia and downpipe business. It's Dylan's company: he measures up, writes the quote and does the work himself.

01The story

A young business, run the old-fashioned way.

Central Spouting Ltd was registered as a New Zealand company in September 2024. We're a young business, and we'd rather tell you that straight than dress it up.

Dylan started Central Spouting because he wanted to do spouting work properly, himself, from the first measure to the last downpipe clip, rather than hand jobs off and hope they turned out right.

That's still how it works. When you ring Central Spouting, you talk to Dylan. He comes and looks at your roofline, writes the quote and is on the tools when the job gets done. If something isn't right, it's his name on it, and he lives and works locally, so he's not hard to find.

The work itself is everything between your roof and the drain: spouting replacement, fascia, downpipes, repairs and gutter cleaning, on everything from character villas to new builds and re-roofs.

02How we work

Quote, measure, install, clean up.

The same four steps on every job, whether it's one leaking corner or a full re-spout with new fascia.

  1. Step 01

    Free quote

    Call Dylan or send photos through the quote form. Simple jobs can often be priced from photos; otherwise he'll arrange a time to come and look.

  2. Step 02

    Measure & check

    Every run measured, the fascia and falls checked, and a written, itemised quote, so you know the price before anything starts.

  3. Step 03

    Install

    Old spouting off, new spouting set to the right fall, downpipes fixed plumb. Find rot behind the old spouting? We show you and agree the cost first.

  4. Step 04

    Clean-up & walk-round

    Offcuts, screws and old spouting cleared away. Then we walk round the house with you and check everything drains.

03What we stand for

Four things we won't compromise on.

No slogans. Just the way every job is run, and what you can hold us to.

  • Straight answersIf a repair will do, we'll say so. If your spouting is past saving, we'll show you why. No upselling and no scare tactics, just an honest opinion and a clear price.
  • The right product for your zoneA house in Castlecliff or Pātea lives with salt air that a house in Taihape never sees. We match the material to your exposure zone and tell you exactly which manufacturer warranty applies.
  • Tidy, every timeOld spouting and offcuts taken away, no screws left in the lawn, and a walk-round with you before we leave. Your place should look better than when we arrived.
  • Backed by our guaranteeEvery install is backed by our workmanship guarantee, and we're fully insured while we're on your property. If something we've done isn't right, we come back and fix it.

04Materials & brands

Products we trust, and why.

Names you'll recognise, with manufacturer warranties you can check for yourself. We'll recommend what suits your home, not what's easiest to fit.

Pre-painted steel

COLORSTEEL® & ColorCote®

Spouting, fascia and downpipes in the colours most New Zealand roofs come in. Strong, crisp lines, and the usual choice for re-spouts and re-roofs.

ColorCote and COLORSTEEL steel rainwater goods carry manufacturer warranties that depend on product and exposure zone; we'll tell you exactly what applies to your home.

uPVC

Marley spouting

Light, won't rust and quick to fit. A cost-effective choice for many homes, sheds and garages, in a range of profiles.

Marley uPVC spouting carries a 15-year manufacturer guarantee.

Longrun

Continuous spouting

Made in long single lengths, so there are far fewer joins to leak. A good fit for long, straight runs on new builds and re-roofs.

Available on request. Ask about profiles and colours when you get your quote.

Near the coast? Homes close to the sea or a tidal estuary sit in a harsher exposure zone, and that changes which products and warranties apply. Dylan checks this before he quotes, not after.
