Be part of something with a lot of heart.

Give your time, make a donation or partner with us. However you help, you'll be helping older people in our community feel connected and cared for.

Volunteer

Give a little time. Make a big difference.

Whether you can help once a week or once a month, there's a role for you. Volunteering with older people is rewarding, fun, and you might just make a friend for life.

We'll talk with you about what suits you and support you along the way.

Partner with us

Let's do more, together.

We'd love to work with local businesses, funders, churches, schools and community groups who share our vision of a community where no one grows old alone.

  • Sponsor an outing or event
  • Fund a programme
  • Offer a venue or transport
  • Staff volunteering days
  • Intergenerational visits with schools and youth groups
