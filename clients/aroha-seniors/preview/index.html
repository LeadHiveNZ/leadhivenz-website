Who we are

A warm place to belong, right here in our community.

Aroha Seniors Charitable Trust is a registered charity based in Flat Bush, Auckland. We create welcoming spaces where older people, vulnerable adults and adults living with disability can make friends, stay active and connected, and get the support they need.

Everything we do is guided by one word: aroha.

Get in touch
Who can come along?

Our programmes are for older people (seniors and kaumātua), vulnerable adults and adults living with disability in Flat Bush and the surrounding area. Whānau and carers are welcome too.

How do I order Meals on Wheels?

Call our Kai Manager, Korina Grace, on 022 605 2361. Meals on Wheels goes out every fortnight.

Does it cost anything?

Get in touch and we'll talk you through what's on and any costs before you come along, so there are no surprises.

I don't drive. Can I still take part?

Please still get in touch. Let us know when you call and we'll talk through the best way to get you there.

English isn't my first language. Is that okay?

Of course. Our community is wonderfully diverse. Tell us which language you're most comfortable in, and we'll do our best to help.

Can I get in touch for a parent or grandparent?

Absolutely. Call, text or send us a message and we'll take it from there, with their permission of course.

How can I help?

You can volunteer, make a donation or partner with us. Every bit of help means more older people feel connected and cared for.
