More than paint

The extras that make a job easier

Painting is rarely just painting. There’s usually a cracked bit of plaster, a soft board or a colour decision holding things up. We look after those parts too, so you don’t have to juggle several trades.

  • Colour help. We can arrange a colour specialist to help you choose with confidence.
  • Scaffolding and cleaners. On bigger exteriors we organise and manage them for you.
  • Repairs before painting. Filling, sanding, minor timber and plaster repairs, done properly.
  • Tiling and waterproofing. Floor and wall tiling and waterproofing, handy for bathroom refreshes.
  • Handyman and maintenance. The small fix-ups that get left on the list.

We use quality Resene and Dulux paints, chosen to suit the surface and the exposure.
