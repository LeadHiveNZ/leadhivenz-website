Local knowledge

Painting for Auckland’s conditions

Auckland’s climate is tough on paint. Strong UV fades colours, humidity encourages mould, and coastal suburbs deal with salt in the air. Older villas and bungalows in the inner suburbs have their own quirks, and newer plaster-clad homes need the right system to stay watertight.

We prepare and specify each job for the house in front of us and where it sits, not with a one-size-fits-all approach. That’s how the finish lasts.
