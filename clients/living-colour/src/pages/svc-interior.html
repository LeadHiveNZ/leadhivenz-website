Inside your home

A fresh interior without the fuss

A good interior repaint changes how a home feels. It should also be easy to live through. We plan the work room by room, keep you updated and tidy up as we go.

Whether it’s a single bedroom, a whole villa or a bathroom that needs a refresh at short notice, the approach is the same. We look closely at the surfaces, talk through finishes and colours, and give you a written quote that shows exactly what’s included.

  • Window joinery and sills
  • Bathrooms and kitchens
  • Hallways and stairwells

Selling, renting or moving out?

A clean, neutral repaint is one of the simplest ways to present a home for sale. We also handle rental and end-of-lease repaints, working to your settlement date or handover so you’re not caught short. Tell us your deadline when you get in touch and we’ll be upfront about whether we can meet it.

Help with colour

Choosing colours is the part most people find hardest. If you’d like a second opinion, we can arrange a colour specialist to help, as we did for Karen’s three bedrooms in Freemans Bay.

Interior FAQs

Before you book

Quick answers about interior repaints. For anything else, see the full FAQ or call us.

Do I need to move the furniture myself?

No. We move furniture into the middle of the room and cover it, and protect floors with drop sheets. If there are fragile or valuable items, it helps if you put those away before we arrive.

Can we stay in the house while you paint?

Usually, yes. We work through the house in an agreed order so you always have somewhere to be, and we use low-odour paints for most interior work.

How long does an interior repaint take?

It depends on the size of the house and how much prep is needed. A single room is often a day or two; a full house takes longer. Your quote will include a realistic timeframe.

Do you paint ceilings and trim as well as walls?

Yes. We can do walls only, or the full room including ceilings, doors, frames, skirting and window joinery. Your quote will list exactly what’s included.
