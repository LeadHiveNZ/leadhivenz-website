What to expect

The practical bits

A few things that help a job run smoothly, whether we’re painting your lounge or a two-storey exterior.

Our weather policy

We never paint exteriors in the rain, on wet surfaces or when it’s too cold for paint to cure. If the forecast turns, we’ll let you know early, switch to prep or interior work where possible, and adjust the timeline with you.

Photos while you’re away

Can’t be there? No problem. Anna in Takapuna had moved to another city while we painted her two-storey home, and we sent ongoing photos of the progress so she could see every stage.

Hours and access

We work Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6pm. We’ll agree start times, parking, access and whether you need to be home before the job begins.

Other trades, sorted

If the job needs scaffolding, cleaners or a colour specialist, we organise and manage them, so you only deal with us.

No surprise costs

If we find something unexpected, like rot behind a board, we show you and agree the cost before doing extra work.

Living with the work

For interiors we plan rooms in an order that keeps your home usable, and use low-odour paints for most indoor work.
