Up on the roof

Good for the roof, good for the street view

Your roof takes the full force of the sun and rain. Once the coating breaks down, rust, lichen and moss move in quickly. A well-prepared recoat restores protection and makes the whole house look cared for.

As with every job, the prep matters most. We wash the roof down, treat moss and lichen, deal with surface rust, and prime where the substrate needs it. Then we apply roof coatings designed for New Zealand conditions in the colour you choose, keeping gutters, paths and gardens protected along the way.

Roofs we can help with

  • Corrugated and long-run iron
  • Concrete tile roofs
  • Flashings and ridging
  • Gutters and spouting
  • Fascias and bargeboards
  • Garage and shed roofs

Safety at height

Roof work needs the right training and gear. The owner holds a Site Safe passport and is certified for working at heights and elevating work platforms. We set up edge protection or scaffolding where the job requires it, and we’re fully insured with $2,000,000 public liability cover.

Is it time?

Signs your roof is due include chalky or faded paint, patches of rust, flaking around fixings and growth that keeps coming back. Send us a few photos from the ground and we’ll give you an honest view on whether it needs a full recoat or just some spot repairs.

Roof FAQs

Roof painting questions

Not sure what your roof needs? Send us photos.

Can you paint the roof and the house together?

Yes, and it often makes sense. Doing both at once means one set of access equipment and one tidy-up, and the colours are planned together.

How long does a roof repaint take?

It depends on the size, pitch and condition of the roof, and on the weather, because roof coatings need dry days to go on and cure. We’ll give you a realistic timeframe in the quote.

Can I change the colour of my roof?

Usually, yes. We’ll talk through options that suit the house, and we can arrange a colour specialist if you’d like help choosing.
