Why prep matters more than paint

Most paint failures (peeling, cracking, bubbling or stains bleeding through) come down to the surface underneath, not the paint itself. Skip the prep and even the best paint won’t last.

So every quote we write spells out the preparation in plain English. You’ll see what we’re filling, sanding, sealing and priming, and why. It’s one reason our quotes aren’t always the cheapest, and one reason customers are glad they went with us.

Preparation we carry out

  • Waterblasting and washing down
  • Mould and mildew treatment
  • Scraping loose and flaking paint
  • Sanding and feathering edges
  • Filling holes, cracks and gaps
  • Sealing and re-caulking joints
  • Spot-priming bare timber and metal
  • Stain-blocking undercoats

Repairs before we paint

We handle minor timber and plaster repairs as part of the job: patching dented or cracked plasterboard, filling damaged weatherboards, and fixing the small things that would otherwise show through a fresh coat. If we uncover something bigger, we’ll stop, show you, and talk it through before going any further.

Prep FAQs

Questions about prep

Is preparation included in the price?

Yes. Prep is part of every painting quote and is itemised, so you can see what’s being done. There are no hidden extras for “surprise” prep later.

What if you find rot or damage once you start?

We’ll stop and show you, explain the options and agree any extra cost with you before doing more. Nothing gets added without your OK.

Can you do repairs without painting?

Yes. We take on maintenance, repairs, tiling and waterproofing as standalone jobs too. Get in touch with a description or a few photos.
