Our story

A small company with a simple promise

Andy set up Living Colour in 2014 with a straightforward idea: do the job properly, tell people what’s going on, and leave their place better than you found it.

More than a decade later, that’s still how we work. The business has grown to paint homes, offices, shops and schools across Auckland, plus work in Wellington, but you still deal with Andy. He comes to look at the job, writes the quote, runs the team and checks the work before he calls it finished.

Andy’s background includes five years on new-build projects for Fletcher Living at Red Beach and Horncastle at Millwater, and two years of insurance reinstatement work for Vero. That mix of fast-paced building sites and careful repair work shapes how we run every job today: organised, safe and thorough.

Our name says what we love: bringing colour back into the places people live and work. Our tagline says what we want for you: we paint, we repair, you relax.

How we work

What you can expect from us

We keep you in the loop

Regular updates, honest answers and progress photos when you can’t be there. It’s the thing our customers mention most.

We quote in detail

Our quotes spell out the prep, the products and the timing, so you can compare properly and know exactly what you’re getting.

We manage the job

Scaffolders, cleaners, a colour specialist: if your job needs them, we organise them. You deal with one person.

We fix it first

Filling, sanding, minor timber and plaster repairs, tiling and waterproofing. We sort the surface before we paint it.

We turn up

On time for the quote, on time for the job, and Saturdays when you need us. Short notice? Ask, and we’ll be straight with you.

We leave it tidy

Protected floors and gardens, a clean site each day and a final walk-through before we pack up.
