FAQ

Good questions, straight answers

The things people usually ask before they book. Can’t see yours? Give us a call.

All FAQs Ask us
Are your quotes free?

Yes. Quotes are free and there’s no obligation. For most jobs we come and look at the work in person, then send you a written, itemised quote so you can see exactly what’s included.

Which paints do you use?

We use quality Resene and Dulux paints, matched to the surface and the conditions. If you have a particular product or colour range in mind, just let us know.

What happens if it rains?

Exterior paint needs dry surfaces and the right temperature to cure properly, so we won’t paint outside in the rain. We keep an eye on the forecast, shift to prep or interior work where we can, and tell you early if the timeline moves.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry public liability insurance up to $2,000,000 and defective-workmanship cover up to $100,000. The owner also holds a Site Safe passport and is certified for working at heights and elevating work platforms.

Do you take on small jobs?

Yes. No job is too big or too small: a single room, a bathroom, a fence or a few repairs before you sell. Small jobs get the same prep and care as big ones.
