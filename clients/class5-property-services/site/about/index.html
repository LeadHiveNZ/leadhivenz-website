The short version

One tradie, the whole list

Class 5 Property Services is Jamie's business. He is a Christchurch local and a practical all-rounder who set up Class 5 for a simple reason: most homes do not need five different trades, they need one reliable person who can work through the list.

So that is how Class 5 works. The same person answers the phone, writes the quote, turns up to do the job and cleans up afterwards. Whether it is a TV on the wall, a patched-up hallway, a new side gate or a deck that has seen better days, you deal with Jamie from start to finish.

The name says it too. Maintenance, repairs and property care: looking after homes properly, for homeowners, landlords and property managers right across Christchurch and surrounds.

When a job needs a licensed electrician, plumber or another specialist, Jamie will say so upfront and work alongside them, rather than take on something that is not his to do.

How Jamie works

Four things you can count on

  • Straight answers

    If a repair will not last, or a job needs a different trade, Jamie will tell you before you spend money on it.

  • Done properly

    Fixed into framing, set out with a level, the right timber for the job. The parts you cannot see matter most.

  • On time

    Turning up when agreed, and keeping you posted if something changes. It should be normal; it is not always.

  • Left tidy

    Drop sheets down, dust vacuumed, offcuts gone. You should only notice that the job is done.
