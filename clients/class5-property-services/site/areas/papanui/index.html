Your local tradie in Papanui

Papanui is full of solid family homes, many of them bungalows and weatherboard houses from the middle of last century, mixed in with newer townhouses along the main roads near Northlands. These houses were built well, but after decades of Canterbury weather and the odd earthquake, most have a list of jobs that need attention.

Typical Papanui jobs for Class 5 include doors that have dropped and started sticking, cracked plaster around door frames, rotten window sills and weatherboards, and timber fences that have leaned after a nor'west gale. These are exactly the jobs that are hard to find a tradie for on their own, and easy to sort in a single visit when they are bundled together.

It is not all repairs. Plenty of Papanui families want more out of the space they have: built-in wardrobes in older bedrooms that were never designed with storage, shelving in a sunroom, a new deck off the kitchen or a TV mounted cleanly on the lounge wall with the cables hidden. Jamie measures carefully and builds to suit the house, not a catalogue.

Papanui is well inside Jamie's Christchurch service area, along with neighbouring St Albans, Merivale and Bishopdale, so getting to you for a quote is straightforward. Send a few photos through the quote form or give Jamie a call and talk through what you need.

You get a free written quote before any work starts, an owner-operator who turns up when he says he will, and a tidy site at the end of the day.
