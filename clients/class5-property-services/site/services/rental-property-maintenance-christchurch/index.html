Repairs handled, tenants kept in the loop

Rental maintenance is mostly lots of small jobs that need doing promptly: a sticking door, a broken towel rail, a hole in the hallway wall, a gate that will not latch. The hard part is finding one person who will turn up for all of them. That is what Class 5 is set up to do.

Jamie arranges access directly with tenants when you want him to, keeps them informed, and sends you before and after photos with the invoice. For property managers, that means fewer phone calls and a clear record against each property.

Between tenancies, Jamie can work through the whole list in one visit: wall repairs, door and lock adjustments, curtain tracks, cupboard hinges, fence and gate repairs and anything else from the exit inspection.

FAQ · Rental maintenance

Common questions

Something else on your mind? Call Jamie on 021 704 252.

Can you deal with tenants directly?

Yes. With your permission, Jamie contacts tenants to arrange access, keeps them updated and lets you know when the job is complete.

Do you work with property management companies?

Yes. Jamie can work to your job-order process, quote against your references and send invoices with photos for each property.

Can you help with Healthy Homes work?

Jamie can carry out practical fixes such as draught-stopping and fitting ground moisture barriers. Heating, insulation and electrical items go to the right specialists, and compliance is signed off by you or your property manager.

Do you cover rentals outside the city?

Yes. Class 5 works across Christchurch and surrounds, including Rolleston, Lincoln, Rangiora and Kaiapoi.
