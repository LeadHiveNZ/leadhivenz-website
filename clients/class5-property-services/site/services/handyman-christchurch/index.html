The whole list, not just one job

Most homes have a list of small jobs that never quite get done because none of them seems worth calling a tradie for. Bundled together, they make a sensible day's work, and you only have to explain things once, book once and pay one invoice.

FAQ · Handyman & small jobs

Common questions

Something else on your mind? Call Jamie on 021 704 252.

Is there a minimum job size?

There is no job too small to ask about. Small jobs are often best bundled together, so it is worth adding anything else on your list when you get in touch.

Do you charge by the hour or by the job?

Most work is quoted as a fixed price so you know the cost upfront. For open-ended lists, Jamie will explain how the job will be charged before he starts.

Do you supply materials?

Yes. Jamie can supply everything needed, or use materials you have already bought. Materials are itemised on the quote.

Can you do electrical or plumbing work?

No. Those need licensed trades. Jamie can work alongside them, though, for example preparing a wall or patching up after they are done.
