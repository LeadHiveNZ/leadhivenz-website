FAQ

Common questions

Anything else? Call Jamie on 021 704 252.

Do you charge to come and quote?

No. Quotes are free and there is no obligation. For most small jobs, a couple of photos sent through the quote form or by text is enough for a firm written price. For bigger work like decks and fences, Jamie will come and measure up.

What areas do you cover?

Class 5 works across Christchurch and surrounds, including Riccarton, Papanui, Halswell, Hornby, Sumner and New Brighton, plus Rolleston, Lincoln, Prebbleton, Rangiora and Kaiapoi. If you are not sure, just ask.

Is any job too small?

Not really. Small jobs are often best bundled together, so put everything on the list (the sticking door, the loose shelf, the hole in the wall) and Jamie will quote the lot in one go.

Do you supply the materials?

Yes. Jamie can supply everything the job needs, or use materials you have already bought. Materials are itemised on your written quote so you can see exactly what you are paying for.

Do you work for landlords and property managers?

Yes. Class 5 handles repair requests and between-tenancy work, can arrange access with tenants directly, and sends before and after photos with every invoice.
