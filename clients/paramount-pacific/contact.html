Questions

Before you get in touch.

Anything else, just ask. There’s no such thing as a silly question about your own home.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. We come out, look at the site and give you a written, itemised quote. There’s no charge and no obligation to go ahead.

Do you take on small jobs?

Yes. A garden path, a short run of fence or a bathroom gets the same care as a new home. Any job, big or small.

Do you work on Waiheke and the Gulf islands?

We do. Island jobs are planned around barge and ferry sailings, with materials sent over ahead of the crew. See all our service areas.

What should I put in my message?

Your suburb, what you’d like done, rough sizes if you know them, and when you’d like it finished. Plans or photos help too; you can email them once we’re in touch.
