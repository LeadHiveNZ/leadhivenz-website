One team

One team for the whole job.

Most building jobs need four or five trades. On ours, they’re on one programme, under one quote, answering to one person.

We handle the foundations, concrete, framing, roofing, fencing and paving ourselves, and plumbing and drainlaying is scheduled into the same programme. That means fewer subcontractors on site, fewer handovers between companies and fewer days lost waiting for the next trade to turn up.

About Willie & the team

  • One quoteEvery part of the job priced together, so nothing falls between separate quotes.
  • One programmeThe work is sequenced so each stage is ready when the next one starts.
  • One point of contactWillie runs the job. You always know who to call.
  • Fewer delaysLess waiting on other companies, so the job finishes when we said it would.

How it works

Four steps from first call to handover.

The same process whether it’s a new home or a garden path. You know what’s happening, what it costs and when it’ll be done.
