Where we work

Nine areas. One crew that knows the ground.

Every part of Auckland builds a little differently: clay, slopes, coastal weather, island access. Here’s what we see most in each area.

01

Auckland

Central Auckland and the isthmus suburbs, where most of our work is renovations and extensions on established sections. We’re used to tight access, close neighbours and older homes with character worth keeping.

  • Mt Eden
  • Epsom
  • Mt Albert
  • Onehunga
02

Franklin

Pukekohe, Waiuku and the rural lifestyle blocks of the south. Long driveways, boundary fencing and new homes on bigger sections.

  • Pukekohe
  • Waiuku
  • Patumāhoe
  • Clarks Beach
03

Hauraki Gulf Islands

Island jobs are mostly about logistics. We plan around barge and ferry sailings, get materials across in good time and keep the programme moving.

  • Great Barrier Island
  • Rakino Island
04

Manukau

South Auckland’s family suburbs: renovations, extensions, driveways and fences for homes that need more space and better flow.

  • Manurewa
  • Papatoetoe
  • Ōtara
  • Flat Bush
05

North Shore

From Devonport to Albany. Renovating 1960s and 70s homes, re-cladding, and fences and gates for sloping driveways and busy streets.

  • Takapuna
  • Devonport
  • Glenfield
  • Albany
06

Papakura

New homes in the growth areas around Takanini, plus renovations, concrete and fencing on established sections.

  • Takanini
  • Red Hill
  • Rosehill
  • Pahurehure
07

Rodney

Warkworth, Orewa, Helensville and the lifestyle properties in between. Longer driveways, rural fencing, new builds and the drainage bigger sections need.

  • Warkworth
  • Orewa
  • Helensville
  • Kumeū
08

Waitakere

West Auckland’s sloping and bush-clad sites, where retaining, foundations and drainage need to be right before the build goes up.

  • Henderson
  • Titirangi
  • Swanson
  • Te Atatū
09

Waiheke Island

Holiday homes, renovations, decks and fences, with the same island planning as the rest of the Gulf: materials barged over and work scheduled around the sailings.

  • Oneroa
  • Onetangi
  • Ostend

Further out

The awkward sites are where experience shows.

Over 20 years we’ve built on most of what the Auckland region has to offer. We plan for the site before we price the job.

01

Island logistics

Materials go over by barge and the programme is built around the sailings, so the crew arrive with everything they need.

02

Lifestyle blocks

Long driveways, boundary and stock fencing, and stormwater for bigger rural sections in Franklin and Rodney.

03

Sloping sites

Retaining, foundations and drainage for the hills of West Auckland and the North Shore, sorted before the build goes up.

04

Tight city sections

Careful access, considerate of the neighbours and a tidy site every day in Auckland’s established suburbs.
