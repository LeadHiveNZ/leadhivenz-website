Our story

Hi, I’m Willie.

My name is Uele Tasmania, but most people know me as Willie. For more than 20 years, my team and I have been building new houses and renovating existing ones right across Auckland.

We specialise in major renovations, fencing and concreting, but we’ll take on any job, big or small. A new home, a full renovation, a boundary fence or a garden path: every one gets the same standard of work and the same attention from me.

We’re professional, and we’re dedicated to meeting the deadline on every job. You get a competitive quote up front, and our commitment that the work will be finished on time and within budget.

That’s how we’ve built Paramount Pacific: one job at a time, done properly, for people who then recommend us to their family and neighbours.

“We take pride in every job as if it were our own.”Uele (Willie) Tasmania, Owner

How we work

Four promises we make on every job.

None of this is complicated. It’s just what you should expect from a builder, done every time.

01

Professional

We turn up when we say we will, keep the site tidy and treat your home and your neighbours with respect. You always know who’s on site and why.

02

Deadline-driven

Every job starts with a realistic programme, and we work to it. If something outside our control moves a date, you hear about it straight away, with a plan.

03

Competitive quotes

A clear, itemised price after a free site visit, so you can compare like for like. If the scope changes, we price the change before we do the work.

04

On time and within budget

Finishing when we said, for what we said, is how we earn the next job. It’s the standard we hold ourselves to on every project.
