What’s included

Every kind of concrete around the house.

Good concrete is mostly what you don’t see: the base, the mesh and the drainage. We spend time there, so the surface you do see stays flat and crack-free for years.

  • DrivewaysPlain, coloured or exposed aggregate, laid to fall so water runs where it should.
  • House slabs & garage floorsReinforced slabs poured to engineer’s specs, levelled and finished.
  • Paths & patiosBroom, trowel or exposed aggregate finishes, with joints placed to control cracking.
  • Retaining wallsConcrete retaining for sloping Auckland sections, with drainage behind the wall.
  • Footpaths & crossingsVehicle crossings and footpaths, built to what the council or Auckland Transport requires.
  • Cut-out & removalBreaking out and carting away old concrete, ready for the new pour.
  • ReinforcingMesh or bar sized to the load, held at the right height in the slab.
  • FinishesBroom, steel trowel, exposed aggregate or coloured, matched to your home and use.

Questions

Concrete questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Ask us on site. There’s no charge for the first visit.

Book a site visit

How long before I can drive on it?

Foot traffic is usually fine after a day or two. Cars after about a week. Heavy vehicles should wait longer, around 28 days. We’ll confirm for your mix and conditions.

Is exposed aggregate more expensive than plain?

Yes. It takes more labour and extra steps. It also gives a textured, slip-resistant surface that hides marks well. We’ll price both so you can compare.

Do you remove my old driveway?

Yes. We break out, cart away and dispose of the old concrete, then rebuild the base before we pour.

Do I need approval for a vehicle crossing?

Generally yes. Work on the crossing needs approval from Auckland Transport. We’ll tell you what applies and help you through it.

What about drainage?

We set the fall so water runs away from the house, and add channels or sumps where needed. Drainage is planned at quote stage, not as an afterthought.
