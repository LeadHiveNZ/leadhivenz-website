How long does a new build take?

It depends on size, design and consent timing. Expect months on site, not weeks. Your quote comes with a written programme, and we update you against it as the job goes.

Can you build from my plans?

Yes. Send us your architect’s or draughtsperson’s drawings and we’ll price them. We’ll point out anything that could cost you time or money before you commit.

Do you handle consent?

We support the process. Your designer lodges the plans with Auckland Council, and we work with them and your engineer to answer queries and book inspections.

Can you build a minor dwelling or granny flat?

Yes. The rules for smaller dwellings have been changing, so we’ll confirm what applies to your section at the first visit.

Which areas do you build in?

All over Auckland, including Franklin, Rodney, the North Shore, Manukau, Papakura and Waitakere. We also work on Waiheke and the Hauraki Gulf islands.
