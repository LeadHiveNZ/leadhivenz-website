What’s included

From the first sheet to the last downpipe.

Auckland roofs work hard. Wind lifts poorly fixed iron, and heavy rain finds every weak flashing. We build and repair roofs so they stay watertight, and we look after the spouting that carries the water away.

  • New roofsNew builds, extensions, garages and sleepouts, roofed to the plans and tied into the existing roofline.
  • Re-roofingStripping tired or failing roofs and replacing them in long-run or corrugated steel, metal tile or concrete tile.
  • Repairs & leak fixesWe trace the source of a leak, not just the stain. Rusted sheets, cracked tiles and failed fixings are repaired or replaced.
  • Flashings & penetrationsRidges, valleys, chimneys, skylights and wall junctions are where most leaks start. We flash them carefully.
  • Underlay, battens & ventilationNew underlay, sound battens and proper ventilation, so moisture doesn’t sit in the roof space.
  • Spouting, gutters & downpipesNew or replacement spouting and downpipes, set to fall and connected to stormwater.
  • Garages & sleepoutsCompact roofs on detached buildings, built to the same standard as the house.
  • Consents & coordinationWhere consent is needed, we work with your designer and manage it with Auckland Council. We’ll tell you at the site visit.

Should I repair or re-roof?

If the damage is local and the rest of the roof is sound, a repair makes sense. If there is widespread rust, lifting fixings, repeated leaks or cracked tiles, a re-roof usually costs less than patching year after year. We’ll inspect it and give you both options with prices.

How long does a re-roof take?

It depends on size, pitch and the weather. A typical house re-roof is a matter of days rather than weeks, but wet weather can move the dates. Your quote includes a realistic timeframe.

What roofing material should I choose?

Long-run or corrugated steel is the most common choice: light, durable and quick to install. Metal tile gives a tiled look at lower weight. Concrete tile is heavy, so the framing has to suit it. We’ll recommend one based on your roof, budget and the look you want.

Do you do gutters and downpipes?

Yes. We install and replace spouting, downpipes and flashings, and we can renew them as part of a re-roof so everything matches and works together.

Will you work in with my renovation?

Yes. If we’re building your extension or renovating your house, the roofing is scheduled into the same programme. If someone else is building, we’ll coordinate with them.
