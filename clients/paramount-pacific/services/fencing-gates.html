Questions

Fencing questions, answered.

Book a site visit

How high can a fence be without consent?

In most cases, fences up to 2 m don’t need building consent. Pool fences and retaining walls follow different rules. We’ll confirm for your site.

Who pays for a boundary fence?

Under the Fencing Act 1978, neighbours usually share the cost of a boundary fence. We can supply a written quote you can give your neighbour.

Timber or aluminium?

Timber is warmer and cheaper up front but needs upkeep. Aluminium costs more and needs very little. We’ll talk through the options on site.

How long does a fence take?

A typical residential fence takes a few days, depending on length, slope and access. Gates and pool fences can add time. You get an estimate with your quote.

Can you automate my existing gate?

Often, yes. It depends on the gate’s weight, frame and hinges. We’ll inspect it and tell you straight whether to automate it or replace it.
