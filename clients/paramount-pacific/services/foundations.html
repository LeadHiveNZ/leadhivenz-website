What’s included

Foundations for new builds and old houses.

Auckland sites vary widely: flat clay, steep slopes, fill, volcanic ground. The right foundation depends on the site, so we start with the engineering.

  • Concrete slab foundationsReinforced raft or ribbed slabs, formed, poured and finished to the engineer’s design.
  • Pile foundationsTimber or concrete piles set to depth and level for sloping or soft ground.
  • FootingsStrip and pad footings excavated and poured to spec.
  • Re-piling & re-levellingReplacing rotted or sunk piles and bringing older houses back to level.
  • Foundation repairsCracked, shifted or undermined foundations assessed and repaired.
  • Retaining as foundationRetaining walls that also support the building, built for the load.
  • Engineered sitesSloping and clay sections built to the specified depth, bearing and drainage.
  • InspectionsCouncil and engineer inspections booked and passed before concrete is poured or covered.

What are the signs my foundations need attention?

Doors and windows that stick, sloping floors, cracks in walls or brickwork, gaps at skirtings, and damp or rotten piles under the house. Get it looked at early.

Slab or piles?

Slabs suit flatter sites. Piles often suit slopes and timber-floor houses. Your engineer decides based on the ground, and we build it.

Do you work with my engineer?

Yes. We build to their drawings, ask for clarification if needed and arrange inspections at the right stages.

How long does re-piling take?

It depends on the size of the house, access and how many piles are needed. We’ll give you an estimate on site and a written programme with your quote.

Can you do foundations for an extension?

Yes. We tie new footings or slabs to the existing house carefully and design around the existing levels.
