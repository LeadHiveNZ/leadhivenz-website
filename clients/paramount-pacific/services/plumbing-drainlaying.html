What’s included

Everything that happens in the pipes and under the ground.

Plumbing and drainlaying are easy to ignore until they go wrong. We plan them in from the start of a build or renovation, so pipes go in before walls close and drains go in before concrete is poured.

  • New-build plumbingHot and cold supply, waste and fixtures for new homes, roughed in at the right stage and finished at handover.
  • Kitchens, bathrooms & laundriesRe-plumbing for renovations: moved fixtures, new layouts and tidy pipework behind finished walls.
  • Hot waterHot water systems supplied and installed, or replaced, positioned for easy servicing.
  • Stormwater & wastewater drainsNew drains laid to fall on a proper bed and backfilled correctly.
  • Council connectionsConnections to council stormwater and wastewater services, with approvals worked through alongside the build.
  • Drain repair & replacementCracked, collapsed or root-blocked drains dug out and replaced. We can arrange CCTV inspection to find the fault first.
  • Site drainage & soakageSumps, soakage and channel drains to move water off the section and away from the house.
  • Subsoil & under-slab drainageSubsoil drains behind retaining walls, and pipework laid under driveways and slabs before the concrete goes down.

Do I need a drainlayer or a plumber?

Often both. Plumbing covers pipes and fixtures inside the house, including hot water. Drainlaying covers the underground pipes that carry wastewater and stormwater to the council connection. We coordinate both so you deal with one team.

Can you connect to council services?

Yes, for new connections and alterations. Most need approval and an inspection. We’ll tell you what’s required at the site visit and work through it with you.

Why is my section boggy?

Usually the water has nowhere to go. Common causes are clay soil, missing or blocked drains, downpipes discharging onto the ground, or a high water table. The fix might be subsoil drainage, soakage or connecting roof water to stormwater. We’ll look at the site and recommend.

What’s involved in re-plumbing a bathroom?

Old pipework comes out, new supply and waste lines go in, and the fixtures are set. Waterproofing and tiling follow. It’s usually part of a wider bathroom renovation, so we plan it in with the other trades.

Do you do emergency drain unblocking?

We focus on build and renovation work, so we’re not an emergency call-out service. Drain repairs and replacements are done by arrangement. Call us and tell us what’s happening.
